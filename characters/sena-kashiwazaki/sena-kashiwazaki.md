### Brief Description

Sena Kashiwazaki is the wealthy, admired daughter of St. Chronica's chairman and one of the Neighbors Club's first recruits. Beautiful, gifted, and socially confident with boys, she joins because envy and arrogance have left her without female friends, a problem she treats as both baffling and deeply unfair.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Sena is a teenage girl of average height with a curvy, athletic figure and notably full proportions. She has fair skin, a soft oval face, and striking blue eyes that are expressive, bright. Her long blond hair falls in thick, smooth layers down her back, with side-swept bangs and a butterfly-shaped hair clip near one temple. Her features are polished and glamorous, giving her an eye-catching presence even when she is standing still. She tends to carry herself with natural confidence, shoulders open and chin slightly raised, as though she is accustomed to being noticed and accommodated. Her movements are energetic and direct rather than restrained. Pride, irritation, delight, and embarrassment all show quickly across her face. When challenged, she leans forward and fixes others with an indignant stare; when pleased, her entire expression brightens. Despite her poised beauty, her reactions can become comically unguarded whenever competition, games, or personal insecurity overwhelm her.

### Backstory

Sena was raised as the privileged daughter of Pegasus Kashiwazaki, chairman of St. Chronica's Academy. Wealth, beauty, intelligence, athletic ability, and social status made her enormously popular with male students, but the attention also isolated her from other girls, who often viewed her with envy or resentment. Accustomed to praise, Sena came to assume that her success should naturally win affection, leaving her confused and offended when it did not. She developed strong confidence around boys while lacking the mutual ease she wanted with female peers. Shortly after Yozora founded the Neighbors Club, Sena responded to its recruitment notice and attempted to join. Yozora initially rejected and antagonized her, but Kodaka recognized that Sena's lack of female friends matched the club's purpose. At the beginning of the manga, Sena joins in the hope of gaining genuine friendship while refusing to believe that her own arrogance or competitiveness might be part of the problem.

### Mannerisms

Sena speaks openly and with confidence, often stressing her own competence as though it should settle an argument. She plants her hands on her hips when indignant, points when accusing someone, and becomes animated during games. Embarrassment quickly turns into loud denial or defensive boasting.

### Personality

Sena is proud, competitive, talented, and emotionally transparent. She expects recognition because most areas of life have rewarded her ability, appearance, and status. When others resist her, she tends to respond with disbelief, indignation, or an immediate effort to prove herself superior. Her arrogance is real, but it sits beside genuine loneliness and a sincere wish to be accepted by other girls. She can be generous, enthusiastic, and surprisingly earnest when pursuing something she values. She throws herself into games and challenges with little restraint, making her energetic but easy to provoke. Yozora's insults reliably draw her into arguments because Sena hates being belittled and lacks the patience to ignore bait. In play, she is bold and decisive, preferring direct action over subtle planning. Her greatest strength is confidence backed by real ability; her greatest flaw is that she often mistakes admiration, victory, or possession for genuine connection.
