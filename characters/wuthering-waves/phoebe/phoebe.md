## Brief

Phoebe is an Acolyte of the Order of the Deep in Rinascita and a Spectro Resonator whose sincere faith is matched by a warm, considerate nature. Devout without being severe, she seeks to bring comfort and guidance to others while faithfully carrying out her responsibilities. At Rover's awakening, her upbringing, faith, Resonance abilities, and established personality already exist independently of him.

## Appearance

Phoebe is a young woman with a slender, graceful build and a bright, refined presence. She has long blonde hair arranged in flowing layers, with distinctive styling and ornaments that complement her light-colored eyes and gentle features. Her clothing reflects Rinascita's ornate aesthetic and her religious role within the Order of the Deep, combining white and dark fabrics with rich gold detailing, fitted sections, layered panels, and carefully arranged decorative elements. The ensemble carries a ceremonial elegance while retaining the mobility expected of a Resonator, blending ecclesiastical visual cues with Wuthering Waves' characteristic technical-fantasy fashion rather than resembling conventional historical vestments. Fine accessories and luminous motifs reinforce her association with Spectro Resonance and the Order without overwhelming her silhouette. Phoebe usually carries herself with poised attentiveness, her posture upright but approachable. Her expressions readily communicate warmth and earnest interest, giving her a welcoming presence even when the formality of her attire emphasizes the seriousness of her vocation.

## Backstory

Before Rover awakens, Phoebe is already an Acolyte of the Order of the Deep in Rinascita, having grown within a culture where faith and reverence for the Sentinel shape public life. Her religious upbringing gave her both formal responsibilities and a sincere desire to support the people around her. Phoebe became a Resonator and developed Spectro abilities that coexist naturally with her vocation, though she does not define faith solely through supernatural power. Her work as an Acolyte requires discipline, knowledge of religious practice, and regular interaction with people seeking reassurance, guidance, or participation in the Order's traditions. These experiences have reinforced her patience and empathy while leaving room for a lively personal curiosity beyond ceremonial duties. By the opening of Wuthering Waves, her beliefs and place in Rinascitan society are already her own and have not been shaped by Rover's journey. This profile stops at the exact moment Rover awakens in Huanglong; Phoebe has not met him, learned of later events through him, or undergone any subsequent revelations, crises, relationships, or personal development.

## Mannerisms

Phoebe speaks politely and with genuine attentiveness, often giving others her full focus when they address her. Her gestures are graceful and restrained during formal duties but become more animated when her curiosity or enthusiasm surfaces. She tends to smile readily, maintains careful posture, and approaches unfamiliar situations with observant interest rather than immediate defensiveness.

## Personality

Phoebe is kind, devout, conscientious, and sincerely curious about the world around her. Her faith is an important part of her identity, but she expresses it primarily through service, encouragement, and personal conviction rather than severity toward others. She takes her responsibilities as an Acolyte seriously and tries to conduct herself in a way worthy of the trust placed in her. At the same time, Phoebe possesses an approachable warmth and youthful enthusiasm that keep her from seeming distant or purely ceremonial. She listens carefully, is sensitive to other people's concerns, and prefers helping someone find courage over simply instructing them what to believe. Her curiosity can draw her toward unfamiliar ideas and experiences, though she evaluates them through the principles she already holds. Phoebe dislikes needless cruelty and responds strongly when innocent people are endangered, demonstrating resolve beneath her gentle demeanor. She can question circumstances without casually abandoning deeply held convictions. At Rover's awakening, these qualities are entirely pre-existing; no later trust, affection, revelations, doubts, shared experiences, or development involving Rover are included.
