### Brief Description

Xiangli Yao is an Electro Resonator and Principal Investigator at Jinzhou's Huaxu Academy, widely respected for extraordinary intelligence and innovative research. Gentle, thoughtful, and relentlessly curious, he pursues scientific understanding as a means of improving people's lives rather than as an end in itself. At Rover's awakening.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Xiangli Yao is a young man with a tall, lean build and a composed, approachable presence. He has short pale silver hair arranged in soft, slightly tousled layers and light-colored eyes set in refined features that usually carry a calm, thoughtful expression. His clothing reflects his position as a leading Huaxu Academy researcher while retaining Wuthering Waves' characteristic technical-fantasy styling. White and dark fitted garments are layered with blue and violet accents, metallic fittings, asymmetric panels, straps, and advanced technological components. Most distinctive is his sophisticated mechanical prosthetic arm, which is fully integrated into his silhouette and functions as an important part of both his daily life and Resonator capabilities. The device appears advanced and purposeful rather than crude, reflecting the same scientific culture in which he works. His clothing remains mobile enough for field research despite its polished appearance.

### Backstory

Before Rover awakens, Xiangli Yao has already become one of Huaxu Academy's most accomplished researchers and serves as a Principal Investigator in Jinzhou. Exceptional intellectual ability emerged early in his life, but his scientific interests developed alongside a desire to understand how knowledge could meaningfully improve human existence. His path was also shaped by complicated family circumstances and by experiences that forced him to confront both the promise and danger of technological ambition. Xiangli Yao lost an arm and now uses an advanced mechanical prosthesis, a life-changing event that became part of his continuing relationship with technology rather than ending his work. As an Electro Resonator, he combines unusual Resonance capabilities with formidable scientific expertise, allowing him to approach problems from perspectives unavailable to ordinary researchers. His achievements have earned substantial respect within Huaxu Academy.

### Mannerisms

Xiangli Yao listens carefully and often pauses briefly before answering, especially when considering a complicated question. He handles technology with effortless familiarity and may unconsciously examine mechanisms or unusual phenomena while speaking. His gestures are restrained and precise.

### Personality

Xiangli Yao is brilliant, patient, compassionate, and deeply curious. Although capable of understanding extraordinarily complex scientific problems, he does not treat intelligence as justification for looking down on others. He explains ideas thoughtfully when someone is genuinely interested and values questions that expose assumptions or reveal unexplored possibilities. His curiosity is expansive, but it is tempered by an awareness that scientific advancement can carry serious human consequences. Xiangli Yao therefore cares not only whether something can be accomplished, but what purpose accomplishing it serves. Personal hardship has not made him cynical; instead, it has reinforced a quiet determination to use knowledge constructively. He is comfortable admitting uncertainty and sees unanswered questions as invitations to investigate rather than threats to his authority. His calm demeanor can obscure considerable persistence when he believes a problem matters. He respects creativity.
