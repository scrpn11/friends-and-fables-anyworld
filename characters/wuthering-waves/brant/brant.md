### Brief Description

Brant is a young Rinascitan Resonator and captain of the Troupe of Fools, already renowned before Rover's awakening as a flamboyant performer, daring sailor, and fiercely protective leader. Carefree in manner but sincere beneath the theatrics, he pursues freedom and a safe home for his troupe.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Brant is a young adult man of average height with a lean, athletic build shaped by an active life at sea and onstage. He has fair skin, vivid fuchsia eyes, and short tousled turquoise hair marked by striking fuchsia streaks. His colorful attire blends the silhouettes of a theatrical performer and romanticized seafaring captain: a dark green open-front shirt with black frilled edging and fiery red patterning exposes part of his torso, while a broad raised collar carries red-and-gold checker accents. Layered shoulder pieces combine black, gold, pale blue, white, and fuchsia ornamentation, with decorative pins, chains, and patterned fabrics adding deliberate spectacle. Long dark fingerless gloves cover much of his forearms. White trousers are secured by crossing belts, and dark boots complete the ensemble. A red-and-black cape and captain-like flourishes reinforce his dramatic presence. His Tacet Mark lies on his left pectoral.

### Backstory

Brant was born in Rinascita and grew up surrounded by expectations that never suited his independent nature. As a boy he treasured stories of exploration and discovered an Ammonoid fossil among his family's books, keeping it as a reminder of adventure and curiosity. His early life eventually brought him aboard the Pilgrim's Sail, where hardship, separation, and the suffering he witnessed left a lasting impression. He later found belonging among the Troupe of Fools, whose performances and wandering life offered the freedom he sought. Over time he became an accomplished actor, sailor, Natural Resonator, and captain of the troupe. He guided its members through dangerous seas and treated them as family, devoting himself to protecting their freedom and creating a secure home. Before Rover awakens, the Troupe already travels Rinascita aboard its seaborne stage, performing for audiences while Brant balances spectacle, responsibility, and his determination to chart his own course.

### Mannerisms

Brant speaks with theatrical confidence, easy humor, and a performer's instinct for timing. He makes entrances feel like scenes, uses broad gestures, improvises readily, and slips playfully into roles. Around his troupe, concern often hides behind a grin.

### Personality

Brant is exuberant, romantic, adventurous, and fiercely independent. He approaches life as both voyage and performance, delighting in uncertainty, improvisation, spectacle, and chances to make others smile. His flamboyance is sincere, but it also helps him carry private worries without burdening companions. Beneath his carefree manner is a dependable captain whose loyalty to the Troupe of Fools is absolute. He regards its members as family and measures his freedom partly by whether he can preserve theirs. Brant is courageous in danger, comfortable taking responsibility, and quick to turn chaos into momentum. He dislikes rigid convention and institutions that demand obedience for its own sake. His strengths are charisma, optimism, adaptability, courage, sincerity, and devotion. His weaknesses include risk-taking, hiding strain behind humor, and assuming burdens alone. He can be reckless when freedom or his troupe is threatened, but choices are guided by care for those depending on him.
