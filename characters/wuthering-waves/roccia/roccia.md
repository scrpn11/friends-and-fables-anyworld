### Brief Description

Roccia is the First Mate, prop master, assistant, and improvisational comedian of Rinascita's Troupe of Fools. Quiet and composed offstage despite her theatrical talents, she keeps the troupe organized while creating performances and caring deeply for its displaced members. At Rover's awakening, her history with the troupe, bond with Pero.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Roccia is a petite young woman with a distinctive theatrical appearance shaped by Rinascita's ornate culture and the eccentric style of the Troupe of Fools. She has pale hair arranged in voluminous twin sections with darker and reddish accents, framing a youthful face whose calm expression often contrasts humorously with her dramatic movements. Her outfit combines black, white, and rich red tones with fitted sections, layered fabric, decorative trim, ribbons, patterned details, and asymmetrical elements evocative of a stage costume without sacrificing mobility. Gloves, stockings, elaborate footwear, and carefully placed accessories reinforce the troupe's carnival-inspired aesthetic. Her most recognizable companion is Pero, an enormous peculiar box capable of producing props and extending strange limbs from within. Roccia often perches on or travels with Pero as naturally as another person might carry a bag. Her posture is usually tranquil and contained.

### Backstory

Roccia was born in Rinascita and grew up under the Order of the Deep's restrictive authority. As a child, she was condemned to the Pilgrim's Sail after performing songs from forbidden plays. The supposed pilgrimage was effectively a death sentence, and her ship was destroyed near Penitent's End. Weak and expecting divine judgment, Roccia instead encountered Pero, a peculiar boxlike Echo that rescued her and brought her into the community that became her new home. Among the Troupe of Fools, Roccia discovered a form of theater free from the Order's censorship and gradually rebuilt her confidence in performance. She became a skilled prop maker, improvisational comedian, and dependable organizer, eventually serving as the troupe's First Mate. Her bond with Pero also produced an unusual ability to communicate with Echoes through their frequencies, leading the troupe's medic to regard her as an Acquired Resonator. By Rover's awakening.

### Mannerisms

Roccia is usually still, soft-spoken, and observant, often letting Pero or a carefully chosen gesture communicate what she does not immediately say aloud. Her face can remain almost stoic while she performs exaggerated theatrical movements, an accidental contrast she has learned audiences find.

### Personality

Roccia is introverted, tranquil, dependable, and quietly compassionate. She rarely demands attention despite being an accomplished performer, preferring to observe, listen, and contribute where she is needed. Her reserve can initially appear cold, but she is deeply invested in the welfare of the Troupe of Fools and especially sensitive to people who have been displaced, abandoned, or deprived of a home. Roccia believes theater can preserve freedom and restore hope, shaped by her own experience of having artistic expression condemned by the Order. She is imaginative and capable of elaborate improvisation, yet in ordinary life she is practical enough to organize supplies, prepare meals, maintain props, coordinate rescues, and keep the troupe functioning. Her bond with Pero is central to her emotional life and artistic identity. Roccia does not romanticize the suffering that brought the Fools together.
