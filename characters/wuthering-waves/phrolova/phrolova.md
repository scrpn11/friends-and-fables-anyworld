## Brief

Phrolova is a powerful Resonator and Overseer of the Fractsidus whose eerie composure conceals intense conviction and formidable control over frequencies associated with Tacet Discords. Calculating, theatrical, and deeply committed to her own understanding of evolution and loss, she is already an established threat before Rover awakens.

## Appearance

Phrolova is a young woman with a slender, elegant build and an unnervingly composed presence. She has very long pale hair with reddish tones and distinctive styling that frames a refined face, while her striking eyes and controlled expression contribute to an almost otherworldly impression. Her elaborate clothing uses dark and pale fabrics with vivid red accents, asymmetric layers, fitted sections, ornamental fastenings, and intricate accessories characteristic of Wuthering Waves' technical-fantasy aesthetic. The ensemble carries a dramatic, ceremonial quality appropriate to her Fractsidus status without sacrificing mobility. Musical and frequency-related visual motifs subtly reinforce the nature of her Resonance abilities, while metallic and anomalous details suggest a connection to forces beyond ordinary human technology. Her silhouette is graceful rather than heavily armored, emphasizing precision and control. Phrolova's posture is poised and deliberate.

## Backstory

Long before Rover awakens, Phrolova has already become an Overseer within the Fractsidus and developed an unusual relationship with Resonance, death, and Tacet Discord frequencies. Her past is marked by profound loss and experiences that shaped a determination to challenge the limits separating life, memory, and the transformations produced by the Lament. As a Resonator, she possesses extraordinary sensitivity to frequencies and the ability to manipulate them in ways that can influence Tacet Discords, making her both a dangerous combatant and an important figure within Fractsidus operations. Rather than seeing the catastrophes of Solaris-3 only as destruction, Phrolova embraces the organization's belief that upheaval can drive a form of evolution, though her personal motives and grief give that ideology an intensely individual meaning. By the opening of Wuthering Waves, her abilities, rank, worldview, and existing relationships within the Fractsidus are already established.

## Mannerisms

Phrolova moves with deliberate grace and rarely appears hurried. Her gestures can resemble those of a conductor directing an unseen performance, especially when using her Resonance abilities. She often watches others in silence before responding and speaks with controlled certainty.

## Personality

Phrolova is intelligent, controlled, enigmatic, and intensely driven. She approaches people and events with the perspective of someone accustomed to seeing patterns in frequencies and consequences that others cannot perceive. Her composure is genuine but not evidence of emotional emptiness; powerful grief and conviction exist beneath it and have helped shape the choices she makes. Phrolova is capable of patience, manipulation, and calculated cruelty when they serve her objectives, yet she does not behave chaotically or harm others merely for amusement. She believes transformation can emerge from catastrophe and is willing to pursue possibilities that most people would reject as dangerous or morally unacceptable. Her confidence can take on a theatrical quality, particularly when she feels she understands a situation better than those around her. She respects unusual strength and meaningful potential but evaluates both according to her own worldview rather than conventional morality.
