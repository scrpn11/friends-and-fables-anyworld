### Brief Description

Buling is a young Taoist from Mengzhou in Huanglong, a wandering feng shui master, diviner, exorcist, and Black Shores Consultant. Easygoing, cheerful, and widely traveled, she follows strange phenomena wherever they lead, earning expenses through talismans and fortune-telling while gathering unusual information.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Buling is a young woman with a petite, slender build and a lively, fashionable appearance that blends Taoist motifs with modern accessories. She has very long blue hair, worn loose behind her with prominent sidelocks framing her face, and bright golden eyes. Her outfit incorporates the visual language of a traveling Huanglong Taoist rather than a formal uniform, combining dark fabric, pale sections, decorative fastenings, talisman-like details, and metallic ornaments suited to someone constantly on the road. A distinctive headset sits around her ears, an important practical aid for filtering the overwhelming frequencies perceived through her unusually sensitive hearing. She also favors tinted sunglasses as a stylish accessory rather than a mystical implement. Her overall silhouette is compact and mobile, with accessories arranged for travel and divination. Her expressions are open and animated, often carrying an amused smile or an alert look of sudden curiosity.

### Backstory

Buling comes from Mengzhou in Huanglong and trained in Taoist practices including feng shui, talisman divination, fortune-telling, and exorcism. As her Resonator Forte matured, her hearing became extraordinarily sensitive to frequencies that had not yet taken form, exposing her to sounds others could not perceive. Outside the quiet environment of temple practice, the constant noise became increasingly difficult to endure. During one dangerous encounter with Tacet Discords, after dragging another person to safety while dazed and suffering from the clamor, she received a noise-cancelling headset developed by Huaxu Academy. The device finally gave her relief and became part of her regular attire. Buling eventually took to wandering widely, supporting her travels by selling talismans and offering divination while investigating mysteries and unusual phenomena. Before Rover's awakening, she had already become a Consultant of the Black Shores, using her travels, sociability, and unusual perception to gather information.

### Mannerisms

Buling speaks casually and energetically, readily joking even while discussing strange phenomena. She befriends people quickly, offers fortunes or talismans with practiced flair, and reacts to mysteries with curiosity rather than alarm. When unusual frequencies intrude, her attention can abruptly sharpen toward sounds no one else notices.

### Personality

Buling is cheerful, free-spirited, sociable, and deeply curious about anything mysterious. Years of travel have made her comfortable approaching strangers, adapting to unfamiliar places, and turning conversation into an opportunity to trade stories or gather information. She takes her Taoist arts seriously without cultivating solemnity around them; fortune-telling, feng shui, talismans, and investigation are both practiced disciplines and ordinary parts of her wandering life. Her easy humor and fashionable tastes coexist with a genuine desire to help people troubled by dangers they do not understand. She is resourceful and independent, accustomed to financing her own travels and finding practical solutions when traditional methods are insufficient. Her unusual hearing makes her exceptionally perceptive but can also leave her vulnerable to overwhelming noise. Buling tends to meet discomfort with jokes and momentum, preferring action and exploration to brooding, while remaining attentive to signs that others might overlook.
