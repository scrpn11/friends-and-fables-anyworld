### Brief Description

Chixia is an energetic young Jinzhou Patroller and a Fusion Resonator whose enthusiasm for helping people is matched by her confidence with a pair of pistols. Friendly, outspoken, and intensely civic-minded, she treats protecting Jinzhou's residents as both a responsibility and a source of genuine pride.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Chixia is a young woman with a lean, athletic build shaped by the active life of a Patroller. She has warm reddish-brown eyes and vivid red hair worn in a short, practical style, with longer sections and accessories adding a lively silhouette. Her expression is usually open and animated, readily shifting into a broad grin or determined focus. Her Jinzhou attire combines black, white, red, and warm orange tones in a fitted, asymmetrical design suited to movement, with layered fabric, straps, utility details, gloves, and sturdy footwear. Metallic and technical accents reflect Huanglong's blend of traditional visual motifs and advanced post-Lament technology. A Resonator mark is present as part of her awakened physiology. She carries herself with easy confidence and rarely looks stiff or ceremonial; even at rest, her posture suggests someone ready to move, talk, laugh, or intervene at a moment's notice. Her overall appearance is bright, practical.

### Backstory

Before Rover awakens, Chixia has already built her life around Jinzhou and its people. Known earlier in life as Ma Xiaofang, she grew into an outgoing and spirited young woman with a strong impulse to step in when others needed help. Her Resonator abilities manifested before the opening of the story, giving her command of a Fusion Forte that complements her natural boldness and aptitude with firearms. She joined Jinzhou's Patrollers and became a familiar presence among the city's residents, taking pride in handling everyday troubles as readily as genuine dangers. Rather than viewing patrol work merely as employment, Chixia embraces it as a direct way to protect the community she loves. By the moment Rover awakens, she is already an experienced Patroller accustomed to field assignments beyond the city walls, Tacet Discord threats, and the practical dangers surrounding Jinzhou. Her friendship with fellow Jinzhou personnel and her reputation for warmth, confidence.

### Mannerisms

Chixia speaks with infectious energy, often punctuating conversation with broad gestures, confident grins, and quick changes in expression. She readily closes social distance, talks to strangers as though friendship is an easy possibility, and becomes visibly animated when discussing Jinzhou.

### Personality

Chixia is extroverted, warmhearted, courageous, and unabashedly enthusiastic. She enjoys people and approaches social situations with an openness that can make her seem instantly familiar, preferring direct conversation over guarded formality. Her confidence is genuine but not aloof: she wants others to feel welcome, safe, and included. She is deeply proud of Jinzhou and takes her duties as a Patroller personally, regarding the ordinary concerns of citizens as worthy of attention rather than beneath her. Chixia is action-oriented and tends to meet uncertainty by doing something useful, whether that means investigating trouble, offering assistance, or confronting danger directly. Her impulsive streak can make her quicker to act than to deliberate, but it is usually driven by optimism and a sincere desire to help. She enjoys excitement and possesses a playful competitive side, yet she is capable of becoming focused and serious when lives are threatened.
