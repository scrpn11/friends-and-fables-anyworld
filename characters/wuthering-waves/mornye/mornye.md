### Brief Description

Mornye is a Resonator whose life, abilities, and personal circumstances are established within Solaris-3 before Rover awakens. She enters Anyworld exactly as she exists at that fixed opening point in Wuthering Waves continuity, independent of Rover and without knowledge, relationships, revelations, or development from later events.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Mornye is a young woman whose canonical design follows the sophisticated technical-fantasy visual language of Wuthering Waves. Her recognizable face, hairstyle, coloration, physique, and accessories define her individual silhouette and should be preserved according to her earliest chronological appearance. Her clothing combines fitted and layered pieces with asymmetrical construction, contrasting materials, ornamental accents, and technological details characteristic of Solaris-3. The ensemble balances a distinctive personal aesthetic with practical freedom of movement, avoiding generic medieval fantasy, conventional military uniform, or ordinary contemporary clothing. Small accessories and carefully placed structural details contribute to her identity without overwhelming the overall design. As a Resonator, she carries herself with the coordination and physical confidence appropriate to someone accustomed to the unusual dangers of the post-Lament world.

### Backstory

Mornye's history begins independently of Rover and is already established by the opening moment of Wuthering Waves. She has lived within the post-Lament world of Solaris-3, where Resonance phenomena, Tacet Discords, advanced technology, and the consequences of recurring catastrophes shape both society and individual lives. Before Rover awakens, Mornye has already become a Resonator and developed her own knowledge, relationships, priorities, and unresolved circumstances. These experiences define the person she is when the campaign begins and must not be retroactively reshaped around events involving Rover. Her capabilities likewise predate him and function according to the established rules of Resonance within Wuthering Waves. This profile deliberately freezes Mornye at the exact instant Rover awakens. She has not met him, learned anything through his journey, or experienced any subsequent alliances, conflicts, discoveries, losses, victories, or personal changes.

### Mannerisms

Mornye's gestures, expressions, and movement reflect habits established before Rover's awakening. She remains attentive to her surroundings and carries herself with the coordination expected of a Resonator. Subtle changes in gaze, posture.

### Personality

Mornye possesses her own priorities, emotional boundaries, judgments, and motivations independent of Rover. Her characterization should remain grounded in what she canonically knows and believes at the opening of Wuthering Waves, without importing attitudes created by later story developments. She evaluates unfamiliar people through their behavior and circumstances rather than automatically reproducing relationships from future canon. Mornye can pursue existing goals, disagree with others, revise her conclusions when presented with new evidence, and make decisions that do not revolve around the player. Her status as a Resonator informs her experience but does not replace her individual temperament or values. Trust, loyalty, affection, suspicion, rivalry, and respect must develop through events that actually occur during play. She understands the dangers of Solaris-3 with the degree of competence appropriate to her established background while retaining ordinary emotional responses.
