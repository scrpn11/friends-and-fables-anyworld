### Brief Description

Lingyang is a sincere, compassionate member of Jinzhou's Liondance Troupe and a guest from a different race. Once a solitary creature surviving in the wilderness, he chose to live among humans and now uses liondancing to bring courage, good fortune, and joy to others. At Rover's awakening, his identity, training, Forte.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Lingyang is a small-statured, athletic young man with pale skin and distinctive leonine traits. He has periwinkle eyes with slit pupils, sharp teeth, large catlike ears, and a long tail. His long white hair fades toward a light cream tone and is gathered into a fluffy ponytail; the ends of his ears are very dark brown, and his tail ends in a similarly dark tuft. His outfit is modeled after Jinzhou liondance attire, combining white, beige, brown, black, and red with layered fabrics, colorful tassels, bells, and decorative details suited to an energetic performer. The design permits the acrobatic movement required for dancing on Plum Blossom Poles while visually connecting him to the traditions he has adopted as his own. His Tacet Mark lies on the inner side of his right upper arm. Lingyang's expressions are lively and approachable, and his posture often carries the springy balance of someone equally accustomed to dancing, climbing, and fighting.

### Backstory

Lingyang remembers a barren wilderness rather than a clear birthplace or childhood. After fleeing the destruction of his homeland, he survived alone through instinct and hunting until observing a human military unit whose cooperation and willingness to protect one another fascinated him. Following traces of humanity eventually brought him to Jinzhou, where a liondance performance awakened a powerful sense of recognition and belonging. He approached the Liondance Troupe before he could properly speak their language and was taken in by senior members. Under their guidance he learned human customs, calligraphy, martial discipline, and liondancing, including ancient steps used to regulate his tremendous natural strength. He chose the name Lingyang and became a leading Liondancer, working tirelessly to master the art and use it to dispel fear rather than inspire it. His Forte manifests through cold despite his desire to bring warmth and encouragement to others. By Rover's awakening.

### Mannerisms

Lingyang is physically expressive, moving with light, practiced energy even outside performances. His tail may wag reflexively when he is happy, betraying emotions he might otherwise contain. He offers sincere lucky greetings, asks people about themselves with genuine curiosity.

### Personality

Lingyang is cheerful, sincere, compassionate, brave, and intensely curious about people. Having once observed humanity from outside it, he remains fascinated by individual personalities, customs, and stories and genuinely enjoys learning what matters to others. He wants his presence to relieve fear rather than create it, a concern shaped by experiences in which people recoiled from his claws, teeth, or unfamiliar appearance even after he helped them. Rather than becoming resentful, he worked to communicate kindness through liondancing, personalized lucky greetings, and dependable action. Lingyang is enthusiastic but not careless about his craft; he trains relentlessly and worries about living up to the cultural importance Jinzhou places on liondance. His natural physical power and hunting instincts coexist with considerable gentleness and self-restraint. He believes courage can be cultivated and that celebration can give people strength against fear. Although sociable.
