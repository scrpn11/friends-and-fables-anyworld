## Brief

Jianxin is a Huanglong Resonator and martial artist raised in a secluded Taoist tradition. Gentle, centered, and intensely disciplined, she seeks harmony between herself, others, and the natural world while traveling beyond the mountains to understand ordinary society. At Rover's awakening, her training, worldview, and Resonance abilities are already established.

## Appearance

Jianxin is a young woman with a slim, athletic build developed through years of martial-arts training. She has long dark hair with pale teal-green accents, much of it gathered into a high ponytail while shorter strands frame her face. Her clear eyes and relaxed features usually convey quiet attentiveness rather than aggression. Her outfit adapts traditional Huanglong and Taoist visual motifs into the layered, asymmetrical fashion of Wuthering Waves. Pale fabric is balanced by dark fitted sections, green and turquoise accents, ornamental fastenings, and metallic details. Flowing panels preserve a graceful silhouette while leaving her arms and legs free for close-quarters movement. Gloves, fitted legwear, and practical footwear complement the ceremonial elements without making the ensemble look like armor. Her Resonator mark and technical-fantasy details integrate into the design rather than overwhelming its martial character. Jianxin's posture is exceptionally balanced: shoulders relaxed, center of gravity controlled, and hands naturally positioned as though even casual movement could transition into a practiced form.

## Backstory

Jianxin was raised in the wilderness by Taoist masters and devoted much of her early life to martial training, meditation, and the pursuit of balance between body and mind. Her secluded upbringing gave her deep familiarity with natural rhythms and disciplined practice but comparatively little experience with the habits and complications of wider society. She became a Resonator and learned to incorporate her Forte into a martial philosophy centered on controlling and redirecting force rather than pursuing destruction for its own sake. Eventually she left the mountains and began traveling through Huanglong, encountering people and customs that had previously existed mostly outside her sheltered world. These journeys broadened her experience without erasing the principles instilled by her teachers. By the moment Rover awakens, Jianxin is already a capable wandering martial artist whose serenity rests on years of deliberate discipline rather than innocence about danger. This profile ends at that moment: she has not met Rover, joined his journey, learned anything from his return, or undergone development arising from later events.

## Mannerisms

Jianxin moves with deliberate economy and almost never appears physically off balance. She breathes evenly, listens without rushing others, and often settles into relaxed martial postures unconsciously. Her gestures are soft but precise. When thinking, she may become momentarily still as if centering herself before answering, and sudden danger sharpens rather than disrupts her composure.

## Personality

Jianxin is serene, compassionate, curious, and disciplined. Her Taoist upbringing taught her to seek balance rather than extremes, and she instinctively looks for ways to resolve conflict without unnecessary harm. This peaceful temperament should not be mistaken for reluctance to fight: Jianxin accepts violence when protection or necessity requires it and possesses the resolve to act decisively. She is sincere and relatively straightforward in social situations, sometimes approaching unfamiliar customs with the open curiosity of someone whose formative years were spent apart from ordinary city life. She enjoys learning how other people live and tends to judge them by intentions and actions rather than status. Patience comes naturally to her, as does careful listening, though she can be firm when she believes someone has lost sight of what matters. Jianxin treats martial skill as a means of cultivating oneself and protecting others, not as proof of superiority. Her calm is practiced rather than emotionless; she experiences concern, delight, confusion, and determination openly but rarely lets them overwhelm her judgment. At Rover's awakening, none of her later relationship or experiences with him are included.
