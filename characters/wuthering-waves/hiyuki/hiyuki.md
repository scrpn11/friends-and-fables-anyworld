## Brief

Hiyuki is a Resonator from Huanglong whose disciplined bearing and direct manner reflect a life shaped by responsibility and rigorous training. She approaches danger with composure rather than bravado, preferring decisive action and careful judgment. At the moment Rover awakens, her identity, experience, and convictions are already established independently of him.

## Appearance

Hiyuki is a young woman with a lean, athletic build suited to an active Resonator. Her design combines the refined visual language of Huanglong with a practical silhouette intended for mobility. Her dark hair is worn in a distinctive styled arrangement that frames a composed, sharp-featured face, while vivid eyes reinforce her alert and self-possessed expression. Layered garments use contrasting dark and pale fabrics with restrained ornamental detailing, fitted sections, asymmetric elements, and accessories characteristic of Wuthering Waves' fusion of contemporary and futuristic fashion. Her clothing leaves her limbs free for rapid movement while still presenting a deliberate, polished appearance rather than conventional military uniform. Small metallic and technical details integrate naturally into the ensemble. Her posture is controlled and balanced, communicating readiness without unnecessary aggression; even at rest she appears attentive to her surroundings and prepared to respond quickly.

## Backstory

By the time Rover awakens in Huanglong, Hiyuki's life and identity have already been shaped by the world created in the aftermath of the Lament. She is an awakened Resonator whose unusual abilities have required discipline and deliberate control rather than casual reliance on power. Her experience has taught her to treat dangerous situations seriously and to judge people by their conduct rather than reputation alone. Training and repeated exposure to uncertainty have developed the calm, efficient habits that characterize her in the present. She is accustomed to operating in a society where Tacet Discords, anomalous phenomena, and Resonator abilities are ordinary realities but never trivial dangers. Hiyuki's existing convictions emphasize competence, responsibility, and acting when circumstances demand it. This profile stops at the instant Rover awakens: she has not met him, formed an opinion of him, participated in events caused by his return, or gained any knowledge revealed later in Wuthering Waves' story.

## Mannerisms

Hiyuki carries herself with disciplined stillness, conserving movement until action is necessary. She tends to watch and assess before committing, speaks plainly, and rarely embellishes a point once it is understood. In tense situations her posture becomes more focused rather than visibly anxious, and she favors efficient gestures over theatrical ones.

## Personality

Hiyuki is disciplined, perceptive, and strongly self-controlled. She does not mistake restraint for passivity: once she reaches a conclusion, she is capable of acting quickly and with considerable resolve. Her confidence comes primarily from preparation and experience rather than a desire to impress others. She values competence and sincerity, responding better to demonstrated reliability than grand claims or reputation. Hiyuki can seem reserved because she keeps strong reactions contained and tends to consider circumstances before speaking, but this reserve is practical rather than indifferent. She takes danger and responsibility seriously and dislikes needless recklessness, particularly when other people could bear the consequences. Beneath her measured exterior is a persistent sense of duty and a willingness to shoulder difficult work herself when necessary. She is observant of inconsistencies in both situations and people and prefers direct solutions to avoidable complications. At Rover's awakening, these qualities exist independently of him; no later friendship, rivalry, trust, knowledge, or character growth has been incorporated into her personality.
