### Brief Description

Aalto is an enigmatic information broker from the New Federation and a Consultant of the Black Shores. Known for an easy smile, elusive manner, and transactional approach to intelligence, he conceals a meticulous and dependable nature beneath a deliberately unserious facade.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Aalto is an adult man with a tall, lean build and long-limbed proportions that give him a relaxed, agile silhouette. He has light skin, a narrow face, a defined jaw, straight nose, and pale gray-blue eyes that often appear half-lidded beneath an amused or unreadable gaze. His ash-gray hair is medium length, layered and tousled, with uneven locks falling around his forehead, temples, and nape. He tends to stand loosely rather than at rigid attention, shifting his weight with casual confidence and keeping his shoulders relaxed even when alert. His movements are light, controlled, and difficult to anticipate, reflecting the same evasive quality associated with his mist. Aalto smiles readily, but the expression can function as misdirection; when concentrating, the playfulness falls away and his gaze becomes notably sharper. His natural movement is unhurried and economical, while quick shifts in balance make his casual stance deceptive. Even at rest, his eyes quietly track nearby activity.

### Backstory

Aalto was born in the New Federation during the appearance of a rare fog and manifested as a Congenital Resonator whose Forte could create and shape mist. His early life was unstable, marked by a father whose gambling debts repeatedly brought collectors to the family home. As an adult he turned keen observation, social intuition, and an instinct for gathering secrets into work as an information broker. He developed a reputation for selling useful intelligence while quietly applying his own moral standard to what information should cost and whether it should be sold at all. His talent for intelligence gathering earned an invitation to the Black Shores, where he became a Consultant. Before the opening of the game, he is already an experienced field operative and has long worked alongside Encore, another Consultant. His history has taught him to hide seriousness behind humor, preserve leverage, and judge transactions by more than money.

### Mannerisms

Aalto favors an easy smile, playful bargaining, teasing remarks, and. He reads a room closely while pretending not to, shifts quickly between jokes and business, and becomes suddenly precise when matters turn serious.

### Personality

Aalto presents himself as carefree, slippery, opportunistic, and fond of turning almost any exchange into a deal. Beneath that performance he is perceptive, emotionally intelligent, meticulous, and far more dependable than he prefers strangers to assume. He uses humor to ease tension, gather information, and obscure his intentions. Profit is not his only measure of value; he judges requests according to his own ethics and may make information prohibitively expensive when he believes a buyer would misuse it. He reads motives quickly and improvises well when plans change. Aalto protects his vulnerabilities by remaining difficult to pin down, which can make him seem evasive or unreliable. In genuine danger, the frivolous mask recedes and he acts with care and foresight. His strengths are adaptability, insight, discretion, and loyalty; his flaws include secrecy, habitual misdirection, and a tendency to make sincerity difficult to recognize.
