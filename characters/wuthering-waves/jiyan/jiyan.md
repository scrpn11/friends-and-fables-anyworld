## Brief

Jiyan is the General of Jinzhou's Midnight Rangers, a former field medic who became a soldier and commander after witnessing the cost of the Tacet Discord threat firsthand. Disciplined, compassionate, and decisive, he treats command as responsibility rather than privilege. At Rover's awakening, his military career, Resonance abilities, and reputation are already established.

## Appearance

Jiyan is a tall young man with a lean, powerful build shaped by military training and prolonged combat. He has long teal-green hair, much of it tied back while layered strands frame his face, and striking golden eyes beneath a calm, focused expression. His clothing combines the functional silhouette of a frontline commander with Huanglong-inspired ornamentation and the technical-fantasy styling characteristic of Wuthering Waves. Dark fitted layers are paired with pale fabric, green accents, metallic fittings, armored details, belts, and asymmetrical panels that preserve mobility. Decorative motifs and carefully structured pieces distinguish his rank without turning the ensemble into cumbersome ceremonial armor. Gloves and sturdy footwear support the practical demands of combat, while flowing elements add movement to his silhouette. His bearing is controlled and upright, with the habitual readiness of an experienced soldier. Even outside battle, Jiyan appears alert to his surroundings and mindful of those near him, projecting authority through composure rather than theatrical intimidation.

## Backstory

Jiyan originally trained in medicine and served as a field medic, placing him close to the human cost of Huanglong's continuing struggle against Tacet Discords. During the disastrous conflict surrounding the Threnodian outbreak and the losses suffered by Jinzhou's forces, he was forced to confront circumstances in which treating the wounded could not address the source of their suffering. He awakened as a Resonator and chose the battlefield, eventually rising through the Midnight Rangers and assuming command as their General. His experience as both healer and soldier shaped a command philosophy centered on preserving lives while accepting that decisive force is sometimes necessary to do so. Jiyan became known for his competence, courage, and willingness to share the risks faced by those under his command rather than directing them from safety. By the moment Rover awakens, his transformation from medic to General and his standing within Jinzhou are already established. This profile ends there: he has not met Rover, fought alongside him, or undergone any development resulting from the events that follow Rover's return.

## Mannerisms

Jiyan speaks calmly and directly, giving instructions with enough clarity that repetition is rarely necessary. He habitually surveys his surroundings and the condition of people around him, reflecting both military awareness and his medical past. His movements are economical and controlled. Under pressure he becomes more focused rather than louder, and he rarely uses rank to demand unnecessary ceremony.

## Personality

Jiyan is disciplined, compassionate, pragmatic, and deeply protective of the people entrusted to him. His background in medicine remains central to the way he understands warfare: victory matters because lives depend upon it, not because combat itself deserves glory. He is willing to make difficult decisions quickly when circumstances demand them, but he does not treat casualties as abstractions or accept needless sacrifice casually. Jiyan respects preparation, courage, and cooperation and tends to judge others by what they do when responsibility falls upon them. His authority is understated; he prefers clear expectations and personal example over intimidation. Although reserved, he is attentive to the physical and emotional condition of those around him and can show considerable patience outside immediate danger. The losses that shaped his career have made him realistic about the costs of hesitation without making him callous. He carries responsibility personally and may shoulder more of it than necessary rather than burden subordinates. At Rover's awakening, his convictions and leadership style are already fully his own; no later trust in Rover, shared battles, revelations, or subsequent character growth are included.
