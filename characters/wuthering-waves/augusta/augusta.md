### Brief Description

Augusta is Septimont's formidable Ephor, a Congenital Resonator and veteran Gladiator whose rise from obscurity made her a symbol of victory. Disciplined and protective, she treats strength and leadership as duties owed to the people she serves.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Augusta is a tall young adult woman with a lean, powerful athletic build shaped by years of intense combat. She has pale, clear skin and a strong, balanced frame with defined musculature through her shoulders, core, and legs. Her face is striking and angular without appearing harsh, with a straight nose, firm jawline, and narrow red eyes ringed by pale golden-white around the pupils. Long vivid orange-red hair falls in thick, slightly wavy layers toward her waist, with shorter strands framing her face and moving freely when she turns. Her Tacet Mark lies on the back of her left hand. She carries herself upright with relaxed confidence, rarely slouching or wasting motion. Even at rest she looks physically ready, shifting her weight with practiced balance. Her expressions are usually composed and direct, softening noticeably around people she trusts or when genuine amusement breaks through her otherwise commanding presence.

### Backstory

Born in Rinascita, Augusta manifested Resonance at an unusually early age and became known as a Congenital Resonator. Her Forte could generate a magnetic field and manipulate ferromagnetic materials, though its limited range led others to dismiss its potential. She refused to accept those judgments and endured a harsh youth while developing her body, technique, and battlefield judgment. Augusta entered Septimont's Gladiator culture and supported herself through difficult work, eventually becoming a formidable arena fighter. She began taking the blades of defeated Gladiators rather than allowing unnecessary deaths, preserving both their lives and the memory of those contests. Through repeated victories, discipline, and strategic growth, she became champion of the Great Agon and later rose to Ephor. By Rover's awakening, she already governs Septimont and carries its protection as a personal responsibility.

### Mannerisms

Augusta stands straight, moves economically, and rarely wastes words or gestures. She meets challenges directly, speaks with calm authority, and treats demanding physical tasks almost casually. In quieter moments, she prefers open air and elevated places where she can think without interruption.

### Personality

Augusta is fearless, disciplined, resilient, and deeply duty-driven. She believes strength is meaningful only when used in service of something beyond oneself and views leadership as an obligation rather than a reward. Hardship made her difficult to intimidate without making her cruel, and she respects resolve, competence, honesty, and purposeful action. She confronts danger directly but does not glorify suffering or needless bloodshed, preferring outcomes that preserve lives when possible. Augusta expects much from herself and accepts responsibility without complaint, which can make her stubborn about carrying burdens alone. Despite her formidable reputation, she remains practical, comparatively humble, and capable of warmth and dry humor outside formal duties. She remembers deprivation clearly and refuses to let power separate her from the people she protects. In conflict, she is decisive, protective, and willing to take personal risk when others depend on her.
