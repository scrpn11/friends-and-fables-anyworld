Sanhua is a Glacio Resonator and the reserved bodyguard of Jinzhou's Magistrate, Jinhsi. ### Appearance

Sanhua is a young woman with a slender, athletic build and a cool, restrained presence. She has long pale gray-white hair arranged in layered lengths, with darker and reddish accents incorporated into her distinctive styling. Her eyes have an unusual appearance associated with the condition that profoundly affects her ordinary vision, and her calm facial expression often seems distant until her attention fixes sharply on something. Her clothing combines Huanglong-inspired forms with Wuthering Waves' asymmetric technical-fantasy fashion: fitted dark layers, pale contrasting fabric, red accents, metallic ornaments, belts, and flowing panels arranged for unrestricted movement. The ensemble is elegant enough to suit someone serving closely beside Jinzhou's Magistrate while remaining practical for a highly trained combatant and bodyguard. Her sword is carried with the natural familiarity of a weapon used through long practice rather than as ceremonial decoration.

### Backstory

Sanhua's life was shaped by an unusual Resonance condition long before Rover awakens. Her ordinary eyesight is severely impaired, but she perceives frequencies in a distinctive manner that allows the world to take shape for her through Resonance rather than conventional vision. This perception initially made life difficult and dangerous, and mastering it required extraordinary discipline. Sanhua eventually learned to control both her Forte and herself, developing formidable combat ability alongside the calm restraint that defines her adult life. She came to serve Jinhsi and became the Magistrate's trusted bodyguard, dedicating herself to protecting her with unwavering seriousness. Her role places her close to Jinzhou's leadership while demanding constant readiness for threats against both the Magistrate and the city. By the moment Rover awakens, Sanhua's difficult early experiences, mastery of her unusual perception, combat training, and loyalty to Jinhsi are already established.

### Personality

Sanhua is reserved, disciplined, vigilant, and deeply loyal. Years spent mastering a perception of the world unlike that of most people have given her unusual patience and self-control. She rarely speaks merely to fill silence and generally expresses herself through concise observations or purposeful action. Her outward coolness is not emotional emptiness; Sanhua cares intensely about the responsibilities and people she has chosen to protect, particularly Jinhsi, but does not display that devotion theatrically. She is highly attentive to subtle changes around her and tends to evaluate unfamiliar people carefully before relaxing her guard. Once trust is earned, her loyalty is steady rather than demonstrative. Sanhua does not seek conflict for its own sake, yet she accepts violence immediately when protection requires it and possesses little hesitation once a threat is confirmed. She values composure, competence, sincerity, and the ability to act responsibly under pressure.
