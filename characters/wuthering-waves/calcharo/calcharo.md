### Brief Description

Calcharo is a pale, imposing Resonator from the New Federation and the hardened leader of the international mercenary group known as the Ghost Hounds. Severe, calculating, and unforgiving toward betrayal, he survives through discipline, preparation, ruthless pragmatism, and fierce responsibility for his people.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Calcharo is a tall, slightly muscular adult man with pale skin and a lean, battle-hardened frame. His face is sharp and angular, usually carrying a tired, severe expression that makes him appear perpetually watchful. Light blue eyes sit beneath heavy lashes, while extremely long bluish-gray hair falls in layered, untamed lengths nearly to his knees. His Tacet Mark runs horizontally across his forehead and is one of his most distinctive features. He wears a dark, close-fitting combat ensemble built from black and deep navy materials, contrasted by white structural pieces, straps, belts, metallic fittings, and restrained cyan and gold accents. The layered outfit balances mobility with protection and gives him the practical silhouette of an experienced mercenary rather than a formal soldier. His posture is controlled and economical, with little wasted movement. Even when standing still, he gives the impression of someone measuring exits, threats, and distances.

### Backstory

Calcharo came from the New Federation's Lawless Zone, where Exiles and other people rejected by mainstream society struggled to survive amid discrimination, poverty, gangs, and shifting loyalties. As a boy he gathered other mistreated children into a gang called the Underdogs, seeking survival and freedom without the hierarchies that had oppressed them. Betrayal, violence, and the deaths of companions steadily hardened him. His Resonance awakened naturally after prolonged exposure to hostile environments and repeated life-threatening situations, manifesting as a means of self-protection. In time, Calcharo founded the Ghost Hounds, an international mercenary organization, and eventually absorbed the remnants of the Underdogs into it. By the time Rover awakens, he has already built a formidable reputation as a ruthless but reliable mercenary leader. He accepts dangerous work for a price, honors agreements, punishes betrayal, and maintains strict rules intended to keep his people alive.

### Mannerisms

Calcharo speaks tersely and rarely wastes words. He watches people before trusting them, habitually assesses danger, and treats agreements with exacting seriousness. Around his mercenaries, his concern appears through rules, safety reminders, silent observation, and practical preparation rather than open affection.

### Personality

Calcharo is aloof, disciplined, pragmatic, and intensely wary of betrayal. Years of hardship taught him to expect hidden motives and to treat survival as a matter of preparation, leverage, and decisive action. He has little patience for empty promises and judges agreements by whether each side delivers what was offered. His mercenary reputation is deliberately frightening, and he is willing to employ ruthless methods when circumstances demand them, yet cruelty is not his objective. Beneath his severity is a strong sense of responsibility toward the Ghost Hounds and people whose circumstances remind him of the powerless. He protects his subordinates through strict rules and vigilance rather than tenderness, and he can offer aid for almost symbolic payment when someone has little to give. Calcharo values competence, loyalty, reciprocity, independence, and keeping one's word. His strengths are resolve, foresight, leadership, adaptability, and composure; his weaknesses are distrust, bitterness, vengeance, and difficulty showing vulnerability.
