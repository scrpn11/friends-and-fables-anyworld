### Brief Description

Ciaccona Toccata is a free-spirited wandering bard from Rinascita who gathers the stories of ordinary people, heroes, miracles, hardship, and loss and transforms them into poetry and song. A gifted Aero Resonator, she travels in search of inspiration and believes even overlooked lives deserve to be remembered.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Ciaccona is a graceful young woman with fair skin, bright green eyes, pointed ears, and vivid red hair arranged in loose layered locks with a small braid falling over one shoulder. Dark hornlike ornaments sweep upward from either side of her head, accented with green decorative pieces that complement her traveling attire. Her clothing favors an airy Rinascitan palette of pale blue, white, teal, green, and warm metallic details, combining elegant draped fabrics with fitted pieces suited to a performer who spends much of her life on the road. Flowing panels and ribbons give her silhouette movement even at rest, while musical and ornamental motifs reinforce her identity as a bard without resembling formal stage costume. She carries herself lightly and expressively, with the easy balance of someone accustomed to performing before strangers. Her overall impression is colorful, romantic, approachable, and unmistakably artistic.

### Backstory

Born Ciaccona Toccata in Rinascita, Ciaccona displayed an extraordinary relationship with music from childhood. She mastered instruments with unusual ease and perceived melodies not merely as notes but as scenes and stories connected to instruments and their performers. Her Forte, Woven Melodies, allowed those perceptions to take form through her poetry and music. The Order eventually became aware of her gift and regarded her as a promising future chorister of the Toccata family. Forte examinations found her Resonance stable despite high Resonant Criticality, with no recorded history of Overclocking. Rather than confining her life to expectations placed upon her, Ciaccona became a wandering bard. By the time Rover awakens, she is already traveling throughout Rinascita, collecting accounts from common people as readily as legends of saints and heroes, preserving joy, turmoil, suffering, death, and farewell in songs meant to carry their stories onward.

### Mannerisms

Ciaccona often responds to the world as though it has supplied the opening line of a new poem. She listens closely when others tell stories, brightens visibly when inspiration strikes, and slips naturally between ordinary conversation and lyrical phrasing. Her gestures are animated but graceful.

### Personality

Ciaccona is free-spirited, romantic, sincere, passionate, and intensely curious about the lives unfolding around her. She approaches travel as an opportunity to listen rather than merely to see, finding artistic value in ordinary experiences that others might dismiss as too small for history. Although naturally cheerful and whimsical, she does not believe songs should contain only beauty. Suffering, turmoil, death, and farewell belong beside miracles and heroic deeds because a story loses its truth when inconvenient emotions are removed. Her empathy makes her an attentive listener who cares about the person behind a tale as much as the tale itself. Ciaccona possesses confidence in her talent without demanding admiration, regarding herself primarily as a storyteller entrusted with carrying other people's experiences forward. Independence matters deeply to her, and she follows inspiration wherever it leads. Beneath her playfulness is a serious conviction that memory, poetry.
