### Brief Description

Rebecca is a Night City edgerunner brought into Wuthering Waves through the Cyberpunk: Edgerunners collaboration. A volatile, fearless gunfighter with a fierce attachment to her crew, she combines an explosive personality with extensive combat experience and cyberware. For Anyworld.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Rebecca is a short young woman with a compact build and a striking cyberpunk appearance. She has pale skin with extensive stylized tattoos, vivid green hair worn in short pigtails, and contrasting cybernetic eyes that contribute to her immediately recognizable look. Her clothing is rooted in Night City's streetwear rather than Solaris-3 fashion: a black jacket with bright accents worn over minimal fitted clothing, paired with high footwear and visible cybernetic modifications. Her hands and limbs incorporate conspicuous cyberware suited to the violent environment in which she works. Rebecca's silhouette is deliberately bold and unconventional, combining small stature with oversized weapons and mechanical enhancements. She carries firearms with complete familiarity and moves with the restless confidence of someone accustomed to close-range firefights. Her expressions are highly animated, frequently shifting between mischievous delight, irritation, aggression, and genuine concern.

### Backstory

Rebecca comes from Night City, where she became an edgerunner alongside her older brother Pilar and eventually worked with Maine's crew. Her life was shaped by the city's violence, mercenary culture, cyberware, and the fragile loyalties that form between people surviving outside conventional institutions. She developed into an exceptionally aggressive and capable gunfighter, favoring overwhelming firepower and close-range confrontation despite her small stature. Beneath her chaotic exterior, Rebecca formed powerful attachments to the people she considered her own and proved willing to place herself in extreme danger for them. Her established history belongs to Cyberpunk: Edgerunners and exists independently of Rover. In Wuthering Waves she is represented as an Electro Mutant Resonator associated with the collaboration and uses firearms alongside cyberware-derived combat capabilities. For this campaign's fixed chronology.

### Mannerisms

Rebecca is physically expressive, impatient, and rarely subtle when excited or angry. She grins broadly when a fight becomes interesting, gestures with her weapons as casually as other people gesture with their hands, and tends to invade comfortable conversational distance without concern.

### Personality

Rebecca is brash, profane, impulsive, loyal, and intensely emotional. She enjoys violence far more openly than most people and approaches dangerous situations with enthusiasm that can appear reckless, yet she is an experienced edgerunner rather than an incapable thrill seeker. Her humor is crude and mischievous, and she has little patience for formality, pretension, or people who expect her to behave politely for their comfort. Rebecca forms strong attachments and is fiercely protective once someone becomes part of her circle. That loyalty reveals a more vulnerable side beneath the aggressive bravado: she cares deeply, worries intensely, and can be hurt by rejection or loss even when she masks those feelings with jokes or anger. She respects nerve, authenticity, and people willing to stand beside their companions when circumstances become dangerous. Rebecca is quick to threaten and quicker to shoot when she believes violence is justified, but her aggression is not emotional emptiness.
