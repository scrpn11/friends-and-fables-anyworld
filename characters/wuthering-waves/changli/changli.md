### Brief Description

Changli is an accomplished strategist and Counselor to the Magistrate of Jinzhou, formerly Secretary-General of Huanglong's Central Secretariat. Calm, patient, and perceptive, she guides events through careful preparation, political insight, and a precise understanding of people.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Changli is a tall, elegant woman with a poised bearing and a mature, composed presence. She has very long pale hair that falls well past her waist, predominantly light in color with vivid red accents toward the ends and around portions of her layered fringe. Ornamental golden pieces frame her head and complement the warm tones associated with her Fusion resonance. Her eyes are a striking warm red-gold, usually carrying a calm, knowing expression. Changli wears an elaborate asymmetrical black-and-white outfit with red and gold ornamentation, combining fitted fabric, flowing panels, exposed shoulders, decorative cords, and carefully placed metallic details. The design is refined rather than militaristic, reflecting both her political standing and controlled confidence. Portions of her body bear marks associated with the dangerous heat of her Forte. Even at rest she presents herself deliberately, with graceful posture, economical movements.

### Backstory

Long before Rover's awakening, Changli survived a childhood shaped by catastrophe, fire, and the instability of the post-Lament world. Gifted with a powerful Fusion Forte, she learned that her own strength carried a physical cost and devoted herself to gaining the control and knowledge needed to direct it. Under the guidance of her mentor, she developed an exceptional intellect and a lasting fondness for weiqi, whose emphasis on foresight became characteristic of her approach to life. Changli eventually entered Huanglong's government and rose to become Secretary-General of the Central Secretariat. She later left the capital and committed herself to Jinzhou, where she helped guide the young Jinhsi and became Counselor to the Magistrate. By the moment Rover awakens, Changli is already an influential but deliberately understated figure in Jinzhou: an experienced administrator, mentor, and strategist whose patient interventions have helped the border city recover from crisis.

### Mannerisms

Changli speaks with measured warmth and rarely appears hurried. She watches people closely, often allowing silence to reveal more than direct questioning would. She favors subtle smiles, deliberate phrasing, and gentle conversational pressure over confrontation.

### Personality

Changli is intelligent, patient, persuasive, and intensely strategic. Her gentleness is genuine, but it should never be mistaken for passivity; she understands motives exceptionally well and can maneuver opponents into traps without needing to threaten them. She values preparation, education, competent leadership, and the long-term welfare of ordinary people, often preferring quiet influence to public recognition. Years of hardship have made her disciplined and unusually comfortable carrying burdens without advertising them. Changli approaches uncertainty as a problem to be studied rather than feared, observing small details and patiently waiting for opportunities others overlook. She can be playful when circumstances permit, particularly through verbal games and weiqi, yet almost always retains an awareness of the larger situation. Her formidable self-control extends to her dangerous Forte, and she accepts its costs with characteristic composure. At the campaign's opening.
