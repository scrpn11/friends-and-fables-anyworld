# Danjin

## Brief Description

Danjin is a young wandering ranger from Huanglong who travels the land pursuing wrongdoers and refining her understanding of justice. Polite, earnest, and uncompromising toward cruelty, she is a Mutant Resonator whose unusual Forte allows her to shape blood into a crimson blade.

## Appearance

Danjin is a slender young woman with fair skin and vivid red hair, styled partly into a youthful bun with loose locks, a small braid, and a traditional hair ornament at her temple. Her eyes are normally clear and expressive, while exerting her Forte can cause her pupils to redden noticeably. She dresses in a mobile, traditional Huanglong-inspired outfit dominated by deep crimson, black, and pale accents, combining fitted fabric with flowing ribbons and ornamental details rather than heavy protection. A ring-shaped Yingluo ornament sits at her neck, while a crimson garter circles her right thigh. A bright jade pendant hangs prominently at her chest; the treasured piece is incomplete, with part of its original circular form missing. Her Tacet Mark lies on her upper left arm. Danjin's overall appearance is neat, lively, and distinctly youthful, with graceful traditional details contrasting the worn sword and dangerous blood-red power associated with her life as a wandering ranger.

## Backstory

Danjin was raised with strong lessons about righteousness and virtue, symbolized by the jade pendant carrying her family's hopes for the person she would become. Her Awakening occurred at an uncertain point under overwhelming emotion and was unusually violent, leaving her classified as a Mutant Resonator whose frequency resembles human blood. She trained extensively to control a Forte capable of shaping her own blood into a weapon, using a hand mirror from her master as part of the discipline that helped her remain composed when surrounded by scarlet. Family tragedy eventually sent Danjin onto the road in pursuit of a particular criminal. What began as a deeply personal search for vengeance broadened as she encountered thieves, bandits, deceivers, and victims across Huanglong. By the moment Rover awakens, Danjin is already an independent wandering ranger who pursues wrongdoers while continually testing and refining her understanding of good, evil, justice.

## Mannerisms

Danjin speaks politely and directly, often with the courteous bearing of a traditional wandering ranger. Her movements are controlled and graceful, especially around her sword, and she watches suspicious behavior closely. Her stern composure softens noticeably around sweets.

## Personality

Danjin is earnest, courteous, compassionate, and governed by an unusually firm moral compass. She believes wrongdoing should be confronted rather than ignored, and deception rarely persuades her to overlook behavior she considers cruel or unjust. Though her straightforward judgments can initially appear naive, they come from experience rather than sheltered idealism: Danjin has traveled alone, encountered dangerous people, and deliberately chosen to keep helping strangers despite what she has seen. She takes the responsibilities of a wandering ranger seriously and continually questions what righteousness truly requires instead of treating virtue as an empty slogan. Her calm manners conceal formidable resolve and a capacity for frightening violence when facing those she judges genuinely wicked. Outside such confrontations she remains considerate, modest, and surprisingly sweet-natured. Small pleasures reveal the young woman beneath the disciplined swordswoman.
