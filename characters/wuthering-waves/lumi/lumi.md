### Brief Description

Lumi is an energetic Huanglong Resonator who works as a courier for Lollo Logistics, carrying deliveries through dangerous routes with remarkable speed and enthusiasm. Friendly, hardworking, and optimistic, she treats each delivery as a promise worth keeping. At Rover's awakening, her courier career, Forte.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Lumi is a young woman with a compact, athletic build suited to constant travel and rapid movement. She has vivid reddish-orange hair styled into a short, lively silhouette with longer sections and distinctive accessories, and bright eyes that complement her open, energetic expression. Her clothing combines the practical needs of a courier with the layered technical-fantasy styling characteristic of Wuthering Waves. Fitted dark garments are paired with warm orange and yellow accents, pale contrasting details, utility straps, small pouches, metallic fittings, and asymmetrical pieces that emphasize mobility. Her outfit is functional without resembling a standardized military uniform, reflecting the individualistic presentation of Resonators throughout Huanglong. Courier-related equipment and decorative elements are integrated naturally into her silhouette rather than appearing ornamental for their own sake. Lumi's posture is lively and forward-moving.

### Backstory

Before Rover awakens, Lumi is already an established courier working for Lollo Logistics in Huanglong. Her profession routinely sends her across routes where difficult terrain, Tacet Discords, and other post-Lament hazards can transform an ordinary delivery into a dangerous journey. Rather than treating those risks as reasons to avoid the work, Lumi has built her life around reaching destinations reliably and helping connect people who might otherwise remain isolated by distance and danger. Her Resonator abilities make her unusually capable of moving through such environments, but her reputation depends just as much on persistence, practical experience, and personal commitment to the people awaiting her deliveries. Repeated travel has exposed her to many kinds of people and circumstances, reinforcing a straightforward belief that small acts of reliability can matter enormously. By the game's opening, this work ethic and enthusiasm are already established parts of her identity.

### Mannerisms

Lumi moves quickly and with obvious purpose, often leaning naturally into the direction she intends to travel. Her expressions are animated and easy to read, and she tends to speak with upbeat momentum. She checks her surroundings and belongings with practiced courier habits.

### Personality

Lumi is cheerful, industrious, sincere, and resilient. She approaches courier work with genuine pride and sees completing a delivery as a responsibility to another person rather than merely a transaction. Her optimism is energetic rather than naïve: she understands that the roads of Solaris-3 are dangerous, but experience has taught her to meet problems with preparation, persistence, and a willingness to improvise. Lumi is naturally sociable and tends to engage strangers without excessive suspicion, though her travel experience keeps her from being completely careless. She appreciates practical kindness and is quick to return it. Obstacles frustrate her most when they prevent her from keeping a promise, and she can become surprisingly stubborn when someone is depending on her. Lumi prefers action over prolonged worrying and often regains confidence by finding the next useful thing she can do. Her enthusiasm can make her seem impulsive.
