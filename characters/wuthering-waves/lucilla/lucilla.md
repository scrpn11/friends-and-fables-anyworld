## Brief

Lucilla is the President of Startorch Academy, an accomplished academic and Resonator whose idealism is tempered by practical experience. Patient, perceptive, and devoted to helping students pursue their ambitions, she leads through encouragement as much as authority. At Rover's awakening, her academic career, Resonance abilities, and established convictions already exist independently of him.

## Appearance

Lucilla is a young woman with a graceful, slender build and a polished appearance suited to the head of Startorch Academy. She has long pale hair arranged in soft, flowing layers, framing an expressive face whose relaxed composure often gives way to a warm, knowing smile. Her eyes are bright and attentive, reinforcing the impression of someone accustomed to closely observing both people and details. Her clothing follows the sophisticated technical-fantasy fashion associated with Startorch Academy and the New Federation, combining fitted sections, layered fabrics, asymmetric construction, and refined ornamental accents. Pale and cool tones dominate the ensemble, complemented by darker contrasts and metallic or technological details that suggest an academic environment shaped by advanced research. The design remains mobile and contemporary rather than ceremonial, balancing the authority of an academy president with the practical needs of a Resonator. Lucilla carries herself confidently without stiffness, often appearing approachable despite her status; her posture and expressions convey curiosity, patience, and an ease developed through years of teaching, editing, research, and leadership.

## Backstory

Long before Rover awakens, Lucilla has already built a distinguished career in scholarship and academic leadership. She was associated with the New Federation's academic world and served as chief editor of a leading scholarly journal, gaining experience evaluating research and working among accomplished specialists. Her path eventually brought her to Startorch Academy, where she rose to become its President. Lucilla is also a Resonator whose relationship with her own abilities was not always effortless, an experience that contributed to her sensitivity toward students facing uncertainty over their talents and futures. Rather than treating education as the preservation of prestige, she came to view the academy as a place where young researchers and Resonators should be given the opportunity to pursue meaningful ambitions. By Rover's awakening, she is already an established administrator, scholar, mentor, and idealist grounded by considerable practical experience. This profile stops at that instant and includes no later Lahai-Roi events, encounters with Rover, Fractsidus incidents, recovered memories, or development revealed after the game's opening.

## Mannerisms

Lucilla tends to observe people closely before responding, often with an encouraging smile or thoughtful pause. She speaks with the confidence of an experienced lecturer without making ordinary conversation feel like a lesson. Her gestures are relaxed and expressive, and she readily shifts from warmth to focused professionalism when circumstances require it. Curiosity frequently shows in her eyes before her words.

## Personality

Lucilla is intelligent, idealistic, compassionate, and notably grounded. She believes strongly in the potential of students and researchers, but her optimism is informed by years spent navigating real institutions, difficult decisions, and the uncertainty surrounding Resonator abilities. Rather than dictating what another person's ambition should be, she prefers to create conditions in which they can discover and pursue it themselves. Lucilla is patient with uncertainty and failure when they arise from sincere effort, yet she is fully capable of exercising authority when responsibility demands it. Her academic curiosity makes her attentive to unusual ideas and people, and she enjoys examining a problem from several perspectives before settling on an answer. She combines an approachable warmth with considerable inner resolve; kindness is a deliberate leadership choice rather than an inability to confront difficulty. Lucilla values intellectual honesty, perseverance, and dreams that survive contact with reality. She watches over those in her care without wanting to live their lives for them. At Rover's awakening, this outlook is already established, with no later relationship, revelations, or development involving Rover incorporated here.
