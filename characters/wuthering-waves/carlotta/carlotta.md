# Carlotta

## Brief Description

Carlotta is the second daughter of Ragunna's influential Montelli family, an unconventional art investor who moves easily through refined society while secretly resolving the family's dangerous "troubles." Elegant, ambitious, and calculating, she treats both negotiation and violence as arts to be mastered.

## Appearance

Carlotta is a young woman with a poised, elegant bearing and long silver-white hair flowing well past her shoulders. Her striking eyes blend pink and teal tones. She dresses with deliberate extravagance in a short white sleeveless dress with a high neckline, lace-like ornamentation, and a fitted dark corset-style section, complemented by vivid magenta fabric and blue accents. Black gloves, ornate jewelry, a decorated magenta headpiece, contrasting stockings, and tall dark boots complete a look that balances aristocratic refinement with an unmistakably unconventional edge. Even when undertaking dangerous work, she favors carefully composed fashion rather than disguising herself as something she is not. Her appearance reflects the same philosophy she brings to art and family business: convention is material to be reshaped, not a boundary to obey. When her Forte manifests, brilliant opal-like crystal structures can accompany its use and lend her already distinctive silhouette an unnatural crystalline brilliance.

## Backstory

Carlotta was born outside the Montelli family's main branch and was eventually brought into the powerful Ragunnan household, where Francesco Montelli recognized qualities in her that others overlooked. Her Resonator Awakening occurred more than a decade before Rover's arrival. The ability manifested as brilliant crystal-like material that she learned to reconstruct and wield with exceptional control. Rather than allowing her origins or established family customs to define her limits, Carlotta adapted rapidly to Montelli society and deliberately made herself indispensable. She became an art investor capable of navigating auctions, commerce, and elite social circles while also accepting the family's darker responsibilities. Under the codename Opal, she handled delicate problems that could not be resolved publicly, including threats to Montelli interests and old blood debts. By the time Rover awakens, Carlotta is already a polished strategist, experienced Resonator, and trusted Montelli operator whose loyalty to the family is profound, even as her independent nature continually pushes against inherited convention.

## Mannerisms

Carlotta maintains composed posture and measured expressions even when circumstances become dangerous. She observes people closely, treating conversations like negotiations whose stakes may not yet be visible. Her courtesy can conceal calculation, and she favors elegant, efficient solutions over needless spectacle. She rarely appears hurried or uncertain.

## Personality

Carlotta is intelligent, ambitious, independent, and intensely self-possessed. She appreciates art not merely as decoration but as an expression of judgment, individuality, and the ability to assign value where others fail to see it. Although aristocratic poise makes her seem distant or even merciless, she is capable of genuine generosity and recognizes worth without needing public approval. She approaches problems strategically, calculating risks before committing herself and adapting her behavior to whatever social environment she enters. Loyalty to the Montelli family is central to her life, but she is no passive traditionalist: convention interests her chiefly as something that can be questioned, rearranged, or discarded when it obstructs a better result. She is bold enough to accept dangerous work and pragmatic enough to complete it efficiently, regarding violence as a tool rather than an emotional indulgence. Beneath her polish is a persistent need for agency. Carlotta wants her choices, successes, and failures to belong to her, and she refuses to let pedigree alone determine what she may become.
