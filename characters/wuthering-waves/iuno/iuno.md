## Brief

Iuno is a Septimont Resonator known as a priestess who reads guidance in the moon and stars. Thoughtful, composed, and quietly compassionate, she combines spiritual conviction with a willingness to act rather than merely observe. At the moment Rover awakens, her identity, beliefs, Resonance abilities, and place within Septimont already exist independently of him.

## Appearance

Iuno is a young woman with a graceful, athletic build and an elegant presence shaped by Septimont's distinctive culture. She has long pale hair arranged in flowing layers, framing a refined face and striking eyes that give her an attentive, almost contemplative expression. Her clothing combines fitted dark fabrics with pale and metallic accents, asymmetrical construction, and ornamental elements that evoke celestial imagery without sacrificing freedom of movement. Exposed sections and layered panels create the elaborate silhouette typical of Wuthering Waves Resonator designs, while jewelry-like details and carefully placed accessories reinforce her priestly association with lunar observation. Her ensemble mixes ceremonial elegance with practical construction suitable for a capable Resonator rather than presenting her as a purely cloistered religious figure. She carries herself upright and calmly, with measured gestures and an easy physical balance. Even when relaxed, her gaze often suggests that part of her attention remains fixed on patterns beyond the immediately visible world.

## Backstory

Before Rover awakens, Iuno is already established in Septimont as a priestess associated with interpreting the moon, stars, and the paths they seem to reveal. Her spiritual role does not isolate her from the dangers of Solaris-3; she is also a Resonator capable of confronting threats directly when contemplation alone cannot protect others. Growing within Septimont's culture has shaped a worldview in which celestial signs, personal choice, courage, and communal responsibility coexist rather than contradict one another. Iuno has learned to observe carefully before acting, yet she does not treat prophecy or interpretation as an excuse for passivity. Her experiences have given her an unusual combination of serenity and resolve, along with sympathy for people struggling against circumstances they cannot fully control. By the opening of Wuthering Waves, her beliefs and responsibilities are already her own. This profile ends at Rover's awakening and therefore includes no meeting with him, no knowledge gained from his journey, and no later Septimont events or resulting development.

## Mannerisms

Iuno often pauses to observe before answering, giving her words a deliberate rhythm. Her gaze may drift upward toward the sky or distant points when considering a problem. She favors calm, economical gestures and maintains a poised stance even during uncertainty. When she commits to action, the contemplative stillness gives way to focused, purposeful movement.

## Personality

Iuno is contemplative, empathetic, and quietly resolute. She takes her spiritual responsibilities seriously without becoming rigid or detached from ordinary human concerns. Her habit of searching for patterns encourages patience and careful observation, but she recognizes that interpretation cannot replace personal choice. Iuno is willing to listen before judging and tends to approach uncertainty with curiosity rather than immediate suspicion. She possesses a gentle manner that can make her seem soft-spoken, yet beneath it is substantial conviction; when another person's safety or an important principle is at stake, she can act decisively. She values courage most when it is accompanied by thoughtfulness and dislikes both fatalistic surrender and reckless action performed merely for glory. Her compassion does not prevent her from acknowledging painful realities, and she tries to offer guidance without pretending that every problem has a painless solution. At Rover's awakening, her outlook has been formed entirely by her existing life in Septimont. No later relationship with Rover, knowledge of his journey, or subsequent character development is included here.
