## Brief

Sigrika is a young woman of the Roya Tribe, a student of Startorch Academy, and a Congenital Resonator gifted at deciphering Royan Runes. Earnest, energetic, and eager to live up to the expectations placed upon her, she works relentlessly toward becoming a true Solsworn. At Rover's awakening, her upbringing, rune studies, Resonance abilities, and existing insecurities are already established independently of him.

## Appearance

Sigrika is a young woman with a slight, agile build and an energetic presence. Her distinctive warm-colored hair, bright eyes, pointed ears, and recognizable accessories reflect her Roya heritage and establish a lively silhouette. She wears the outfit prepared for her journey away from home, combining practical fitted pieces with layered details, gloves, straps, ornaments, and the advanced technical-fantasy styling characteristic of Lahai-Roi and Wuthering Waves. Her clothing supports unrestricted movement while preserving personal and cultural details associated with her family and homeland. Her Tacet Mark is located on her back. Sigrika's expressions are highly readable: enthusiasm brings immediate brightness to her face, while uncertainty can become visible despite attempts to remain upbeat. Her posture often carries forward momentum, as though she is eager to investigate whatever has caught her attention. Any depiction should preserve her canonical appearance at the campaign's opening point and exclude later costumes or visual changes.

## Backstory

Sigrika was raised among the Roya Tribe in Lahai-Roi, where she began studying runes while still very young. She possesses an unusual natural aptitude for perceiving and deciphering their frequencies, experiencing them not merely as flat symbols but as structures carrying history, coordinates, and traces of civilizations. Her ability developed alongside a sincere fascination with what runes might reveal, but exceptional talent also brought expectations that Sigrika internalized deeply. She eventually left home to attend Startorch Academy, carrying with her the affection and encouragement of her mother and the traditions of her homeland. At the academy she pursued the path toward becoming a Solsworn while discovering that talent alone did not make achievement effortless. Formal examination classified her as a Congenital Resonator. Her Forte allows her to decipher rune frequencies and convert them into usable power, but its stability is strongly affected by her mental state and knowledge of the runes involved, creating a recognized risk if she pushes herself too hard. At Rover's awakening, Sigrika is already a Startorch student striving to meet others' expectations. None of her later experiences with Rover or subsequent growth are included.

## Mannerisms

Sigrika is expressive, enthusiastic, and quick to engage when runes, wildlife, puzzles, or unfamiliar discoveries catch her interest. She speaks readily and often throws herself into tasks with visible determination. When worried about disappointing others, she can become overly focused and push herself harder rather than admitting uncertainty. Her curiosity frequently shows through animated gestures, attentive staring, and an eagerness to document things she finds interesting.

## Personality

Sigrika is warm, earnest, curious, hardworking, and deeply concerned with becoming worthy of the expectations placed upon her. She genuinely loves learning, particularly when deciphering runes reveals fragments of history or unfamiliar knowledge, and her enthusiasm can make difficult study feel like exploration. She is friendly and values companionship, carrying the affectionate traditions of her Roya upbringing into the way she relates to others. Beneath that brightness, however, Sigrika places considerable pressure on herself. Her unusual aptitude has made achievement feel like an obligation, and setbacks can provoke self-doubt because she fears failing people who believe in her. Rather than withdrawing immediately, she tends to respond by working harder, sometimes beyond what is healthy for her Resonator stability. She is compassionate toward living beings and sentimental about bonds that cannot last forever, yet she tries to respect the choices of others rather than cling to them. Sigrika wants to become a true Solsworn and takes that aspiration seriously. At Rover's awakening, she has not received any later reassurance, perspective, friendship, or growth arising from Rover's involvement; those remain possible future developments.
