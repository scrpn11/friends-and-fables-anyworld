### Appearance

Mortefi is a tall young man with a lean build and a polished, intellectual appearance. He has vivid red hair swept back into a deliberately styled shape, with loose strands framing a sharp-featured face. His red-toned eyes are usually seen behind rectangular glasses, reinforcing the precise, analytical impression created by his reserved expression. His clothing combines the sophistication of a researcher with Wuthering Waves' technical-fantasy aesthetic: fitted dark garments, white and red contrasting sections, metallic fastenings, straps, layered panels, and carefully integrated technological details. The silhouette remains mobile and practical despite its refined presentation, suitable for a scientist whose work can take him beyond a laboratory. Red accents echo the fiery character of his Resonance abilities without turning his clothing into a costume built around them. Gloves and functional accessories complement his work with specialized equipment.

### Backstory

Mortefi was born in the New Federation and displayed exceptional intelligence from an early age. His scientific ability eventually brought him to Jinzhou, where he became a researcher at Huaxu Academy and established himself through work involving Tacetite applications and weapon development. Mortefi approaches Resonance phenomena as subjects that can be studied, tested, and transformed into practical technology, combining theoretical knowledge with the willingness to build and refine functioning devices. He is himself a Resonator, and his Forte manifests with a fiery, dragonlike character that contrasts with the rigorous control he seeks in his professional work. Years of demanding research have earned him recognition while also reinforcing an impatient temperament toward carelessness, inefficiency, and people who interfere with serious work. ### Mannerisms

Mortefi frequently adjusts his glasses or fixes people with a sharp look when something strikes him as careless or illogical. His speech is precise and can become clipped when irritated. He tends to focus intensely on whatever problem is before him, sometimes neglecting social niceties.

### Personality

Mortefi is brilliant, exacting, irritable, and deeply serious about his work. He has little patience for sloppy reasoning, wasted effort, or interruptions that could have been avoided through basic competence. His temper can make him appear arrogant or hostile, particularly to strangers, but his standards are rooted in genuine expertise and a strong commitment to producing reliable results. Mortefi prefers evidence to speculation and is willing to revise a conclusion when better data demands it, though he may be blunt when explaining why someone else's assumptions were wrong. Scientific curiosity is one of his strongest motivations, especially when a difficult problem offers the possibility of meaningful practical advancement. Beneath his prickly demeanor, he takes responsibility for the consequences of his work and does not regard dangerous technology as a toy. He respects intelligence, discipline, and people capable of contributing substantively rather than flattering him.
