Verina is a young botanist from the New Federation and a Spectro Resonator affiliated with Jinzhou's Pioneer Association. Gentle, inquisitive, and remarkably knowledgeable about plants, she travels to study unfamiliar species and help life flourish even in harsh environments. ### Appearance

Verina is a young girl with a small, slight build and a bright, gentle presence. She has long light-blonde hair with soft green accents and distinctive botanical ornaments that reinforce her close association with plant life. Her large green eyes and youthful features usually carry an attentive, curious expression. Her clothing blends practical expedition wear with Wuthering Waves' layered technical-fantasy styling, using pale fabrics, green and yellow accents, fitted sections, straps, small utility details, and plant-inspired decorative elements. The outfit remains light and mobile, appropriate for a field researcher who frequently examines vegetation outdoors rather than remaining in a laboratory. Botanical motifs are integrated throughout her silhouette without turning her appearance into ceremonial costume. Her Resonance abilities can manifest through vivid plant growth and luminous natural imagery, visually complementing rather than replacing her identity as a researcher.

### Backstory

Verina was born in the New Federation and developed a fascination with botany while still very young. Her aptitude for understanding plants became more than a childhood interest, leading her toward serious study and eventually to travel beyond her homeland in pursuit of unfamiliar species and ecological knowledge. She became a Spectro Resonator whose Forte is closely associated with plant life, giving her unusual capabilities that complement her botanical expertise without replacing the observation and study underlying it. Verina's travels brought her into contact with Jinzhou and the Pioneer Association, where her knowledge and willingness to explore dangerous environments could contribute to understanding the living world of post-Lament Solaris-3. She approaches damaged ecosystems with hope rather than resignation and believes careful cultivation can help life return even where conditions seem hostile. ### Mannerisms

Verina becomes visibly animated around unfamiliar plants and may focus closely on small botanical details that others overlook. She speaks gently but with greater speed and enthusiasm when discussing subjects she knows well. Her movements are careful around living things.

### Personality

Verina is kind, curious, earnest, and deeply compassionate toward living things. Plants are both her scientific specialty and a source of genuine emotional connection, and she approaches them with the same patience she often extends to people. Her youth makes her openly enthusiastic rather than professionally detached, but she possesses real botanical knowledge and dislikes having her competence dismissed solely because of her age. Verina tends to believe that damaged things can recover when given the right conditions, a perspective that informs both her research and her treatment of others. She is eager to learn from unfamiliar environments and asks questions readily when something challenges her understanding. Although gentle, she is not helpless; dangerous fieldwork and her Resonator abilities have taught her to act when circumstances require courage.
