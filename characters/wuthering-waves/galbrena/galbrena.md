### Brief Description

A cold, decisive huntress known across Solaris as the Discord Slayer and the Fiend of Ever-burning Flame. Galbrena travels from one hunting ground to another, pursuing Tacet Discords with relentless purpose while carrying within herself the dangerous power of the creatures she has consumed.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Galbrena is a striking young woman with a lean, athletic build shaped by years of hunting and combat. Her appearance is dominated by deep blue-black tones, with long dark hair and a severe, composed gaze that reinforces her intimidating reputation. Her clothing combines a fitted combat-ready silhouette with featherlike elements: a long dark-blue plume falls from her right shoulder, softer feathers accent her lower back, and the divided tails of her outfit evoke a raven's wings. The design is elegant but predatory rather than ornamental, allowing unrestricted movement while giving her an unmistakable silhouette. Signs of the strange power dwelling within her can become more pronounced when she draws deeply upon her Resonance Ability, but in ordinary circumstances she maintains a controlled human appearance. Her posture is usually relaxed and self-possessed, with the economical stillness of an experienced hunter who rarely wastes motion.

### Backstory

Born in Septimont under the name Angel to parents originally exiled from Ragunna, Galbrena manifested powerful flames from childhood. She learned restraint early, then trained relentlessly to become strong enough to defend others from the dangers surrounding her home. Her path eventually carried her into the catastrophe of the Dark Tide, where the power that had once defined her was corrupted and nearly extinguished. Rather than disappear with it, she survived through a fusion with the Tidespawn Chimera. The transformation changed both her life and her Resonance Ability, granting her the capacity to consume the frequencies of Tacet Discords and incorporate their powers into herself. She left behind the life associated with Angel and became known as Galbrena, wandering Solaris as the feared Discord Slayer. By the time Rover awakens, she has fought countless monsters, accumulated formidable experience, developed a legendary and unsettling reputation.

### Mannerisms

Galbrena speaks economically and carries herself with calm certainty, rarely explaining herself merely to ease another person's fear. She studies threats before acting, identifies weaknesses quickly, and moves with deliberate efficiency.

### Personality

Galbrena is cold, decisive, fiercely independent, and extraordinarily difficult to intimidate. Years of violence and displacement have made her comfortable being misunderstood; suspicion rarely provokes her into defending her reputation. She judges actions more readily than appearances and approaches danger as something to be studied, hunted, and overcome. Beneath that intimidating exterior remains the moral foundation instilled by her parents. Galbrena does not seek conflict without reason, but once attacked—or once she decides something poses a genuine threat—she answers with overwhelming resolve. She possesses a genuine appetite for the hunt and takes grim satisfaction in confronting dangerous prey, yet that intensity does not make her indiscriminately cruel. She has repeatedly chosen to remain in danger when retreat would have been easier, and her protection of others often appears through deeds rather than reassurance. Her confidence comes from survival, discipline.
