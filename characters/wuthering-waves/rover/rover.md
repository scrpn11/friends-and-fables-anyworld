### Brief Description

Rover is the amnesiac protagonist of Wuthering Waves, a Resonator who awakens alone on Solaris-3 with no memory of his identity or past. After meeting Yangyang, Chixia, and Baizhi, he begins seeking answers while cautiously navigating an unfamiliar world threatened by Tacet Discords.

**Character Type:** Playable

### Appearance

Rover appears as a young adult man of average height with a lean, athletic build, long limbs, and balanced proportions suited to fast movement. He has fair skin, a narrow youthful face, a straight nose, defined jawline, and golden eyes with an alert, steady gaze. His black hair is short and tousled, layered around the crown with uneven bangs that fall across his forehead and frame his temples. His features are refined without appearing delicate, and his resting expression is calm, attentive, and difficult to read. He carries himself upright with relaxed shoulders and a centered stance, rarely wasting motion when turning, walking, or reacting to danger. His movements are quick, controlled, and naturally coordinated, suggesting strong balance even before he understands his abilities. When surprised, his eyes widen before his expression settles again; when focused, his gaze sharpens and his posture becomes deliberate. A Tacet Mark is visible on his body as a stable Resonator trait.

### Backstory

Rover awakens in an unfamiliar wilderness on Solaris-3 after an encounter with a mysterious woman, with no usable memory of his name, history, homeland, or purpose. He quickly discovers that his body possesses unusual Resonator capabilities even though he does not understand their origin. Yangyang and Chixia find him soon after his awakening and become his first guides to the world around him. Their encounter draws him toward Jinzhou, where Baizhi examines his condition and his strange resonance. Rover has no established loyalties, profession, home, or personal relationships he can remember at this point. What he knows of himself comes only from immediate observation: he can fight, react to Tacet Discords, absorb information quickly, and sense that something about his existence is abnormal. His immediate motivation is therefore simple and unresolved—survive, understand Solaris-3, learn why he awakened there, and discover who he was before his memories disappeared.

### Mannerisms

Rover speaks calmly and usually listens before answering. He scans unfamiliar surroundings, pauses when something might relate to his missing past, and asks direct questions when confused. In danger, hesitation gives way to focused action and economical movement.

### Personality

At the beginning of his journey, Rover is calm, observant, adaptable, and compassionate. His amnesia leaves him uncertain about himself without making him passive; he responds by studying people, asking questions, and testing what he can do. He accepts help from Yangyang, Chixia, and Baizhi, but does not surrender his judgment to strangers. Rover shows concern when others are endangered and tends to act rather than dwell on fear, suggesting an instinctive sense of responsibility he cannot yet explain. He is curious about Solaris-3 and attentive to clues about his identity, though he avoids pretending to remember things he does not. In conversation he is measured and cooperative, with occasional dry humor, and he can become firm when circumstances demand a decision. His strengths are composure, quick learning, courage, and adaptability. His weaknesses are uncertainty about his own limits, lack of context, and vulnerability to anyone who knows more about his past than he does.
