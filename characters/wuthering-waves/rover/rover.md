### Brief Description

Rover is the amnesiac protagonist of Wuthering Waves, a multi-attribute Resonator who awakens on Solaris-3 with no memory of their past. Drawn into Jinzhou's crises and the wider struggle against the Lament, they seek the truth of their identity while forming bonds across the world.

**Character Type:** Playable

### Appearance

Rover has one of two canonical human presentations selected by the player, both appearing as a young adult of lean, athletic build with balanced proportions and an agile, combat-ready bearing. The feminine form has fair skin, long black hair gathered partly into a low ponytail with loose sections framing the face, and striking golden eyes. The masculine form has fair skin, short tousled black hair with layered bangs and the same distinctive golden eyes. Both have refined, youthful features, a straight nose, defined jawline, and a calm neutral expression that shifts readily into alert focus. Their posture is upright but relaxed, with economical movement and quick changes of balance suited to swordplay and traversal. A Tacet Mark is present on the body as a stable Resonator trait. Whether standing still or moving through danger, Rover carries a composed, observant presence, rarely appearing physically imposing despite their unusual capability.

### Backstory

Rover awakens in an unfamiliar wilderness on Solaris-3 after contact with a mysterious entity, possessing no reliable memory of who they were or why they were sent there. They are discovered by Yangyang, Chixia, and Baizhi and soon become involved in Jinzhou's defense against Tacet Discords and the gathering threat surrounding the Threnodian known as Ovathrax. As their journey expands beyond Huanglong, fragments of an older identity emerge. Rover learns that their former self possessed extraordinary authority, maintained deep ties to the Black Shores, and deliberately erased much of their own recorded history before the present awakening. They once served as the Black Shores' Chief Steward and pursued a long-term answer to the Lament, accepting personal loss as part of that search. In the present, Rover continues forward without surrendering to the expectations attached to that past, seeking truth through direct experience while repeatedly becoming central to crises across Solaris-3.

### Mannerisms

Rover speaks calmly and tends to listen before answering. They scan unfamiliar surroundings, pause when new information connects to their lost past, and answer danger with focused economy. Dry humor appears occasionally, while concern is usually expressed through immediate action.

### Personality

Rover is calm, adaptable, compassionate, and determined. Memory loss leaves them curious rather than helpless; they approach unfamiliar people and places with caution but remain willing to engage, learn, and help. They value human life, cooperation, and the possibility that civilization can survive the Lament through collective effort rather than dependence on a chosen few. Rover rarely seeks authority, yet others often defer to them because they remain composed under pressure and make decisive choices when circumstances deteriorate. They are capable of warmth and loyalty, forming bonds across nations without demanding that others fit one moral or cultural standard. Their patience has limits: deliberate cruelty, exploitation, and threats to innocent people can provoke a cold, direct response. Rover's strengths are resolve, empathy, adaptability, and strategic instinct; their flaws include guardedness, carrying burdens alone, and accepting personal sacrifice when pursuing a larger goal.
