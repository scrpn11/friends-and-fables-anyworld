### Brief Description

Rover is the amnesiac male protagonist of Wuthering Waves, awakening alone on Solaris-3 with no memory of his identity, history, or purpose. At the game's opening moment, he has no established relationships or known affiliations and is defined only by his unexplained presence and latent Resonator nature.

**Character Type:** Playable

### Appearance

Rover appears as a young adult man of average height with a lean, athletic build, long limbs, and balanced proportions suited to quick movement. He has fair skin, a narrow youthful face, a straight nose, defined jawline, and striking golden eyes with an alert, steady gaze. His black hair is short and tousled, layered around the crown with uneven bangs falling across his forehead and framing his temples. His features are refined without seeming delicate, and his resting expression is calm, watchful, and difficult to read. He carries himself upright with relaxed shoulders and a centered stance, rarely wasting motion when turning, walking, or reacting. His movements are controlled, coordinated, and economical, suggesting strong balance even before he understands what his body can do. When surprised, his eyes widen briefly before settling; when focused, his gaze sharpens and his posture becomes deliberate. A Tacet Mark is visible on his body as a stable Resonator trait.

### Backstory

At the opening of Wuthering Waves, Rover has just awakened on Solaris-3 after contact with a mysterious woman and possesses no usable memory of his name, origins, history, relationships, loyalties, profession, or purpose. Nothing about his earlier life is known to him, and no later revelation about his identity belongs in this starting snapshot. He has not yet formed bonds, joined any group, reached a settlement, or learned how the world understands Resonators, Tacet Discords, or the Lament. His circumstances are immediate: he is conscious in an unfamiliar place, his memories are absent, his body bears a Tacet Mark, and he appears able to respond instinctively to danger despite lacking context for that ability. He has no confirmed goals beyond orienting himself, surviving, determining where he is, and discovering what happened to his memory. Every relationship, allegiance, reputation, and recovered piece of personal history must develop during play rather than be assumed in advance.

### Mannerisms

Rover speaks calmly, pauses to assess unfamiliar situations, and looks around before committing to a response. His movements become precise when danger appears. Confusion shows more as focused attention and brief hesitation than panic, and he tends to ask direct questions when context is missing.

### Personality

At the game's opening moment, Rover is calm, observant, cautious, and adaptable, but much of his personality remains undefined because his memories are gone and he has only just awakened. He does not begin with established loyalties, relationships, ambitions, political views, or assumptions about Solaris-3. What is immediately visible is composure under uncertainty, a willingness to examine unfamiliar circumstances, and enough self-control to act without panicking. He is curious about his surroundings and his state, but he does not pretend to know more than he does. In conversation he is measured and direct, asking for context rather than filling gaps with certainty. When threatened, he responds decisively and relies on instinct before understanding why those instincts exist. His strengths are composure, attention, adaptability, and courage under pressure. His weaknesses are lack of context, uncertainty about his capabilities, and vulnerability to claims about a past he cannot verify.
