### Brief Description

A conscientious, level-headed Resonator from Ashinohara and a first-year student of Startorch Academy. Chisa approaches the world as a collection of structures that can be observed, separated, and understood. Quiet and analytical, she finds mechanical problems easier to resolve than the fragile, complicated bonds between people.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Chisa is a pale young woman with a slender build, long glossy black hair falling nearly to her waist, and narrow pale-red eyes distinguished by unusual white pupils. A small beauty mark sits beneath the outer corner of her right eye, while a thin red ribbon decorates the hair near her right temple. Her Tacet Mark is located on her upper right arm. She wears an older black Startorch Academy sailor-style uniform with a white-trimmed collar and red neck scarf, layered beneath a loose black jacket whose fabric fades toward light gray. Dark mid-calf socks and polished black heeled penny loafers complete the restrained school uniform. Around her neck is a black Resonator suppression collar, monitoring equipment fitted because of the instability associated with her Forte. Her severe dark palette, straight posture, and observant gaze give her an austere first impression that contrasts with the sensitivity beneath her reserve.

### Backstory

Chisa was born in Ashinohara and could perceive strange threads connecting people from childhood. As she grew older, distance within her family and hostility from classmates made human relationships seem increasingly fragile and difficult to understand. At fifteen, sustained bullying culminated in her awakening as a Mutant Resonator, manifesting the Forte later designated Eye of Unraveling. Difficulty controlling it led authorities to fit her with a Resonator suppression collar. Chisa eventually received acceptance to the distant Startorch Academy in Lahai-Roi, where Resonators could study without the prejudice she had known at home. With her family's blessing she left Ashinohara and entered the Academy as a first-year student, pursuing its demanding scientific and technical curriculum. Before the present era began, a Void Storm engulfed Honami City and caught Chisa within its anomalous Sonoro Sphere. Time no longer passed normally around her.

### Mannerisms

Chisa speaks calmly and precisely, often pausing to observe before responding. Her eyes narrow when analyzing structures or relationships, as though tracing invisible lines. She favors practical conclusions, maintains controlled posture, and rarely wastes motion.

### Personality

Chisa is conscientious, level-headed, observant, and intensely analytical. She instinctively breaks complicated situations into smaller structures, trusting careful examination and logical action more readily than intuition. This makes her composed under pressure and unusually capable of identifying the point where a seemingly impossible problem can be separated or resolved. Human relationships are the exception. Chisa understands that bonds can break, yet precisely because they are fragile she considers them deeply valuable. Earlier rejection and isolation left her guarded and accustomed to handling difficulties herself, but not indifferent to others. She notices vulnerability, remembers kindness, and can act with quiet consideration even when she struggles to express attachment naturally. Her reserved demeanor may appear distant, though beneath it is someone sensitive to connection and loss. She values independence, precision, sincerity.
