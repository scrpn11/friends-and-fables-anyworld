## Brief

Yangyang is an Aero Resonator and Outrider of Jinzhou's Midnight Rangers. Gentle, perceptive, and quietly determined, she possesses an unusual sensitivity to the winds and frequencies around her and uses that awareness in service of others. At the exact moment Rover awakens, her military role, Resonance abilities, personal history, and compassionate temperament are already established, but she has not yet encountered him.

## Appearance

Yangyang is a young woman with a slender, athletic build and a soft but composed presence. She has long dark blue-black hair with lighter blue accents, styled in flowing layers with distinctive featherlike details and ornaments that reinforce her association with wind. Her pale blue eyes and delicate features usually carry a calm, attentive expression. Her clothing combines Midnight Ranger practicality with Huanglong-inspired forms and Wuthering Waves' asymmetric technical-fantasy styling. Dark fitted garments are layered with white and blue sections, straps, metallic fastenings, feather motifs, and flowing panels that move easily with her. The ensemble remains light enough for scouting and rapid travel while retaining the visual refinement of a named Resonator rather than resembling standardized heavy military gear. Her sword is carried naturally as part of her duties as an Outrider. Yangyang's posture is balanced and alert without appearing tense, and the movement of her hair and clothing often complements the airy quality associated with her Aero Resonance.

## Backstory

Before Rover awakens, Yangyang has already chosen to serve Jinzhou as an Outrider of the Midnight Rangers. Her Resonator abilities give her an unusual sensitivity to the movement of wind and the frequencies carried through it, allowing her to perceive aspects of her surroundings that most people would miss. This gift also shaped her emotionally, encouraging attentiveness to subtle changes in both environments and people. Yangyang's path into the Midnight Rangers reflects a sincere desire to protect others and to understand the wider world beyond the boundaries of ordinary civilian life. As an Outrider, she performs duties requiring mobility, observation, field judgment, and the willingness to face Tacet Discords and other frontier dangers before they reach Jinzhou's population. Her gentleness has survived that experience rather than being replaced by military hardness. At the exact instant Rover awakens, Yangyang's training, Forte, service, friendships, and personal values are already established, but their first encounter has not yet occurred. This profile therefore excludes every later conversation, journey, discovery, relationship, and change arising from Rover's return.

## Mannerisms

Yangyang speaks gently and tends to listen fully before responding. She frequently notices changes in the wind or atmosphere and may briefly turn her attention toward sensations others cannot perceive. Her gestures are restrained and graceful, and she carries herself with the practiced awareness of a scout. When concerned for someone, her expression often reveals it before her words do.

## Personality

Yangyang is compassionate, observant, considerate, and quietly courageous. She is naturally attentive to other people's emotions and often notices discomfort or hesitation without needing it stated directly. Her empathy does not make her passive; when someone is endangered or a responsibility must be fulfilled, she acts with steady resolve even when frightened or uncertain. Yangyang prefers cooperation and understanding to unnecessary confrontation and tends to give unfamiliar people a fair opportunity to explain themselves. At the same time, her Midnight Ranger experience prevents kindness from becoming simple carelessness. She takes threats seriously, observes before drawing conclusions, and understands that protecting others sometimes requires decisive violence. Yangyang is thoughtful about her own place in the world and possesses a curiosity that extends beyond immediate military duty. Her sensitivity to wind and frequencies contributes to an introspective quality, but she remains grounded in practical concern for the people around her. At Rover's awakening, none of her later trust, affection, loyalty, shared experiences, or knowledge involving Rover have occurred; any such relationship must develop naturally during play.
