### Brief Description

Qiuyuan is a wandering swordsman and Resonator from Huanglong whose disciplined swordsmanship is matched by a quiet, independent temperament. He travels according to his own principles rather than seeking rank or recognition, carrying the habits and perspective of an experienced martial wanderer. At Rover's awakening, his skills, history.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Qiuyuan is a young man with a lean, athletic build shaped by extensive travel and martial training. His canonical appearance follows Huanglong's fusion of traditional martial aesthetics and Wuthering Waves' technical-fantasy design language. His distinctive hair, sharp facial features, and composed gaze give him the reserved presence of an experienced swordsman. Layered garments combine fitted dark sections with lighter fabric, restrained contrasting accents, asymmetric panels, belts, metallic fittings, and ornamental details influenced by Huanglong culture. The ensemble remains practical enough for long journeys and unrestricted sword movement rather than resembling heavy armor or formal military uniform. His weapon is carried as naturally as any other part of his traveling equipment, reflecting familiarity rather than display. Decorative details support his individual identity without obscuring the clean silhouette of a wandering martial artist.

### Backstory

Before Rover awakens, Qiuyuan has already spent years following the path of a wandering swordsman through Huanglong and beyond the safety of settled communities. His life is defined less by formal office than by movement, personal discipline, and the continual refinement of martial skill. Becoming a Resonator added unusual capabilities to a foundation built through deliberate sword training rather than replacing it. Travel exposed him to the hazards of post-Lament Solaris-3, including Tacet Discords and the ordinary human conflicts that persist alongside them, giving him practical experience that cannot be learned through secluded practice alone. Qiuyuan developed a personal code shaped by judgment and responsibility rather than unquestioning allegiance to an institution. He does not require public recognition to validate his ability and is comfortable allowing deeds to speak for him. By the opening of Wuthering Waves, his reputation, experience, Resonance abilities.

### Personality

Qiuyuan is reserved, disciplined, perceptive, and strongly independent. He values mastery earned through persistent practice and has little interest in boasting about his ability. His confidence is quiet because he sees skill as something demonstrated through action rather than reputation. Qiuyuan prefers judging circumstances for himself and is reluctant to surrender personal responsibility simply because an authority has already reached a conclusion. This independence does not make him antisocial; he can cooperate readily with people whose conduct earns his respect, but he does not manufacture familiarity where none exists. Years of travel have made him pragmatic about danger and human weakness without stripping away his principles. He tends to listen carefully, speak concisely, and avoid unnecessary conflict, though he acts decisively when violence becomes unavoidable. Worthy skill interests him, especially when it reveals discipline or an unfamiliar approach he can learn from.
