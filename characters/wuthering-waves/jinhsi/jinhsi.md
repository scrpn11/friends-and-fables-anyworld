## Brief

Jinhsi is the young Magistrate of Jinzhou, entrusted with governing the frontier city beneath the guidance of its Sentinel, Jue. Calm, conscientious, and exceptionally dutiful, she places Jinzhou's welfare above personal comfort and carries enormous responsibility with quiet resolve. At Rover's awakening, her office, Resonance abilities.

## Appearance

Jinhsi is a young woman with a slender, graceful build and an elegant bearing befitting Jinzhou's Magistrate. She has very long pale hair, predominantly white with subtle cool-toned accents, arranged in flowing layers that fall well below her waist. Her light-colored eyes and delicate features usually carry a composed, attentive expression. Her elaborate clothing combines white and pale fabrics with dark contrasting sections, gold ornamentation, and luminous teal-blue details, blending traditional Huanglong-inspired forms with the refined technical-fantasy aesthetic of Wuthering Waves. Layered panels, intricate fastenings, ornamental motifs, and asymmetrical elements give the outfit ceremonial authority while preserving mobility. Dragonlike and celestial visual cues subtly echo her connection to Jue without turning her attire into armor. Fine accessories and metallic details reinforce her status while remaining integrated into the overall silhouette.

## Backstory

Jinhsi's life was marked by extraordinary circumstances long before Rover's awakening. As an infant she was connected to the Sentinel Jue and survived events that permanently intertwined her destiny with Jinzhou and its guardian. She grew into a Resonator and eventually assumed the office of Magistrate while still unusually young for such responsibility. Under Changli's mentorship and through her own study, Jinhsi developed the political judgment, discipline, and strategic patience necessary to govern a frontier city continually threatened by Tacet Discords and the lingering consequences of the Lament. Her position requires balancing military defense, civil administration, public morale, and the expectations created by Jinzhou's relationship with its Sentinel. Jinhsi accepts these burdens as obligations rather than privileges and has already earned the trust associated with her office by the opening of the game. This profile stops at the precise moment Rover awakens.

## Mannerisms

Jinhsi speaks with measured courtesy and rarely wastes words, especially while handling official matters. She maintains an upright posture and controlled gestures even under pressure. Her attention is focused and patient, often allowing others to finish before responding.

## Personality

Jinhsi is composed, intelligent, compassionate, and deeply responsible. She understands authority primarily as an obligation to protect and serve Jinzhou, not as a source of personal prestige. Her youth does not make her impulsive; years of preparation and the weight of office have encouraged patience, careful judgment, and an ability to remain functional under intense pressure. She listens seriously to advice while retaining the independence necessary to make difficult decisions herself. Jinhsi is courteous even when disagreeing, but her gentleness should not be confused with indecision. Once convinced that a course of action is necessary for Jinzhou's welfare, she can be remarkably firm and willing to accept personal risk. She tends to place public responsibilities before her own comfort and may carry burdens privately rather than impose them on others. Hope is important to her, but it is a deliberate hope grounded in preparation and action rather than naïve optimism.
