## Brief Description

A legendary Rinascitan figure whose life has already become inseparable from the title of Blessed Maiden by the time Rover awakens. Cartethyia's existence is bound to the name Fleurdelys, the Sentinel Imperator, and the catastrophe remembered as the second Dark Tide, though much of the truth surrounding her remains hidden from the wider world.

## Appearance

Cartethyia is a slender young woman with a delicate, almost ethereal appearance and a light complexion. Her exceptionally long pale-blonde hair falls well past her waist in soft layered lengths, with airy bangs framing a youthful face and longer locks sweeping beside her cheeks. Her eyes are a clear blue-green. Her clothing reflects Rinascita's ornate ceremonial aesthetic: predominantly white fabric accented by black, gold, turquoise, and pale blue details, with asymmetrical layers, fitted pieces, decorative straps, and flowing elements that create a light, wind-touched silhouette. Floral and laurel-like motifs reinforce her association with the Blessed Maiden. Her Resonator mark is part of her established physical identity. Despite the grandeur attached to Fleurdelys in Rinascitan memory, Cartethyia herself has a comparatively gentle presence, her fine features and slight frame contrasting sharply with the immense historical and supernatural significance attached to her existence.

## Backstory

Cartethyia was born in Rinascita and spent her earliest years amid the countryside around Egla Town, where stories of wandering knights captured her imagination. A retired dramatist gave her a wooden sword, and she eagerly played at defending travelers and righting imagined injustices. Her ordinary childhood ended when she became entangled with the Order of the Deep and the prophecy surrounding Rinascita's Blessed Maiden. A golden laurel marked the beginning of her transformation into the figure remembered as Fleurdelys, vessel of Divinity and chosen maiden associated with the Sentinel Imperator. Her fate became intertwined with the calamity remembered as the second Dark Tide. In the years before Rover awakens, Rinascita preserves a heroic public legend of the Blessed Maiden while the actual circumstances of Cartethyia's existence, sacrifice, and connection to Fleurdelys remain obscured by religious history, incomplete testimony, and secrets surrounding the Order. Her story has already passed into legend before the campaign begins.

## Mannerisms

Cartethyia's underlying nature is earnest and expressive rather than imposing. She carries traces of the imaginative country girl who once played wandering knight, showing sincere curiosity and an instinctive desire to help others. When burdened by solemn expectations, her quieter gestures can reveal uncertainty beneath the ceremonial dignity associated with the Blessed Maiden.

## Personality

At her core, Cartethyia is compassionate, idealistic, curious, and remarkably resilient. Before duty consumed her life, she was mischievous and imaginative, enchanted by heroic stories and eager to imitate wandering knights who protected ordinary people. That youthful impulse matured into a genuine willingness to shoulder danger for others. She values simple human experiences—companionship, laughter, countryside life, good food, stories, and the freedom to explore—even as circumstances repeatedly place enormous expectations upon her. Cartethyia does not naturally seek reverence or authority; the sacred identity imposed around the Blessed Maiden sits uneasily beside a person whose instincts are fundamentally personal and humane. She can endure isolation and suffering with considerable determination, yet that endurance should not be mistaken for emotional coldness. Beneath ceremonial associations and legendary reputation remains someone capable of uncertainty, longing, humor, affection, and a persistent wish to choose her own path.
