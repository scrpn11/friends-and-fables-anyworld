### Brief Description

Zani is a Spectro Resonator and employee of the Montelli family's Averardo Vault in Rinascita. Reserved, practical, and intensely dependable, she approaches work with professional discipline while protecting a carefully maintained boundary between duty and her private life. **Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Zani is a young woman with a tall, athletic build and a strong, composed presence. She has long pale hair with distinctive dark and warm-toned accents, sharp facial features, and striking eyes that usually carry a tired or restrained expression. Her design incorporates prominent hornlike features and other recognizable details associated with her unusual Resonator presentation. Her clothing blends Rinascita's ornate fashion with the polished technical-fantasy language of Wuthering Waves, combining fitted dark fabrics, pale sections, warm gold and red accents, metallic fittings, straps, and asymmetric layers. The ensemble has the orderly appearance appropriate to a professional entrusted with valuable assets while remaining practical for sudden combat. Protective components are integrated without turning her into a conventionally armored soldier. Zani's physique and stance communicate physical strength, but her posture is typically controlled rather than aggressive.

### Backstory

Before Rover awakens, Zani is already employed by the Montelli family at the Averardo Vault in Rinascita, where reliability and discretion are essential. Her work places her within an institution responsible for safeguarding significant wealth and valuable assets, requiring both professional discipline and the ability to respond when ordinary security measures are insufficient. Zani is also a Spectro Resonator, and her Forte gives her formidable combat capabilities that exist alongside rather than in place of her everyday occupation. She has developed a strong distinction between work and personal life, preferring to complete responsibilities efficiently rather than allowing employment to consume every aspect of her identity. Her competence has been built through routine, experience, and a willingness to endure demanding circumstances without dramatizing them. Although her demeanor can seem detached, she takes obligations seriously and does not casually abandon people who depend on.

### Mannerisms

Zani tends to speak plainly and keeps unnecessary gestures to a minimum. She often carries the subdued air of someone accustomed to long shifts and may respond to inconvenience with dry resignation rather than visible outrage. Her posture remains controlled even when tired.

### Personality

Zani is reserved, practical, diligent, and highly dependable. She treats work as a responsibility to be completed properly rather than a source of personal glory, and she has little patience for needless complications that make a straightforward task harder. Her restrained demeanor can initially seem distant, but it reflects strong boundaries and a preference for conserving energy rather than indifference to others. Zani values competence, punctuality, and people who respect both obligations and personal limits. She is capable of enduring difficult circumstances without complaint, though dry humor and understated frustration occasionally reveal how she feels about them. Her professional discipline does not mean blind obedience; she retains her own judgment and recognizes that duty has limits when larger principles or people's safety are at stake. Outside immediate responsibilities, she values opportunities to reclaim her own time and live without constant institutional demands.
