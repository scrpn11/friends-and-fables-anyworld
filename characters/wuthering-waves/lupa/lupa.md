## Brief

Lupa is a renowned gladiator of Septimont, a fierce Resonator whose enthusiasm for competition and battle has made her a celebrated figure in the city's arena culture. Bold, instinctive, and intensely energetic, she pursues worthy challenges with wholehearted passion. At Rover's awakening, her reputation, abilities.

## Appearance

Lupa is a young woman with a powerful, athletic build reflecting the physical demands of Septimont's gladiatorial culture. She has long pale hair with vivid warm accents and distinctive animal-like styling cues that reinforce her fierce, lupine visual motif. Her sharp eyes, expressive features, and confident grin give her an immediately energetic presence. Her outfit blends the elaborate technical-fantasy styling of Wuthering Waves with Septimont's arena-inspired aesthetic: fitted dark materials, pale contrasting sections, bright red and orange accents, metallic ornaments, straps, asymmetric panels, and protective elements arranged to preserve mobility. Rather than enclosing her in heavy armor, the design emphasizes speed, strength, and freedom of movement appropriate to an experienced arena fighter. Decorative motifs and accessories reinforce her individual identity and local culture without obscuring her athletic silhouette. Lupa carries herself with loose confidence.

## Backstory

Long before Rover awakens, Lupa has already established herself within Septimont's fiercely competitive arena culture. Her life as a Resonator and gladiator has been shaped by repeated contests in which skill, instinct, courage, and the ability to perform under pressure determine both survival and reputation. Rather than viewing competition solely as violence, Lupa embraces it as a way to test herself and understand the strength of others. Her successes have earned public recognition, but acclaim has not dulled her appetite for difficult opponents or reduced combat to routine spectacle. She values the immediacy of a genuine challenge and has developed the confidence to trust her body, instincts, and Resonance abilities when situations become chaotic. Septimont's traditions and expectations form the social environment in which her reputation has grown, while her individual passion distinguishes her even among other fighters. ## Mannerisms

Lupa is physically expressive and rarely hides her excitement when something interests her. She tends to lean toward challenges rather than away from them, grins readily when competition becomes serious, and uses broad, confident gestures. Her attention sharpens visibly around capable fighters.

## Personality

Lupa is bold, passionate, competitive, and remarkably direct. She loves testing herself against difficult challenges and respects people who commit themselves fully rather than relying on empty reputation. Competition excites her because it reveals what people can actually do when pushed, not because she enjoys cruelty. She is comfortable trusting instinct and momentum, but years of arena experience mean those instincts are backed by genuine skill rather than simple recklessness. Lupa tends to express approval, frustration, amusement, and curiosity openly, making her easier to read than more guarded Resonators. She dislikes excessive hesitation and responds enthusiastically to people willing to meet difficult circumstances head-on. Her confidence can become provocative, especially when she senses someone is holding back, but she values a worthy opponent even when that person defeats or surprises her.
