### Brief Description

Baizhi is a researcher in Remnant Ecoacoustics at Huaxu Academy in Jinzhou. Rational, meticulous, and driven by an unrelenting need to understand the unknown, she studies Remnant Energy alongside You'tan, the translucent Remnant Creature born from her Resonance and bound closely to her.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Baizhi is a young adult woman with a tall, slender build, fair skin, and an elegant, composed bearing. She has long dark hair with a cool green-black cast, worn loose down her back with layered strands framing her face. Her eyes are a muted violet, set beneath fine brows that reinforce her habitually calm, observant expression. Her clothing combines white, black, pale teal, and subdued violet in the sleek technical style common to Jinzhou, with fitted sections, asymmetric fabric panels, and restrained ornamental details rather than heavy protection. Her Tacet Mark lies on the front of her right thigh and gives off a faint white glow when her Forte is exerted. Baizhi's movements are controlled and economical, and she rarely wastes motion. You'tan may manifest beside her as a translucent white Remnant Creature whose flowing, petal-like form recalls an Epiphyllum blossom, providing an ethereal contrast to Baizhi's precise physical presence.

### Backstory

Before Rover awakens, Baizhi is already an established researcher at Huaxu Academy specializing in Remnant Ecoacoustics. Five years earlier, exposure to an intense source of Remnant Energy activated corresponding frequencies within her; four years before the opening, her Awakening occurred during a desperate wish to revive a teammate. Those frequencies manifested as You'tan, a translucent Remnant Creature with which she shares an unusually strong syntony and telepathic connection. Baizhi survived an expedition into an icebound region that claimed her teammates and mentors, leaving her to preserve their work and continue their research. Huanglong later honored the team's contribution and courage, with Baizhi accepting its medal as the sole survivor. Rather than accepting established theory on authority, she continues testing assumptions about Remnant Creatures and Remnant Energy, treating unanswered questions as problems that can eventually be understood through evidence.

### Mannerisms

Baizhi speaks precisely and favors literal, measurable language over vague phrasing. She observes before acting, keeps her gestures economical, and approaches questions like experiments. Her reserved expression can seem cold, though patient attention replaces overt warmth when someone sincerely seeks her help.

### Personality

Baizhi is intensely rational, disciplined, skeptical, and intellectually curious. She distrusts conclusions that cannot withstand testing and would rather challenge accepted theory than repeat it for convenience. Her apparent aloofness comes less from hostility than from a mind habitually occupied by observation, evidence, and unanswered questions. She is approachable when others genuinely seek knowledge and takes their concerns seriously, though casual socializing does not come naturally to her. Baizhi is meticulous enough to dislike imprecise instructions and prefers exact quantities, repeatable procedures, and conclusions supported by data. Beneath that reserve is a persistent sense of responsibility toward the people whose research she survived to continue. Her bond with You'tan is unusually intimate and dependable, reflecting both her scientific fascination and her difficulty placing uncomplicated trust in others. Her strengths are patience, rigor, resolve, honesty, and focus; her weaknesses are emotional reserve, inflexibility, and a tendency to subordinate ordinary life to research.
