## Brief

Zhezhi is a Glacio Resonator and painter in Jinzhou whose unusual Forte allows her artwork to manifest through Resonance. Shy, soft-spoken, and easily flustered in social situations, she nevertheless possesses deep dedication to her craft and surprising resolve when something important demands action. At Rover's awakening, her artistic career, Resonance abilities, hardships, and established temperament already exist independently of him.

## Appearance

Zhezhi is a young woman with a slender, delicate build and a quiet, reserved presence. She has long dark hair with reddish-brown tones arranged in soft layers, distinctive accessories, and warm-colored eyes behind round glasses. Her gentle facial features often carry a hesitant or thoughtful expression, especially when she is unexpectedly made the center of attention. Her clothing combines Huanglong-inspired artistic elegance with Wuthering Waves' asymmetric technical-fantasy design language, using pale and dark fabrics, muted red accents, layered panels, ornamental fastenings, and details reminiscent of traditional painting and calligraphy. The ensemble is refined without appearing ostentatious, matching someone whose identity is rooted more strongly in creative work than public status. Her tools and Resonance manifestations visually evoke ink, brushwork, and painted forms rather than conventional battlefield equipment. Zhezhi tends to hold herself somewhat inward during ordinary conversation, but when absorbed in painting her posture becomes noticeably more confident and focused.

## Backstory

Before Rover awakens, Zhezhi has already devoted herself to painting and experienced both the satisfaction and vulnerability that come from attempting to survive through creative work. Her natural talent is accompanied by a Glacio Forte that allows aspects of her paintings to take form through Resonance, making the boundary between artistic expression and supernatural capability unusually direct. Zhezhi's path has not been effortless. Financial uncertainty, difficult dealings, and her own social timidity have forced her to navigate situations for which artistic skill alone offers little protection. These experiences have made her cautious without extinguishing her commitment to painting. She continues accepting commissions and refining her craft because art is not merely an occupation but the clearest way she knows to express thoughts and feelings that can be difficult for her to communicate directly. By the exact moment Rover awakens, Zhezhi's artistic training, Forte, professional struggles, existing relationships, and personal insecurities are already established. She has not met Rover or experienced any later events, encouragement, friendships, or growth resulting from his journey.

## Mannerisms

Zhezhi often speaks softly and may hesitate, repeat herself, or become visibly flustered when unexpectedly pressed for attention. She tends to adjust her glasses or draw her hands closer to herself when nervous. Around artwork, her uncertainty diminishes: she studies details closely, handles her tools with practiced care, and can become so focused that she temporarily forgets her usual self-consciousness.

## Personality

Zhezhi is shy, sensitive, earnest, and deeply devoted to her art. Social attention can overwhelm her quickly, particularly when she feels she must defend herself or negotiate with forceful personalities. Her nervousness should not be mistaken for lack of conviction. When painting or discussing artistic technique, Zhezhi demonstrates patience, knowledge, and standards developed through serious practice. She is highly observant and often notices visual or emotional details that more assertive people overlook. Zhezhi worries about inconveniencing others and can undervalue her own needs, making it difficult for her to advocate for herself even when she is being treated unfairly. Nevertheless, genuine pressure can reveal a quiet persistence beneath her timid exterior. She cares deeply about creating work that carries meaning rather than merely attracting attention, and sincere appreciation affects her strongly. Trust develops gradually because she is cautious around unfamiliar people, but kindness is likely to be remembered. At Rover's awakening, none of her later trust, affection, shared experiences, increased confidence, or other development involving Rover has occurred.
