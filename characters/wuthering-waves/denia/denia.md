### Brief Description

Denia is a Department of Voidmatters student at Startorch Academy who hides extraordinary perception behind the reputation of a cheerful professional slacker. Habitually smiling, joking, and dozing through lectures, she quietly studies the people around her while concealing a far darker origin and purpose than her classmates realize.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Denia is a young woman whose relaxed presentation suits the deliberately unthreatening persona she maintains at Startorch Academy. She has long dark hair with pale contrasting sections and striking eyes, her features usually arranged into the mild, practiced smile for which she is known among other students. Her academy clothing combines a predominantly dark palette with pale fabric, metallic detailing, and futuristic accessories characteristic of Lahai-Roi's advanced academic culture. The outfit is fitted and asymmetrical without appearing formal, reinforcing the impression of a student who would rather lounge through a lecture than stand at attention. Her overall silhouette is sleek and youthful, with technological details integrated naturally into her clothing rather than worn as conventional armor. Even when outwardly idle, her gaze can become unusually attentive, betraying the careful observer beneath her sleepy manner.

### Backstory

Denia's life began under the control of the Fractsidus, where she was treated less as a child than as an asset shaped for the Grand Architect's designs. The name Denia was one she chose from a fragmentary memory of farewell, while uncertainty about her own origins remained deliberately unresolved. She learned observation, imitation, masking, and deception while growing up among people who expected her to serve a predetermined purpose. Selected for unusual compatibility with Aleph-1, she was exposed to Voidmatter and became capable of manipulating it, an experience that linked her existence to the emptiness the Fractsidus wished her to embody. Yet prolonged imitation of ordinary human life gradually became something more genuine. Before Rover awakens, Denia is living as a Department of Voidmatters student at Startorch Academy and a Synchronist candidate. She cultivates the image of a harmless slacker, attends campus activities, naps through classes.

### Mannerisms

Denia wears a gentle smile almost by reflex and frequently answers serious situations with dry jokes or casual wit. She is notorious for sleeping through lectures yet somehow notices details others miss. She watches people quietly, sometimes tilting her head before responding.

### Personality

Denia presents herself as easygoing, witty, sociable, and spectacularly unmotivated. She has perfected the role of the professional slacker: sleeping through lectures, avoiding unnecessary effort, making light of troublesome situations, and greeting others with a reliably gentle smile. Much of that behavior began as deliberate imitation. Raised to observe and reproduce human responses, she became exceptionally perceptive about expressions, motives, emotional distance, and the small habits through which people reveal themselves. Her humor and apparent laziness therefore conceal a calculating intelligence and persistent awareness of her surroundings. Yet the distinction between performance and sincerity has already begun to erode by the time Rover awakens. Denia genuinely enjoys seeing other people happy and has developed attachments that conflict with the emptiness expected of her. She remains guarded about herself, avoids exposing painful questions about her identity.
