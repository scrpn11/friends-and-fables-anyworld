## Brief

Taoqi is a Havoc Resonator and director responsible for border defense within Jinzhou's Ministry of Development. Easygoing in everyday life but exceptionally dependable when duty calls, she believes good preparation and efficient work are more valuable than needless urgency. At Rover's awakening, her career, Resonance abilities, defensive responsibilities, and established temperament already exist independently of him.

## Appearance

Taoqi is a young woman with a curvy, sturdy build and a relaxed, self-assured presence. She has long pink hair arranged in soft flowing layers, with distinctive darker accents and accessories, and warm reddish eyes set in gentle features that often carry a sleepy or unhurried expression. Her clothing blends Jinzhou's Huanglong-inspired aesthetics with the layered technical-fantasy styling of Wuthering Waves. Dark fitted garments are combined with pale and pink sections, metallic ornamentation, belts, asymmetric panels, and protective details suited to someone involved in city defense. The design preserves mobility while giving her a more substantial silhouette than many lighter combatants, complementing her defensive fighting style without encasing her in conventional heavy armor. Her broadblade is an important part of her combat presentation and contrasts deliberately with her otherwise relaxed demeanor. Taoqi usually stands without unnecessary tension, often appearing comfortable even while carrying substantial equipment. When circumstances become serious, that casual posture gives way to grounded, controlled readiness rather than frantic movement.

## Backstory

Before Rover awakens, Taoqi is already an established member of Jinzhou's Ministry of Development with responsibility for matters related to border defense. Her work connects the practical growth of the city with the necessity of protecting its infrastructure and inhabitants from the persistent dangers surrounding the frontier. Although her unhurried manner can initially give the impression that she lacks ambition, Taoqi has built a reputation for completing important work reliably and for understanding that sustainable defense depends on preparation rather than constant visible urgency. As a Resonator, she possesses Havoc abilities that complement a combat style centered on resilience, protection, and powerful counteraction. Her professional life requires cooperation with both administrative and defensive institutions, giving her a practical understanding of how Jinzhou's security depends upon planning beyond the battlefield itself. By the game's opening, Taoqi's responsibilities, competence, Forte, and personal philosophy toward work are already established. This profile stops at Rover's awakening, excluding her later meeting with him, opinions formed through his actions, shared events, and any subsequent development.

## Mannerisms

Taoqi moves at an unhurried pace whenever circumstances allow and rarely appears pressured merely because others are rushing. She may look sleepy or relaxed while listening, but she pays more attention than her demeanor suggests. Her speech is calm and practical. When genuine danger appears, her posture becomes grounded and purposeful immediately, revealing the competence beneath her casual habits.

## Personality

Taoqi is relaxed, practical, dependable, and quietly hardworking. She dislikes unnecessary stress and sees little value in making a task look difficult when careful organization can make it manageable. Her tendency to conserve effort should not be mistaken for laziness: when something genuinely matters, especially the safety of Jinzhou and its people, she accepts responsibility without hesitation and follows through reliably. Taoqi is patient with others and generally prefers calm conversation to confrontation, but she can become firm when someone threatens work or people under her protection. She values sensible planning, adequate rest, and sustainable effort rather than glorifying exhaustion for its own sake. Her confidence is understated because she does not feel compelled to advertise competence before demonstrating it. In combat, the contrast between her easygoing personality and defensive resolve becomes especially clear; she is willing to stand her ground when others depend upon her.
