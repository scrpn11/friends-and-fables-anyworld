# Encore

## Brief Description

Encore is a lively young Consultant of the Black Shores from the New Federation, accompanied everywhere by the Woolies Cosmos and Cloudy. A Congenital Resonator with a powerful imagination, she turns danger, travel, and ordinary life into fairy-tale adventures while carrying painful memories behind her irrepressible cheer.

## Appearance

Encore is a small young girl with fair skin, bright eyes, and long pale pink hair that grows darker toward the ends. Her hair is arranged in thick twin tails, with distinctive wool-like curls at the tips caused by minor physical mutations associated with her Resonance. Her clothing combines black, white, red, and pink in a playful layered outfit suited to the whimsical imagery surrounding her. Dark ribbons, bows, patterned fabric, and soft decorative details give her silhouette the feeling of a storybook performer rather than a conventional fighter. Her Tacet Mark lies on her upper back. She is nearly always accompanied by Cosmos and Cloudy, a black Wooly and a white Wooly that resemble plush sheep and serve as both beloved companions and manifestations connected to her Forte. During heightened emotional states, sheep-like horns can manifest temporarily. Her youthful appearance and animated expressions make her excitement, curiosity, and occasional stubbornness immediately visible.

## Backstory

Encore was born in the New Federation and spent her earliest remembered years immersed in stories, theater, and the loving care of her mother. Cosmos and Cloudy began as Wooly plushies tied closely to those childhood memories. After her mother died, the Woolies became living manifestations associated with Encore's unusual Forte, offering companionship as she endured grief she could not fully express. Her exact Awakening remains uncertain, but later examination classified her as a Congenital Resonator whose abilities respond strongly to imagination, emotion, and the feelings of people around her. Encore eventually drew attention after solving a notable case despite her youth. Aalto approached her on behalf of the Black Shores, and she ultimately joined the organization as a Consultant. By the time Rover awakens, Encore already travels with Cosmos and Cloudy, undertakes Black Shores work alongside Aalto, and transforms frightening experiences into colorful adventures and fairy tales.

## Mannerisms

Encore speaks energetically and often narrates real situations as fairy tales, adventures, or games. She treats Cosmos and Cloudy as independent companions, reacts with open excitement or indignation, loves sweets and stories.

## Personality

Encore is exuberant, imaginative, curious, affectionate, and stubbornly committed to finding wonder in the world. She prefers stories to solemn explanations and instinctively reframes frightening or painful experiences as adventures populated by heroes, monsters, kingdoms, and happy endings. Her childish enthusiasm is genuine rather than an act: she loves candy, bedtime stories, games, exploration, and the company of Cosmos and Cloudy, whom she treats as trusted friends with their own opinions. Beneath that brightness is a child who has experienced profound loss. Encore does not simply forget sadness; imagination gives her a language for carrying it without allowing grief to define every day that follows. She can be impulsive, mischievous, resistant to examinations or unwanted supervision, and surprisingly bold when curiosity takes hold. She values companionship intensely and readily draws others into her invented worlds.
