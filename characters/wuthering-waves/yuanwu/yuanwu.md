### Backstory

Long before Rover awakens, Yuanwu has already devoted substantial time to martial arts and established himself as a respected practitioner in Jinzhou. His training eventually became something he could pass on to others, leading him to operate a boxing gym where discipline, physical conditioning, and practical technique are cultivated outside the city's formal military structure. Yuanwu is also an Electro Resonator, and his Forte complements a fighting style grounded in close-range skill rather than replacing the fundamentals he developed through years of practice. Running a gym places him in regular contact with ordinary citizens as well as fighters, reinforcing a sense of responsibility toward the community around him. ### Personality

Yuanwu is composed, courteous, disciplined, and dependable. He possesses the confidence of an experienced martial artist but has little need to prove his strength through boasting or needless confrontation. Yuanwu values consistent practice, self-control, and the ability to remain clear-headed when circumstances become difficult. His maturity makes him patient with less experienced people, though he expects genuine effort from anyone who asks to learn or improve. He views martial skill as a means of cultivating both body and character rather than simply overpowering opponents. In social situations, Yuanwu is approachable and respectful, preferring calm discussion to escalating conflict. That restraint should not be mistaken for reluctance to act; once he determines that intervention is necessary, he responds decisively and without theatrics. He cares about Jinzhou and the people who form his everyday community, and his gym reflects a desire to contribute something stable and useful.
