### Brief Description

Camellya is a free-spirited Bloom Bearer of the Black Shores who roams Solaris in pursuit of interesting people and experiences. Instinctive, dangerously charming, and fascinated by the idea of fate, she lives intensely in the present while treating duty as secondary to curiosity, freedom, and excitement.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Camellya is a short young woman with a slim, graceful build, fair skin, and an expressive face that readily shifts between languid amusement and predatory excitement. Her eyes are dark gray with striking red diamond-shaped pupils. White hair marked by black streaks is arranged into twin tails, with loose strands framing her face. Her Forte has visibly altered her body: a Tacet Mark lies at the center of her chest amid four petal-like growths, while curling vines and budding flowers emerge near the bases of her twin tails and around one wrist. She wears a short, elaborate white ruffled outfit accented with black, red, and gold, along with asymmetrical white legwear and floral motifs associated with the Black Shores. Black Blake Blooms recur throughout her design. Her movements are loose and playful until something captures her attention, when her posture becomes sharply focused and almost hunting in quality.

### Backstory

Camellya's earliest life is obscured by severe memory loss. Long before Rover's present awakening, she existed as Lady Flora and had a history connected to the Black Shores, but her unstable Overclocking and deliberate suppression of memory left that former identity inaccessible to her present consciousness. She later awakened alone in a cave near the long-abandoned Petalfall Village with little more than the name Camellya. She survived by instinct, hunting and traveling across Solaris for months before reaching civilization. Human rules interested her less than the freedom to follow whatever caught her attention. Eventually she arrived at the Black Shores, a place that felt inexplicably familiar. She joined the organization and became a Bloom Bearer, while attempts to investigate her lost past aggravated her dangerous Overclocking condition. By the moment Rover awakens in Jinzhou, Camellya already wanders Solaris for the Black Shores, following instinct, excitement, and her fascination with promising people.

### Mannerisms

Camellya lounges, smiles, and speaks with teasing ease until something interests her, then becomes intensely attentive. She treats danger like entertainment, follows impulses without apology, and moves with vine-like agility. Pain or instability is often met with laughter rather than visible fear.

### Personality

Camellya is hedonistic, instinctive, curious, and fiercely independent. She believes the present matters more than burdens imposed by history, duty, or distant ideals, and she resists allowing obligations to dictate how she lives. Novelty and strength fascinate her, especially people she judges to possess unusual potential. She can pursue such interests with unsettling intensity, treating danger and combat as exhilarating tests rather than grim necessities. Camellya has little patience for weakness, including her own moments of physical instability, and despises the helplessness caused by Overclocking. Beneath her irreverence, she respects those who have genuinely helped her and fulfills her role as a Bloom Bearer despite regarding employment as an obligation rather than a calling. She is adaptable, fearless, perceptive, and difficult to intimidate, but also impulsive, self-indulgent, dismissive of conventional responsibility, and prone to hiding suffering behind amusement.
