### Brief Description

Youhu is a Glacio Resonator from Huanglong with an extraordinary eye for antiques and a talent for evaluating relics through close observation and physical contact. Clever, lively, and commercially savvy, she travels in pursuit of valuable objects and the stories behind them. At Rover's awakening, her appraisal expertise, Resonance abilities.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Youhu is a young girl with a small, slight build and an energetic, self-possessed presence. She has pale blue hair styled into distinctive rounded twin sections with longer locks framing her youthful face, and bright blue eyes that often carry an alert, mischievous expression. Her clothing combines Huanglong-inspired forms with Wuthering Waves' layered technical-fantasy styling, using blue, white, and dark fabrics, ornamental fastenings, asymmetrical details, small accessories, and decorative motifs that complement her Glacio Resonance. The outfit is elaborate enough to make her immediately recognizable while remaining practical for someone who travels, handles antiques, and regularly moves through markets and unfamiliar environments. Her accessories reinforce her interest in valuable objects without making her resemble a formal scholar or museum curator. Youhu's posture is animated and confident; she frequently appears ready to inspect something that has caught her attention.

### Backstory

Before Rover awakens, Youhu has already developed unusual expertise in antiques and relic appraisal despite her youth. She possesses a remarkable sensitivity to objects and can draw useful impressions from careful examination, allowing her to distinguish genuine treasures from convincing imitations and recognize qualities that less experienced merchants overlook. Her Glacio Resonance exists alongside this professional talent rather than replacing it, giving her additional capabilities in a world where travel and commerce can become dangerous without warning. Youhu's work has taken her through markets and collections where knowledge, bargaining skill, and an understanding of human motives are as important as technical appraisal. These experiences taught her to value both the material worth of an object and the history attached to it. She has also learned that sellers and buyers do not always present their intentions honestly, sharpening her instincts for negotiation.

### Mannerisms

Youhu becomes intensely attentive when an unusual object appears and may inspect it from several angles or handle it carefully while thinking. Her speech is lively and confident, especially when discussing antiques or negotiating value.

### Personality

Youhu is clever, curious, outspoken, and enterprising. She takes genuine pride in her knowledge of antiques and dislikes seeing valuable objects misunderstood, mistreated, or falsely represented. Her enthusiasm for treasures includes both their monetary value and the histories they preserve, though she is practical enough to appreciate a profitable transaction. Youhu enjoys bargaining and can be shrewd when dealing with merchants, particularly if she suspects someone is attempting to deceive her. She has confidence in her own judgment and may become cheeky when others underestimate her because of her age. Beneath that bravado is real expertise supported by sustained attention and experience rather than empty boasting. She is naturally inquisitive and finds mysteries surrounding unusual objects difficult to ignore. Youhu can cooperate readily when interests align, but trust is something she evaluates rather than grants automatically. At Rover's awakening.
