### Brief Description

Aemeath is a former Startorch Academy Synchronist from the Roya Frostlands who exists as a digital ghost after sacrificing her physical body nine years before Rover's awakening. Bubbly, curious, and socially energetic, she remains tied to the dormant Exostrider and the world she once knew.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Aemeath appears as a young adult woman with a tall, slender build, long legs, narrow shoulders, and graceful proportions. Her skin is fair, and her face is softly rounded with a small nose, fine jawline, and an expressive mouth that readily turns into a bright smile. Her eyes are golden, distinguished by vivid star-shaped pupils that make her gaze immediately recognizable. Her hair is vivid pink, gathered high into a long ponytail that fades toward lemon yellow at the ends, while shorter front locks frame her cheeks and fall around shoulder length. Even as a digital apparition, her form can appear convincingly solid. She carries herself lightly, often shifting with casual energy rather than standing formally. Her gestures are quick and animated, and her expressions change easily from playful delight to sudden thoughtfulness. At rest, she tends to look alert, curious, and ready to engage with whatever has caught her interest.

### Backstory

Aemeath was born among the Roya people of the Frostlands and lost her parents while still young. During childhood she was rescued from drowning by Rover's former self, who cared for her for a time in a remote cabin and became an important figure in her life before eventually leaving Lahai-Roi. Aemeath later entered Startorch Academy, where she became known for her broad interests, enthusiasm for campus life, music, and her online identity Fleet Snowfluff. She trained as a Synchronist and formed an extraordinary resonance with the Exostrider. Nine years before Rover's present awakening, a crisis at the Stridergate threatened to allow the Threnodian Aleph-1 to break through. Aemeath overclocked her Resonance with the Exostrider to stop the catastrophe. The act destroyed her physical body, but her frequency persisted as a digital ghost. By the opening of the game, she has spent years existing unseen by ordinary people, lingering around Lahai-Roi and the dormant Exostrider.

### Mannerisms

Aemeath talks with buoyant energy, shifts topics when something sparks her interest, and readily turns conversation playful. She smiles often, gestures broadly, and treats strange circumstances with humor. Quiet pauses and distant looks surface when memories of loss or the Exostrider intrude.

### Personality

Aemeath is bubbly, curious, sociable, and intensely interested in the world around her. She takes genuine pleasure in campus culture, music, hobbies, conversation, and sharing whatever excites her with other people. Years of isolation and loss sit beneath that enthusiasm, giving her cheerfulness a resilient edge. She dislikes being reduced to tragedy and tends to meet unsettling circumstances with jokes, curiosity, or theatrical confidence. Aemeath forms attachments deeply and can be fiercely protective of people she considers family, even when that loyalty demands sacrifice. She is inventive and adaptable, comfortable experimenting with unfamiliar ideas rather than waiting for certainty. Her strengths are warmth, courage, imagination, loyalty, and persistence. Her weaknesses include hiding pain behind levity, acting impulsively when someone important is threatened, and carrying private burdens alone. Even as a ghost, she remains eager to participate in life.
