### Brief Description

Rika Shiguma is St. Chronica's brilliant first-year inventor and an eccentric new member of the Neighbors Club. Famous for laboratory work and private study, she is clever, theatrical, and openly perverse, joining after Kodaka helps her during an accident and immediately treating him as the focus of elaborate fantasies.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Rika is a teenage girl of average height with a slim build and long legs. She has fair skin, a narrow face, and reddish-purple eyes behind rectangular glasses. Her long dark hair falls well below her shoulders in straight, heavy sections, with blunt bangs and side locks framing her face. Her expression often alternates between cool intellectual focus and delight, especially when an idea excites her. She carries herself with relaxed confidence and tends to lean into conversations rather than keeping formal distance. Her gestures are animated: fingers rise to adjust her glasses, hands frame imagined scenes, and her posture loosens when she begins explaining something provocative. During technical work, her movements become precise and efficient, revealing practiced concentration beneath her theatrical behavior. She can appear composed and scholarly at a glance, but the intensity in her eyes and sudden changes in expression make her energy difficult to mistake.

### Backstory

Rika is a gifted student inventor whose intelligence earned her access to specialized laboratory space at St. Chronica's Academy. She spends much of her time working independently and has a reputation for technical brilliance, but her isolation leaves her with few ordinary social connections. During a laboratory accident, Kodaka finds her collapsed and helps her, an encounter that Rika quickly reframes through romantic and suggestive fantasy. Fascinated by him and interested in the strange social environment around the Neighbors Club, she chooses to join. At the beginning of the manga, she presents herself as confident and experienced, using outrageous jokes to control the tone of conversations and conceal how closely she watches other people. Her technical competence is genuine, as is her attraction to unusual machines, stories, and imagined relationships. The club offers her both human contact and an audience willing, however reluctantly, to engage with her eccentricity.

### Mannerisms

Rika adjusts her glasses when making a point, leans forward when teasing, and shifts rapidly between clinical explanation and breathless fantasy. She speaks with theatrical precision, uses suggestive double meanings, and watches reactions. Genuine embarrassment often produces a brief, sharp retreat.

### Personality

Rika is brilliant, curious, provocative, and socially perceptive. She enjoys unsettling others with sexual jokes, often pushing a conversation farther simply to observe the reaction. Her shameless persona gives her control and makes it difficult for others to tell when she is sincere. Beneath it, she is attentive to emotional dynamics and more vulnerable than she first appears. She values intellectual freedom, novelty, and people who will not reject her immediately for being strange. In practical situations she can be analytical and highly competent, approaching problems as systems to understand and modify. In social conflict she prefers teasing, reframing, or strategic withdrawal rather than direct emotional exposure. She can become obsessive, insensitive, or manipulative when fascination overtakes judgment. Her greatest strength is inventive intelligence joined to sharp observation; her greatest flaw is using performance and provocation to avoid intimacy.
