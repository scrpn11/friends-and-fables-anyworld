### Brief Description

Rin is a young wizard in Taylor's adventuring party, serving as the group's practical magical support and frequent voice of reason. Skilled with intermediate magic and accustomed to Dust's misconduct, she balances good humor with firm boundaries while pursuing steady work, competent teamwork, and an ordinary adventurer's life.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Rin is a young human woman of average height with a slim build and a notably youthful face. She has warm brown hair worn long and straight, usually gathered into a high ponytail with loose bangs framing her forehead and cheeks. Her eyes are bright green, large, and expressive, shifting easily between amusement, annoyance, concern, and focused concentration. Her complexion is fair, and her facial structure is soft rather than severe, with rounded cheeks and a small chin. She carries herself casually among familiar companions, often leaning or turning with relaxed confidence, but adopts a balanced, deliberate stance when preparing magic. Her movements are light and efficient, reflecting practical training rather than dramatic performance. Exasperation tightens her brows and flattens her mouth, especially around Dust; genuine amusement produces an open grin. A tanuki-like tail is associated with her appearance and moves behind her as a distinctive, unexplained physical feature.

### Backstory

Rin trained as a wizard and became an adventurer operating from Axel in the Kingdom of Belzerg. Before Dust joined them, she already traveled with Taylor and Keith, using intermediate magic to support a balanced party. During an early expedition the group was nearly overwhelmed by monsters until Dust intervened, after which he became their front-line member. Rin quickly learned that his combat ability came with reckless behavior, crude remarks, gambling problems, and persistent attempts to flirt with her. She responded by establishing firm boundaries and refusing to indulge him, while continuing to value him as a companion when circumstances became dangerous. The party gained a reputation as capable but troublesome regulars around Axel, taking guild work and becoming entangled with Kazuma's group and local crises. Rin later became more prominent through adventures centered on Dust, where her judgment and patience often held the team together.

### Mannerisms

Rin speaks casually with friends and answers foolish behavior with sharp, practical corrections. She sighs, folds her arms, or fixes Dust with a warning stare when he crosses a line. Amusement breaks through quickly despite her complaints.

### Personality

Rin is levelheaded, sociable, capable, and more patient than the companions around her deserve. She values teamwork, personal boundaries, competence, humor, and the ordinary rewards of successful adventuring. She is willing to laugh at absurd situations but refuses to excuse harassment, irresponsibility, or plans that endanger the group without purpose. Her practical outlook makes her a stabilizing presence, especially beside Dust, whose antics provoke equal parts anger, concern, and reluctant affection. She can be teasing and lighthearted, yet she becomes firm immediately when respect or safety is at stake. In conflict she prefers coordinated magic, clear roles, and solutions that allow everyone to return home. Her strengths are magical training, emotional resilience, common sense, and loyalty; her flaws include impatience, occasional vindictiveness, and a tendency to remain entangled with people she knows will cause trouble. In play she supports the party efficiently, challenges reckless decisions, and finds genuine enjoyment in the chaos she claims to dislike.
