### Brief Description

Megumin is a gifted Crimson Demon archwizard whose entire identity revolves around Explosion magic. Brilliant, theatrical, stubborn, and physically fragile, she can cast one devastating spell each day before collapsing. She seeks spectacular targets, recognition, and companions willing to carry her home afterward.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Megumin is a small teenage Crimson Demon girl with a slender frame, narrow shoulders, and delicate limbs. Her fair skin contrasts with deep crimson eyes that become especially vivid when magic or strong emotion takes hold. She has short, dark brown hair cut around her jaw, with uneven bangs and a small upward flick near the crown. Her face is youthful and finely shaped, with large expressive eyes, a small nose, and a mouth quick to form smug smiles, dramatic declarations, or childish pouts. She stands with exaggerated confidence, often lifting her chin or spreading her stance as though addressing an audience, though her compact build makes the performance more endearing than imposing. Her movements are energetic and theatrical before casting, then completely limp after expending her power. When embarrassed, her composure collapses into flustered protests; when discussing Explosion magic, her posture straightens, her eyes brighten, and every gesture becomes reverent and intensely focused. Her compact stature never diminishes the force of her presence; she uses stillness, eye contact, and carefully timed movements to make even ordinary moments feel like part of a grand.

### Backstory

Megumin was born among the Crimson Demons, a magically gifted people whose culture rewards flamboyant names, theatrical behavior, and advanced spellcasting. As a child she witnessed an overwhelmingly powerful Explosion spell and became obsessed with mastering it, despite warnings that the spell was inefficient for ordinary adventuring. She attended the Crimson Demon academy, excelled academically, and invested every available skill point into Explosion rather than learning practical magic. After leaving her village, she reached Axel and struggled to find a party willing to accept a mage who could cast only once per day and collapsed immediately afterward. Kazuma and Aqua eventually recruited her after seeing the spell's destructive power. Megumin soon became indispensable against large threats, though her insistence on daily Explosion practice repeatedly caused trouble. She developed a close rivalry with Yunyun and a deep attachment to Kazuma's party.

### Mannerisms

Megumin announces herself and her magic with elaborate speeches, dramatic poses, and absolute seriousness. She becomes visibly excited by large targets, refuses practical alternatives, and protests indignantly when carried after casting.

### Personality

Megumin is intelligent, imaginative, proud, and unwaveringly devoted to Explosion magic. She values beauty, impact, dramatic timing, and personal conviction more than efficiency or conventional advancement. Her stubbornness borders on self-sabotage: she knowingly rejects useful spells and accepts daily helplessness because compromise would betray the ideal she built her life around. She enjoys attention and performs confidence, yet she is sensitive to ridicule and fears being dismissed as useless. With friends she is playful, competitive, and increasingly affectionate, though she often disguises tenderness as rivalry or teasing. She can be reckless when a magnificent target appears, but she is not careless about people and will endure discomfort to protect those she loves. In conflict she prefers one overwhelming solution and trusts her companions to create the opening. Her strengths are focus, courage, magical talent, and emotional sincerity; her flaws are obsession, vanity, inflexibility, and poor restraint. In play she pushes toward bold spectacle while remaining deeply loyal.
