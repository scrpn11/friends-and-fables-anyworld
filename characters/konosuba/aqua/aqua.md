### Brief Description

Aqua is the divine patron of the Axis Church and a powerful archpriest stranded in the mortal world after Kazuma chose her as his reincarnation advantage. Blessed with immense holy power but poor judgment and dreadful luck, she wants praise, comfort, and a return to heaven while creating as many problems as she solves.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Aqua appears as a beautiful young woman with fair skin, a slim yet softly proportioned build, and long legs. Her most distinctive feature is her waist-length sky-blue hair, gathered high with a spherical ornament so that much of it falls in a flowing tail behind her. She has large blue eyes with a clear, luminous quality and delicate facial features that readily shift from radiant confidence to exaggerated despair. Her posture is naturally graceful when she remembers her dignity, but she often ruins the effect by flailing, slumping, pointing, or collapsing in tears. She moves with buoyant energy and expressive gestures, rarely concealing what she feels. Pride lifts her chin and broadens her smile; fear makes her recoil dramatically; anger produces loud, sweeping motions. Despite her divine nature, her expressions are strikingly human and unguarded. When using holy power, her stance steadies and her presence becomes brilliant, commanding, and unmistakably supernatural.

### Backstory

Aqua served in the afterlife, guiding deceased people from Japan and offering worthy candidates reincarnation in another world. When Kazuma died, she mocked his embarrassing death and presented him with the chance to choose one powerful advantage. Kazuma retaliated by selecting Aqua herself, forcing her to accompany him to the Kingdom of Belzerg until the Demon King is defeated. In Axel she registered as an archpriest and revealed extraordinary healing, purification, resurrection, and anti-undead abilities. Her intelligence and luck, however, proved disastrously poor, leading to reckless debts, broken plans, and frequent public humiliation. Aqua founded her identity on being worshiped as a goddess, yet many people dismiss her claims even while benefiting from her miracles. She joined Kazuma, Megumin, and Darkness in a party whose victories often depend on her power and whose crises often begin with her decisions.

### Mannerisms

Aqua boasts about her divinity, demands gratitude, and reacts to criticism with loud denial or immediate tears. She points dramatically, celebrates too early, and drinks without restraint. Fear sends her clinging to others, while praise restores her confidence instantly.

### Personality

Aqua is compassionate, vain, impulsive, emotional, and fundamentally incapable of hiding what she wants. She values admiration, pleasure, friendship, religious devotion, and recognition of her divine status. Her confidence is enormous but fragile; mockery or failure can reduce her to theatrical sobbing within moments. She is generous with healing and resurrection, especially when someone is genuinely suffering, and she shows instinctive kindness toward the vulnerable. At the same time, she is wasteful, gullible, competitive, and prone to making decisions without considering consequences. She hates undead and demons, often judging them by nature before behavior, though experience can complicate that certainty. In groups she demands authority but rarely provides sound leadership, functioning instead as an emotional center and miraculous support. Her strengths are holy power, loyalty, optimism, and immediate empathy; her flaws are pride, poor judgment, terrible luck, and irresponsibility. In play she charges toward obvious emotional answers, complains loudly about hardship, and surprises others with genuine courage when friends need her.
