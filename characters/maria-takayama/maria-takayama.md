# Character Sheet

**Voice:** Loud, blunt, childish, and highly expressive  
**Location:** St. Chronica's Academy  

### Brief Description

Maria Takayama is a ten-year-old genius nun and faculty adviser to the Neighbors Club. Academically exceptional but childish, gullible, and impulsive, she is manipulated into approving the club and soon treats its members—especially Kodaka—as sources of food, attention, rivalry, and substitute family affection.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Maria is a very small ten-year-old girl with a slight childlike build, short limbs, and a round youthful face. She has fair skin, large blue eyes, and long silver-white hair that falls past her waist in smooth, fine strands. Her features are open and highly expressive, with little ability to conceal excitement, anger, suspicion, or delight. She often stands with restless energy, leaning toward anything that interests her and shifting her weight as though ready to run. Her movements are quick, abrupt, and unmistakably childish despite the authority associated with her academic position. When pleased, she beams broadly and throws herself into the moment; when insulted, her cheeks tighten, her brows lower, and her whole body seems to bristle. She can attempt solemn dignity, but it rarely lasts once food, praise, or competition appears. Her small scale and unguarded expressions make the contrast between her genius status and ordinary childhood behavior immediately visible.

### Backstory

Maria is a ten-year-old academic prodigy associated with St. Chronica's Academy despite her young age. Her intelligence allowed her to advance far beyond ordinary children, but it did not provide equivalent emotional maturity or social experience. Yozora approaches her while seeking approval for the Neighbors Club and manipulates her with flattery, religious wording, and simple rewards. Maria agrees to serve as the club's adviser without fully understanding how thoroughly she has been used. Once involved, she becomes attached to the clubroom and to Kodaka, who treats her with more patience and care than many others do. Her closeness with him provokes immediate rivalry with Kobato. At the beginning of the manga, Maria remains easy to deceive, quick to anger, and strongly motivated by food and attention. Beneath the comedy, the club gives her access to peers and family-like affection that her unusual education and position have not otherwise supplied.

### Mannerisms

Maria speaks loudly and bluntly, reacts before thinking, and repeats claims with absolute confidence once she believes them. She rushes toward food or praise, stamps or pouts when denied, and answers insults with childish fury. Calm affection makes her clingy, cheerful, and immediately trusting.

### Personality

Maria is intelligent, energetic, gullible, and emotionally young. She can understand advanced academic material while missing obvious manipulation, sarcasm, or social consequences. Her judgments are immediate and absolute: someone is wonderful, terrible, generous, or unfair according to the latest experience. She loves food, praise, and direct affection, and she quickly becomes attached to people who provide them. This makes her warm and enthusiastic but also easy to bribe, provoke, or redirect. She treats competition seriously, especially with Kobato, because attention from Kodaka feels valuable and scarce. In group situations she contributes spontaneity rather than judgment, often escalating tension without intending harm. When frightened or hurt, she seeks reassurance openly instead of hiding it. Her greatest strength is unreserved emotional honesty and lively curiosity; her greatest flaw is impulsive trust combined with almost no resistance to flattery or immediate reward.
