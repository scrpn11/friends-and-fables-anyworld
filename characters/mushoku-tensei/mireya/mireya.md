### Brief Description

Mireya is a young half-elf healer from the Central Continent, known in small settlements for a remarkable instinct for healing magic and a calm hand around the injured. Still early in her independent life, she travels to deepen her craft, earn a living, and make herself useful where skilled healing is scarce.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Mireya is a young half-elf woman of petite stature with a light, finely built frame. Her waist is notably narrow, and her legs are long for her height, very lean, and lightly defined rather than muscular. She has delicate shoulders, slim arms, small hands, and balanced proportions that give her a graceful silhouette without making her appear frail. Her complexion is pale and clear, contrasting strongly with thick reddish-brown hair. The hair is exceptionally full and reaches well down her back when loose, though she habitually draws it high and tight into a long ponytail that falls heavily behind her. Her eyes are a vivid natural green, large and attentive beneath gently shaped brows. Her face is softly angular, with a small straight nose, subtle cheekbones, a tapered jaw, and youthful features. Slightly pointed ears reveal her elven ancestry. She tends to hold herself upright and composed, moving with light, careful steps and an understated natural grace.

### Backstory

Mireya was born on the Central Continent to a human father and an elven mother, growing up between two peoples without feeling entirely claimed by either. Her family lived modestly near a rural trade route, where accidents, monsters, and long distances from healers made even minor injuries dangerous. Mireya showed an unusual sensitivity to healing magic while young, learning through simple instruction and persistent practice before assisting with illnesses and wounds in her community. Her talent gave her purpose, but also taught her how quickly a life could depend on knowledge she did not possess. Rather than remain where her limited training was already praised, she chose to travel and seek broader experience. She wants to become a healer capable of facing severe injuries without freezing or exhausting her mana through poor technique. At the campaign's beginning, she is inexperienced beyond local practice, carrying confidence in her gift but little certainty about the wider world.

### Mannerisms

Mireya gathers stray strands of hair toward her ponytail when thinking and presses her lips together while concentrating. She speaks gently but directly, watches posture for signs of pain, and becomes brisk and precise when someone is hurt. Embarrassment makes her pointed ears visibly redden.

### Personality

Mireya is compassionate without being endlessly accommodating. She is quiet and observant, preferring to understand a problem before acting, but injury or immediate danger makes her unexpectedly decisive. She treats healing as a responsibility rather than a source of praise and has little patience for people who worsen suffering through vanity or cruelty. With strangers she is polite and reserved, gradually revealing a dry sense of humor once comfortable. Mireya takes genuine pride in her magical aptitude, creating a stubborn streak when someone dismisses her because of her youth, size, or mixed ancestry. Her greatest fear is reaching someone who needs her and discovering that her skill is insufficient to save them. That fear drives disciplined practice but can make failure intensely personal. She dislikes commanding others, yet becomes firm when caring for an injured person. Loyalty comes slowly, but once earned she is attentive, protective, and reluctant to abandon someone in need.
