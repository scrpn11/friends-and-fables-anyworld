### Brief Description

Kelabren is a thirty-six-year-old swordsman and dark-magic user who fights with a matched pair of chokuto. His style blends aggressive close-quarters swordsmanship with dark magic that reinforces his body, allowing him to strike harder, move faster, endure punishment, and keep fighting beyond ordinary human limits. He can also shape darkness into direct offensive spells, making him dangerous both at blade range and at a distance.

**Character Type:** Playable

### Appearance

Kelabren is 5-foot-8 with a practical, wiry build rather than a bulky one. His body is conditioned for balance, acceleration, and sustained close combat. He has captivating blue eyes, a broad genuine smile, and dark thick hair kept longer on top with shorter controlled sides. He carries two chokuto at his hips and favors durable travel clothes that leave his movement unrestricted. When channeling dark magic, shadow gathers around his limbs and blades in dense black-violet currents that seem to cling to him rather than radiate outward.

### Backstory

Kelabren was born in a small settlement on the Central Continent and grew up in a working family far removed from nobility or formal magical institutions. His family taught him discipline, practical survival, and the value of getting people home safely. During a violent monster attack compounded by severe weather, Kelabren made a desperate decision while trying to guide his family to safety. He survived; his parents and younger sister did not.

He carried the guilt forward into adulthood, burying it beneath drinking, flirting, jokes, and a deliberately careless reputation. He eventually developed an unusual affinity for dark magic. Rather than following the path of a conventional magician, Kelabren learned to force mana directly through his muscles, nerves, and bones, using darkness to reinforce his body for sword combat. He trained with two straight chokuto and gradually built a fighting style around overwhelming an opponent with speed, pressure, reinforced physical power, and close-range dark magic.

His magic resembles a warrior's discipline more than traditional back-line spellcasting. He prefers to fight in reach of his blades, using dark magic to sharpen his reactions, harden his body, strengthen his strikes, and project cutting or crushing bursts of darkness when steel alone is not enough.

### Mannerisms

Kelabren grins when tension rises, leans too close while flirting, and answers concern with a joke or exaggerated toast. He sprawls across chairs and doorframes, winks at strangers, and lets his smile disappear for a heartbeat when talk turns to family, storms, or helpless people. In combat he becomes noticeably quieter, with his humor giving way to intense concentration and clipped movements.

### Personality

Kelabren is charming, flirtatious, reckless, and difficult to embarrass. He turns danger, authority, grief, and his own mistakes into banter, drinks heavily, and acts as though consequences belong to tomorrow. His humor is warm and disarming, but it also lets him dodge sincerity and questions that might expose how deeply he hurts.

Beneath the careless performance, Kelabren is perceptive, protective, and far more dependable in a crisis than his reputation suggests. He dislikes cruelty, exploitation, and people who use status to trap the powerless. He prefers improvisation to planning and takes foolish risks when someone else is in danger. Guilt over his family's deaths has convinced him that closeness gives him another chance to fail someone, so he keeps people at arm's length even while craving connection.

### Stat Adjustments

## Skills

- **Dark Magic Affinity:** Kelabren possesses a strong affinity for darkness-based mana and has learned to manipulate it primarily through his own body and weapons rather than through conventional long-form spellcasting.
- **Dark Reinforcement — Strength:** Channels dark mana through his musculature to dramatically increase striking power, grip strength, and explosive force.
- **Dark Reinforcement — Agility:** Reinforces nerves, reflexes, and leg drive with dark mana, allowing sudden bursts of speed, rapid directional changes, and faster sword exchanges.
- **Dark Reinforcement — Endurance:** Wraps his body in dense reinforcing mana to endure impacts, resist fatigue, and continue fighting through punishment that would normally slow him down.
- **Dark Reinforcement — Full Body:** Combines strength, agility, and endurance reinforcement into a single combat state. This is Kelabren's preferred mode when fighting seriously with both chokuto.
- **Dark-Coated Blades:** Channels dark mana along one or both chokuto, increasing the force and penetration of his cuts and allowing the blades to interact with magical defenses more effectively.
- **Ki Awareness:** Kelabren has developed a combat instinct for sensing hostile presence, movement, and killing intent in his immediate surroundings. It functions more like heightened battle awareness than formal magical detection.

## Spellbook

### Dark Magic

- **Dark Reinforcement: Black Drive** — Floods Kelabren's body with dark mana to heighten strength, speed, reflexes, and durability for close combat.
- **Dark Cloak: Iron Night** — Condenses darkness around his body as a reinforcing layer that reduces the effect of impacts and helps him remain standing through sustained punishment.
- **Dark Blade: Black Edge** — Coats a chokuto in compressed dark mana, strengthening the cut and allowing the blade to bite more effectively into armor, monsters, and magical defenses.
- **Dark Slash** — Releases a compressed crescent of dark mana from a sword swing, extending the reach of a cut beyond the physical blade.
- **Dark Piercer** — Fires a narrow, concentrated lance of darkness intended to punch through a single target or magical defense.
- **Black Impact** — Condenses dark mana around a fist, foot, shoulder, or sword hilt before releasing it in a short-range burst of concussive force.
- **Dark Binding** — Sends bands of shadow toward a target to hinder movement and create an opening for close-range sword attacks.
- **Abyssal Sweep** — A broad horizontal release of dark mana intended to pressure multiple nearby enemies or force them back.
- **Darkness-Cloaked Twin Strike** — Coats both chokuto in dense dark mana and delivers a simultaneous or chained two-blade attack with substantially greater force than an ordinary sword combination.
