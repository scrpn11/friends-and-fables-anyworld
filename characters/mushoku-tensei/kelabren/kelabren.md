### Brief Description

Kelabren is a thirty-six-year-old human secretly retained by Ranoa University of Magic to identify rare candidates for his body-reinforcement magic. To most of the university he is merely an eccentric, frequently drunk member of the faculty; only the university's head knows his true assignment.

**Character Type:** Playable

### Appearance

Kelabren is a thirty-six-year-old human man standing 5-foot-8, with a compact, wiry build developed for speed, balance, and sustained physical exertion rather than imposing size. His shoulders are moderately broad above a lean torso and narrow waist, while his forearms and hands show the subtle definition of long martial training. Captivating blue eyes are his most striking feature, often carrying an amused brightness even when drink has left them tired or unfocused. His mature face has a straight nose, defined jaw, and faint lines of strain around the eyes without appearing heavily weathered. Thick dark hair is slightly unruly, longer on top and shorter along the sides. His complexion is lightly weathered from years outdoors. He habitually holds himself with loose, almost careless balance, sometimes slouching when relaxed. Even when he appears unsteady or inattentive, his natural movements remain economical, controlled, and capable of becoming abruptly precise.

### Backstory

Kelabren was born to a working family on the Central Continent. During a violent monster attack worsened by severe weather, he chose a dangerous route while guiding his parents and younger sister toward shelter. They died; he survived, and the guilt followed him into adulthood. His unusual magical studies eventually produced a discipline that channels mana through the body to reinforce strength, speed, endurance, and martial technique. His mastery drew the attention of the head of Ranoa University of Magic, who brought him onto the faculty under a concealed mandate: observe students and decide who, if anyone, deserves instruction in his methods. Staff and students know nothing of the arrangement or of the twin chokuto he keeps hidden. Even the head does not know Kelabren's deeper purpose. He intends to find loyal disciples and eventually forge them into a private force capable of carving out a realm free from the cruelty, corruption, and exploitation he sees in existing states.

### Mannerisms

Kelabren grins when tension rises, flirts freely, and answers concern with jokes or an exaggerated toast. At the university he often wanders the halls visibly drunk or disappears into his office to drink alone. When something truly interests him, the drunken looseness vanishes.

### Personality

Kelabren is charming, flirtatious, reckless, and difficult to embarrass. He uses humor and alcohol to keep grief at a distance, presenting himself as less attentive and responsible than he truly is. He despises cruelty, exploitation, corruption, and systems that let powerful people prey upon the vulnerable. Protective instincts make him dependable when danger becomes real, but guilt and fear of attachment encourage him to keep relationships shallow. Kelabren values practical ability, resolve, adaptability, and loyalty more than pedigree or prestige. As an observer, he prefers to watch people when they believe no authority is judging them. He can be patient when pursuing a larger purpose and is willing to conceal his intentions from allies. His ambition is immense but private: he wants loyal, capable followers to seize territory and establish a realm governed. He sees teaching as both a responsibility and a means of finding those people.
