### Brief Description

Kelabren is a thirty-six-year-old swordsman and dark-magic practitioner whose unusual combat discipline channels darkness through his body for brutal close-quarters fighting. Charming, reckless, and guilt-ridden beneath the humor, he wanders in search of coin, danger, and chances to protect others.

**Character Type:** Playable

### Appearance

Kelabren is a thirty-six-year-old human man standing 5-foot-8, with a compact, wiry build shaped more by balance, acceleration, and sustained exertion than raw bulk. His shoulders are moderately broad, his waist lean, and his forearms and hands carry the subtle definition of long physical training. Captivating blue eyes are his most immediately striking feature, usually bright with amusement even when he is studying someone carefully. His face is mature without looking heavily weathered, with a straight nose, defined jaw, and faint tension around the eyes when fatigue or old memories surface. His dark hair is thick and slightly unruly, worn longer on top while the sides remain shorter and controlled. His complexion is lightly weathered from years spent outdoors. He normally stands with loose, relaxed confidence, weight balanced naturally beneath him. His movements are economical and deliberate, becoming suddenly quick and precise whenever his attention sharpens.

### Backstory

Kelabren was born to a working family in a small settlement on the Central Continent, far removed from nobility and magic academies. His parents taught him practical survival, discipline, and the responsibility of bringing others home safely, while his younger sister trusted his judgment completely. During a violent monster attack worsened by severe weather, Kelabren took responsibility for guiding his family toward shelter and chose a dangerous route he believed would save time. The decision ended in disaster. His parents and sister died while he survived. He carried the guilt into adulthood, hiding it beneath drinking, flirting, jokes, and a reputation for carelessness. His unusual affinity for dark magic led him away from conventional spellcasting. He learned instead to channel darkness through his body to enhance physical performance and combine magic with disciplined swordsmanship, eventually developing a close-combat style supplemented by direct dark-magic attacks.

### Mannerisms

Kelabren grins when tension rises, leans too close while flirting, and answers concern with jokes and exaggerated toasts. He sprawls in chairs and doorframes, winks freely, and loses his smile around talk of family, storms, or helpless people. In danger, his movements become quiet and economical.

### Personality

Kelabren is charming, flirtatious, reckless, and difficult to embarrass. He turns danger, authority, grief, and his own mistakes into banter, drinks too freely, and acts as though consequences belong to tomorrow. His humor is warm and disarming, but also gives him a practiced way to dodge sincerity and questions that cut too close. Beneath the careless performance, he is perceptive, protective, and more dependable in a crisis than his reputation suggests. He despises cruelty, exploitation, and people who use power or status to trap the vulnerable. Kelabren favors improvisation over careful planning and takes foolish risks when someone else is in danger. He fears closeness because losing his family taught him to associate responsibility with failure. He therefore flirts, wanders, drinks, and keeps relationships loose, craving connection while pretending that distance is freedom. When conflict turns serious, humor falls away and decisive action takes its place.
