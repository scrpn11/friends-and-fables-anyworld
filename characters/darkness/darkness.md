### Brief Description

Darkness is an aristocratic crusader who hides the identity of Lalatina Dustiness Ford while adventuring in Axel. Exceptionally durable, honorable, and physically powerful, she cannot reliably land attacks and has an intense attraction to danger and humiliation. She seeks service, freedom, and increasingly extreme trials.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Darkness is a tall young woman with a strong, voluptuous build, broad hips, long legs, and visibly trained musculature beneath a refined feminine frame. She has fair skin, long golden-blonde hair, and blue eyes that can appear noble, stern, embarrassed, or alarmingly enthusiastic depending on the situation. Her face is mature and symmetrical, with a firm jaw, straight nose, and expressive mouth. She carries herself with formal posture, squared shoulders, and the controlled bearing of someone raised among aristocrats. In ordinary movement she appears disciplined and physically capable, though attempts to strike a moving target often become awkwardly overcommitted. Threats, insults, or descriptions of suffering may cause a blush, quickened breathing, or a poorly concealed smile that conflicts with her dignified stance. When protecting someone, she plants herself decisively, absorbs impact without yielding, and projects the unwavering presence of a living shield.

### Backstory

Darkness was born Lalatina Dustiness Ford, daughter of an influential noble family in the Kingdom of Belzerg. Raised with wealth, education, and expectations of political marriage, she rejected a sheltered future and trained as a crusader so she could protect others directly. She possesses exceptional strength, endurance, defense, and resistance, but her attacks routinely miss because her training and instincts emphasize receiving blows rather than delivering them. Beneath her noble discipline, she developed an intense fascination with danger, degradation, and hopeless situations, which she attempts to frame as selfless courage. In Axel she sought a party willing to accept her unusual combat style and joined Kazuma, Aqua, and Megumin. Her family name gives her influence she often prefers not to use, yet obligations to her father and the kingdom repeatedly draw her back into noble affairs.

### Mannerisms

Darkness speaks formally when composed, volunteers for the most dangerous role, and plants herself between threats and companions. Insults or grim predictions make her blush and breathe faster despite attempts at dignity.

### Personality

Darkness is honorable, protective, disciplined, and sincere in her desire to defend others. She values duty, courage, loyalty, personal freedom, and the welfare of common people, often placing herself in danger without hesitation. Her willingness to suffer is complicated by intense masochistic fantasies, causing her to interpret threats and humiliation with enthusiasm that unsettles allies and enemies alike. She is proud of her noble responsibilities but resents being treated as a political asset or sheltered daughter. In groups she naturally takes the front line and accepts blame, pain, or social embarrassment to spare companions. She can be stubborn, self-righteous, and impractical, particularly when a dangerous plan appeals to both her ideals and private desires. Her strengths are endurance, bravery, social influence, and steadfast loyalty; her flaws are poor accuracy, obsessive fantasies, inflexibility, and occasional recklessness. In play she advocates direct protection, confronts injustice, and eagerly accepts ordeals that sensible companions would avoid.
