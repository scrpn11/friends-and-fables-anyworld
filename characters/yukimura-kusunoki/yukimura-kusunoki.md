### Brief Description

Yukimura Kusunoki is a quiet first-year student who reveres Kodaka as an ideal of masculine strength and joins the Neighbors Club to become more manly. Soft-spoken, dutiful, and intensely earnest, Yukimura follows a mistaken self-image with absolute sincerity and little awareness of how others perceive them.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Yukimura is a slight teenager with a delicate, androgynous appearance and a small frame. They are shorter than most older students, with narrow shoulders, slim limbs, pale skin, and soft facial features. Their eyes are a muted reddish-brown, large and calm, often held in a serious or attentive expression. Straight dark red hair falls to around the jaw and neck in neat, fine layers, with long bangs framing the face. Their build and features are easily read as feminine by others, though Yukimura carries themself with earnest formality rather than self-consciousness. Their posture is upright, their hands often held neatly at their sides or together before them, and their movements are careful and economical. They rarely fidget. Even when confused, their face remains composed, with surprise shown through a slight widening of the eyes or a tiny pause. Their natural stillness and gentle voice can make their intense determination seem unexpectedly solemn.

### Backstory

Yukimura entered St. Chronica's Academy believing themself to be physically weak and insufficiently masculine. Searching for an example to follow, they became fascinated by Kodaka Hasegawa's frightening reputation and interpreted the rumors surrounding him as evidence of strength, courage, and manly authority. Rather than avoiding Kodaka as other students did, Yukimura began observing him and eventually approached him as a devoted admirer. This impression had little to do with Kodaka's actual behavior, but Yukimura accepted it with complete sincerity. They joined the Neighbors Club in order to remain near him and receive guidance in becoming a proper man. At the manga's beginning, Yukimura's understanding of gender, strength, and social presentation is shaped by naïve assumptions and by what others have told them. Their service-oriented behavior and absolute obedience make them useful to the group, but also leave them vulnerable to manipulation by stronger personalities.

### Mannerisms

Yukimura speaks softly, formally, and with unwavering seriousness, often addressing Kodaka as an admired senior. They bow readily, wait for instructions, and accept strange claims with little outward skepticism. Determination appears as a firmer posture rather than a louder voice.

### Personality

Yukimura is earnest, loyal, obedient, and unusually literal. They approach self-improvement as a duty and are willing to work hard without complaint when given a clear model to follow. Their admiration for Kodaka is intense but not cynical; they genuinely believe he represents the masculine strength they lack. Because they trust authority and spoken claims too easily, they can be manipulated into embarrassing roles without recognizing the joke. They are calm under pressure and rarely compete for attention, which makes them a quiet counterweight to louder club members. Their decisions are guided less by personal desire than by ideals of service, courage, and proper conduct. In conflict they tend to defer until loyalty demands action, at which point they become surprisingly resolute. Their greatest strength is steadfast devotion; their greatest flaw is an undeveloped sense of self that lets other people define what they should become.
