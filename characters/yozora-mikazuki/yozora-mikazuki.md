### Brief Description

Yozora Mikazuki is a sharp-tongued second-year student and founder of the Neighbors Club. Brilliant, proud, and deeply isolated, she hides her fear of rejection behind control, mockery, and rigid rules while using the club as a practical way to create the friendships she cannot ask for directly.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Yozora is a teenage girl of average height with a slender, balanced build and long limbs. She has pale skin, a narrow face, and large violet eyes set beneath straight brows that often sharpen into a cool, appraising stare. At the beginning of the manga, her black hair is very long, smooth, and straight, falling well past her waist with blunt bangs framing her forehead. Her features are refined and conventionally beautiful, but her habitual expression is reserved, severe, or faintly contemptuous rather than welcoming. She stands with controlled posture and minimal wasted movement, giving the impression that she is always measuring the room before acting. Her gestures are precise, and even small shifts of her gaze can feel deliberate. When surprised, embarrassed, or emotionally cornered, her composure briefly cracks: her eyes widen, her shoulders tense, and her face becomes more openly youthful before she quickly restores her guarded mask.

### Backstory

Yozora grew up lonely and defensive, learning early to treat vulnerability as something other people could exploit. As a child she befriended Kodaka Hasegawa while presenting herself under the nickname Sora, but their separation left the bond unresolved. Years later she attends St. Chronica's Academy and remains socially isolated despite her intelligence and beauty. She copes by inventing an imaginary friend named Tomo and speaking to her when alone. Kodaka accidentally discovers this after returning to a classroom, forcing Yozora into an awkward conversation about friendship. Recognizing that both of them lack real social lives, she quickly creates the Neighbors Club under a respectable religious pretext and recruits Kodaka as its first member. At the beginning of the manga, she treats the club as both an experiment and a shield: a structured place where friendship might develop without requiring her to confess that she wants it.

### Mannerisms

Yozora speaks in a dry, clipped tone and uses cutting observations as both humor and defense. She holds eye contact when challenging someone, folds her arms when displeased, and becomes especially still when embarrassed. Praise or tenderness often makes her look away before she answers.

### Personality

Yozora is intelligent, disciplined, observant, and fiercely self-protective. She notices weaknesses quickly and often turns them into jokes, rules, or leverage before anyone can use hers against her. Her leadership is controlling rather than warm: she organizes, directs, and provokes, expecting others to keep up. She values loyalty and hates superficiality, but her fear of humiliation makes honest affection difficult. She can be jealous, vindictive, and verbally cruel, especially toward rivals or people who expose her insecurity. Even so, she is not indifferent. She wants genuine companionship and is willing to create opportunities for it, though she disguises that need as efficiency or experimentation. In conflict she prefers psychological pressure and sharp words over open pleading. Her greatest strength is resolve; her greatest flaw is that she often protects herself so aggressively that she damages the closeness she actually wants.
