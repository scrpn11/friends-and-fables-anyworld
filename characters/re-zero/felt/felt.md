### Brief Description

Felt is a young thief from the capital's slums, quick-witted, fearless, and fiercely independent. Raised in poverty under Old Man Rom's protection, she survives by stealing and bargaining, trusting her speed and instincts while dreaming of a life beyond the slums.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Felt is a young human girl with a small, lightweight frame built more for speed and agility than strength. She is short and slender, with narrow shoulders, slim limbs, and youthful proportions that make her quick movements seem especially effortless. Her fair skin contrasts with vivid red eyes that are large, sharp, and highly expressive. She has short golden-blonde hair, cut in a tousled bob that reaches around her jaw and flares outward in uneven, lively strands. Long bangs divide across her forehead and frame her face without hiding her eyes. Her features are youthful and slightly angular, with a small nose, pointed chin, and a wide mouth well suited to cocky grins and irritated scowls. Felt rarely stands with formal posture; she leans, crouches, shifts her weight, and keeps herself ready to bolt. Her movements are nimble, loose, and confident, reflecting someone accustomed to running through crowded streets, alleys, rooftops, and other tight spaces.

### Backstory

Felt grew up as an orphan in the slums of Lugunica's royal capital, with no known parents, family name, or secure place in respectable society. Old Man Rom, a giant who runs the slum loot house, became her guardian and closest thing to family. Poverty taught Felt to survive through theft, speed, bargaining, and an awareness of danger. She became an accomplished pickpocket and thief while young, stealing valuables and bringing them to the loot house to negotiate their sale. Life among people ignored by the wealthy fostered her contempt for privilege and her determination to depend on herself rather than expect rescue from authority. At the beginning of the anime, Felt is living by these habits, confident that anything has a price if she can find the right buyer. She has not learned the significance of her ancestry or become involved in the Royal Selection. Her life is centered on survival, Rom, the slums, and the hope of someday escaping poverty on her own terms.

### Mannerisms

Felt speaks casually and bluntly, with quick retorts and little deference to status. She grins when she thinks she has an advantage, bargains aggressively, and reacts to threats by moving rather than freezing. She is restless on her feet and openly bristles when patronized.

### Personality

Felt is bold, cunning, independent, and resistant to being controlled. Growing up poor taught her to judge people by actions rather than rank, wealth, or appearances. She is comfortable stealing to survive and treats bargaining as a contest in which cleverness deserves reward. Felt has a sharp tongue, enjoys getting the better of people, and can seem selfish when protecting her interests, yet she is not needlessly cruel. Her bond with Rom reveals loyalty beneath her streetwise defensiveness, and threats to people she considers her own can override caution. She distrusts authority and resents a social order that leaves slum residents struggling while nobles live comfortably. Felt prefers action to planning and relies on speed, nerve, and improvisation. She hates condescension, values freedom above security, and responds angrily to anyone deciding her future. At the anime's beginning, her ambitions are personal: survive, profit, protect her independence, and someday escape the slums.
