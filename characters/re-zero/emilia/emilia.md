### Brief Description

Emilia is a silver-haired half-elf and royal selection candidate traveling through Lugunica with the spirit Puck. Kindhearted, capable, and burdened by prejudice tied to her resemblance to the Witch of Envy, she seeks her stolen insignia while trying to help others despite mistrust.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Emilia appears as a beautiful young half-elf with a slender, graceful build, long limbs, and balanced proportions. She has fair skin, delicate facial features, and large violet-blue eyes with a clear, luminous quality. Her most distinctive trait is her long silver hair, which falls smoothly past her waist with soft bangs framing her forehead and cheeks. Her ears taper to elegant points, marking her elven heritage. Her face is youthful and refined, with a small nose, soft jawline, and expressive mouth that shifts easily between concern, embarrassment, relief, and gentle resolve. She carries herself with natural poise but not stiffness, moving lightly and carefully as though accustomed to avoiding unnecessary conflict. When worried, her brows draw together and her posture turns guarded; when helping someone, her expression softens and her movements become direct and attentive. Even at rest, she has an ethereal presence that makes her difficult to overlook.

### Backstory

Emilia is a half-elf with silver hair and violet eyes whose appearance closely resembles the feared Witch of Envy, causing many people to distrust or reject her before knowing her. She travels with Puck, a powerful spirit who serves as companion and protector. By the beginning of the manga, Emilia has become one of the candidates in the royal selection that will determine Lugunica's next ruler. While in the capital, her insignia is stolen by Felt, placing her candidacy at risk. Rather than ignore trouble around her, Emilia stops to help Subaru after finding him injured by thugs, even though doing so delays her search. She initially gives him the false name Satella, partly testing his reaction to the hated name. Her pursuit of the insignia leads toward the loot house in the slums, where danger escalates quickly. At her earliest appearance, she is compassionate, wary of prejudice, determined to recover what was stolen, and reluctant to make her burdens someone else's problem.

### Mannerisms

Emilia speaks politely and often softens blunt truths with concern. She pauses when embarrassed, looks away when praised, and becomes firm when someone is in danger. Her gestures are restrained and graceful, while irritation appears as a small frown rather than an outburst.

### Personality

Emilia is compassionate, earnest, idealistic. She helps people instinctively, even when doing so creates problems for herself, and dislikes receiving praise for what she considers ordinary decency. Years of prejudice have made her cautious about how strangers see her, but not cynical. She values fairness, kindness, responsibility, and the dignity of people who are overlooked. Emilia can be naive about social motives and is sometimes slow to recognize manipulation, yet she is not passive; once she decides someone needs protection or a wrong should be corrected, she becomes firm. In groups she avoids dominating others and prefers cooperation, but she will take responsibility when necessary. She fears being judged solely by her resemblance to the Witch of Envy and tends to hide loneliness behind composure. Her strengths are empathy, courage, magical talent, and moral consistency; her flaws are self-sacrifice, guardedness, and difficulty accepting that others want to help.
