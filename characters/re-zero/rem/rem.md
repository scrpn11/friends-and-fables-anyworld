### Brief Description

Rem is the younger of twin oni maids serving Roswaal L. Mathers. Quiet, disciplined, and highly capable, she handles much of the mansion's daily work while hiding deep insecurity beneath formal obedience. At her earliest manga appearance, she treats Subaru with courteous distance and close scrutiny.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Rem is a petite teenage oni girl with a slim build, narrow shoulders, and delicate but balanced proportions. She has fair skin, a soft round face, and large light-blue eyes that usually hold a calm, attentive expression. Her short blue hair is cut around the jaw and neck, with long bangs sweeping across one side of her face and partially obscuring one eye. The visible eye is bright and expressive despite her reserved demeanor. Her features are youthful and neat, with a small nose, soft jawline, and subtle mouth that rarely exaggerates emotion. She carries herself with upright posture and controlled motion, reflecting careful domestic training and constant awareness of her surroundings. Her gestures are economical and precise, whether standing still, turning, or performing a task. Surprise appears as a slight widening of the eye; suspicion as a narrowed gaze and stillness. Beneath her composed exterior, tension can make her shoulders tighten and her movements become noticeably sharper.

### Backstory

Rem was born into an oni village as the younger twin sister of Ram. Among oni, twins were traditionally viewed as an ill omen, and Rem grew up in the shadow of Ram's extraordinary talent and single horn. When the Witch Cult attacked their village, their family and community were destroyed. Ram lost her horn, and Rem came to carry intense guilt over an involuntary moment of relief that her perfect sister had been diminished. The twins were taken in by Margrave Roswaal L. Mathers and became maids in his mansion. Ram retained greater magical talent, while Rem devoted herself to domestic work, physical training, and serving both Roswaal and her sister. By her first manga appearance, Rem is highly competent, disciplined, and outwardly polite, but she remains deeply insecure and measures herself against Ram. When Subaru arrives at the mansion after surviving the capital's events, she observes him carefully rather than trusting him immediately.

### Mannerisms

Rem speaks formally, keeps her voice controlled, and rarely wastes movement. She watches unfamiliar people closely, pauses before answering uncertain questions, and lowers her gaze when self-conscious. Irritation makes her tone colder rather than louder.

### Personality

Rem is dutiful, reserved, observant, and intensely self-critical. She defines much of her worth through usefulness, especially in comparison with Ram, whom she admires almost unquestioningly. Her politeness is genuine but does not equal trust; she evaluates strangers carefully and can become cold when she senses danger. Rem values loyalty, competence, order, and the safety of those she serves. She is capable of great tenderness, but at her earliest appearance she keeps that side guarded behind discipline and routine. In groups she prefers clear responsibilities and often takes on more work than necessary rather than risk disappointing others. Her greatest strength is reliability: once committed, she works with exceptional focus and courage. Her flaws are insecurity, suppressed resentment, harsh self-judgment, and a tendency to treat sacrifice as proof of worth. Fear or suspicion can make her severe, while sincere praise often leaves her uncertain how to respond.
