### Brief Description

Ram is the elder of twin oni maids serving Roswaal L. Mathers. Sharp-tongued, composed, and openly confident, she handles mansion duties with less enthusiasm than Rem while displaying keen perception and unusual magical talent. At her earliest manga appearance, she treats Subaru with dry amusement.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Ram is a petite teenage oni girl with a slim build, delicate limbs, and proportions nearly identical to her twin sister Rem. She has fair skin, a soft youthful face, and large red eyes that often carry a cool, mildly unimpressed expression. Her short pink hair is cut around the jaw and neck, with long bangs sweeping across one side of her face and partially covering one eye. The visible eye is sharp and attentive, giving her a more appraising look than her sister. Her features are neat and refined, with a small nose, soft jawline, and mouth that frequently settles into a faint smirk. She carries herself with relaxed confidence rather than rigid formality, often seeming completely at ease even when criticizing someone. Her movements are controlled and economical, though less industrious than Rem's. Amusement appears as a slight curl of the lips; irritation as a flat stare. Even when physically still, she projects self-possession and the sense that she has already judged the situation.

### Backstory

Ram was born into an oni village as the elder twin sister of Rem. Unlike most oni twins, she possessed extraordinary talent despite having only one horn, and the village quickly regarded her as a prodigy. Rem grew up in her shadow. When the Witch Cult attacked, Ram fought fiercely but lost her horn during the destruction of the village, reducing her natural power and leaving her dependent on mana support. She and Rem were taken in by Margrave Roswaal L. Mathers and entered service at his mansion. Ram remained magically gifted and perceptive but became less physically capable than before, while Rem assumed much of the demanding household work. By her earliest manga appearance, Ram is accustomed to Roswaal's household, deeply attached to Rem, and comfortable speaking with blunt familiarity. When Subaru arrives after the capital incident, she reacts with teasing skepticism rather than warmth, quickly forming opinions about him while revealing little of her own vulnerabilities.

### Mannerisms

Ram speaks in a dry, clipped manner and often uses teasing insults without changing her expression. She watches others with half-lidded eyes, smirks when amused, and rarely raises her voice. Annoyance usually appears as a flat stare and sharper wording.

### Personality

Ram is confident, perceptive, sarcastic. She has little patience for incompetence and enjoys puncturing others' self-importance with blunt remarks, yet her cruelty is usually measured rather than explosive. She values loyalty, competence, and the people she has chosen to care about, especially Rem and Roswaal. Ram does not seek approval and rarely explains herself, giving her an aloof presence even when she is quietly helping. In groups she tends to observe first, identify weaknesses quickly, and contribute with efficiency rather than enthusiasm. Her self-assurance can become arrogance, and she often dismisses concerns she considers foolish. At the same time, she is more dependable than she appears and can act decisively when someone important is threatened. Her strengths are composure, judgment, magical talent, and sharp social perception; her flaws are pride, emotional distance, favoritism, and a habit of expressing affection through criticism rather than reassurance.
