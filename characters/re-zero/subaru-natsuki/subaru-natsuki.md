### Brief Description

Subaru Natsuki is a brash, ordinary Japanese teenager abruptly transported to the Kingdom of Lugunica. Lacking combat talent but armed with stubborn resolve and the hidden Return by Death ability, he becomes determined to repay Emilia's kindness and survive a world he barely understands.

**Character Type:** Playable

### Appearance

Subaru is a teenage Japanese boy of average height with a lean, lightly athletic build and ordinary proportions. He has short, spiky black hair with uneven bangs and a few locks jutting outward around the crown. His eyes are dark and sharp, with a distinctive sanpaku look that can make him seem more intense or confrontational than he intends. His face is youthful but angular, with strong brows, a straight nose, and an expressive mouth that shifts quickly between confidence, panic, irritation, and embarrassment. His skin is lightly tanned. He tends to stand with restless energy, often leaning forward when excited or challenging someone. His gestures are broad and animated, especially when talking himself up or trying to force optimism into a bad situation. When frightened or exhausted, his posture collapses noticeably, but determination returns as a squared stance and direct stare. Even at rest, his face remains alert and easy to read.

### Backstory

Subaru lived in modern Japan as a withdrawn teenager who had stopped attending school and spent much of his time at home. One evening, after leaving a convenience store, he was suddenly transported without explanation to the royal capital of Lugunica. Expecting the situation to resemble the fantasy stories he knew, he quickly discovered that he had no obvious special power, money, allies, or knowledge of the world. After being attacked by thugs, he was helped by a silver-haired half-elf who introduced herself as Satella and was searching for a stolen insignia. Grateful and fascinated, Subaru chose to help her recover it. Their search led him into danger and his first death. He then discovered that dying returns him to an earlier point in time while preserving his memories, an ability he cannot explain to others. At the manga's beginning, he is frightened but determined to use each restart to save the girl who first showed him kindness.

### Mannerisms

Subaru talks quickly, jokes under pressure, exaggerates confidence, and often turns panic into loud bravado. He gestures broadly, points when arguing, and grins when trying to reassure others. Fear makes him ramble; resolve makes his speech shorter and more direct.

### Personality

Subaru is impulsive, stubborn, theatrical, and desperate to prove that he matters. He uses humor, bravado, and genre-savvy assumptions to hide insecurity and fear. He forms attachments quickly and risks himself for people who show him kindness. His greatest strength is persistence: pain, humiliation, and failure can break his composure, but rarely his willingness to try again. He is observant in bursts rather than consistently cautious, often understanding emotions only after acting recklessly. Subaru craves recognition and can become self-centered when he believes effort should earn gratitude, yet he is capable of genuine empathy and sacrifice. In groups he talks like a leader before earning authority, then adapts when reality proves him wrong. He hates helplessness, fears abandonment and meaningless failure, and responds to crisis by searching for any action that might save someone. His flaws are pride, impatience, volatility, and pushing himself beyond reason.
