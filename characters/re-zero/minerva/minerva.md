### Brief Description

Minerva is the Witch of Wrath, a compassionate but dangerously shortsighted healer whose rage is directed at suffering itself. She cannot tolerate seeing people hurt, acts immediately to save whoever is before her, and exists in the present era only as a preserved soul within Echidna's Castle of Dreams.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Minerva appears to be in her late teens and stands about five feet tall, with a petite frame and a compact build. Her face is youthful and expressive, shaped by large, slightly slanted sky-blue eyes pale enough to seem almost translucent. She has long, gleaming golden-blonde hair gathered into a loose, messy ponytail on the left side of her head, with stray strands brushing her cheek and framing her face. Her complexion is fair and smooth, and her features are soft rather than severe despite the intensity of her expressions. She carries herself with restless energy, often leaning forward as though ready to charge toward whatever has caught her attention. Her emotions show openly: anger tightens her brows and jaw, distress brings tears quickly, and concern can transform her whole face in an instant. Even when still, she gives the impression of barely contained motion, with quick shifts of posture and abrupt, forceful gestures that make her seem physically direct and difficult to ignore.

### Backstory

Minerva lived in an age scarred by relentless conflict. The suffering around her enraged and devastated her, and she eventually concluded that crying alone would change nothing. She trained herself to become strong enough to intervene directly, but even force could not end the cycle of violence. After absorbing the Witch Factor of Wrath, she awakened an Authority that let her heal injuries by striking the wounded. From then on, she traveled wherever she found people suffering and healed them without hesitation. The power carried catastrophic consequences elsewhere in the world, yet Minerva refused to prioritize distant harm over the pain directly in front of her. Nations tried to capture or stop her, but failed. Her compassion made her especially vulnerable to traps involving innocents, and she was ultimately lured in by using elves as bait. She died more than four centuries ago, after which Echidna preserved her soul in the Castle of Dreams.

### Mannerisms

Minerva speaks loudly when upset, scolds people for endangering themselves, and often expresses concern through abrupt physical action rather than careful reassurance. She cries easily while insisting she is angry, leans forward when arguing, and reacts instantly to visible pain.

### Personality

Minerva is compassionate, emotional, impulsive, and deeply sincere. She hates conflict and suffering, and when someone is hurt in front of her she acts immediately, without much patience for caution or long-term calculation. Her anger is usually rooted in grief or empathy, though she interprets many of her own tears as rage. Minerva's concern for suffering is genuine and immediate; she does not ignore pain because it is inconvenient. Her greatest flaw is the narrowness of that concern. She focuses intensely on what she can see and dismisses consequences that are distant, abstract, or outside her view, even when the consequences are severe. She is also notably naive and easy to deceive, especially when innocents appear to be in danger. She is not cruel, calculating, or detached; she is driven by emotion, acts first, and often trusts the urgency of her feelings more than strategy or foresight.
