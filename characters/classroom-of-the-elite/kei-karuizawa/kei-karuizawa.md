### Brief Description

Kei Karuizawa is a socially prominent Class D student who presents herself as confident, fashionable, and secure within the class hierarchy. Beneath that image lies a strong need for protection and belonging, making reputation, alliances, and social positioning unusually important to how she navigates school life.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Kei is a petite, slender young woman with a lively, approachable face and an expressive presence that draws social attention. She has light blonde hair framing her face and shoulders, warm brown eyes, fair skin, and soft features. Her body language is more animated than that of quieter classmates: she shifts her weight casually, reacts quickly with her face, and turns toward friends with open posture. Her smiles can be bright and effortless, while irritation or embarrassment appears just as quickly through pouts, narrowed eyes, or abrupt changes in expression. She moves with the ease of someone accustomed to being observed by peers. Around people she trusts, her posture loosens and her gestures become more spontaneous; when she feels socially threatened, tension appears in her shoulders and gaze even if she tries to maintain confidence. Her overall impression is outgoing, attractive, and socially comfortable, though careful observation can reveal guardedness beneath the surface.

### Backstory

Before entering the Advanced Nurturing School, Kei endured bullying that taught her that social isolation could be dangerous. She adapted by learning how status, popularity, and protective relationships can shield a vulnerable person from becoming an easy target. At the start of the anime, she has carried those lessons into Class D and positions herself near the center of the girls' social hierarchy. Her public confidence is therefore not merely vanity; it functions as armor. She associates closely with popular classmates and values appearing secure, connected, and difficult to isolate. Her past is not known to most people around her, and she has no desire to expose it. Her circumstances are relatively stable because her social position provides protection, but that stability depends on maintaining reputation and belonging. She has not yet formed her later private alliance with Ayanokōji or revealed the vulnerability beneath her public persona.

### Mannerisms

Kei speaks casually and expressively, often matching the energy of the group around her. She smiles easily, reacts with quick changes in tone, and uses confident body language in public. When insecure, she becomes sharper, more defensive, and more attentive to who is watching.

### Personality

Kei is socially perceptive, adaptable, expressive, and strongly motivated by security. She understands group dynamics instinctively and values reputation, belonging, and the protection that comes from being connected to influential peers. Publicly she can seem confident, assertive, and occasionally dismissive, especially when maintaining status requires taking a side. Privately she is far more sensitive to rejection and humiliation than she wants anyone to know. Her fear of isolation can push her toward conformity or defensive behavior, but it also makes her attentive to shifts in social atmosphere. She is not a mastermind, yet she possesses practical social intelligence and knows how information moves through peer groups. In play, she should protect her standing, seek reliable allies, react strongly to threats against her reputation, and reveal genuine vulnerability only when she feels unusually safe. She can be kind and loyal, but security usually comes before abstract ideals.
