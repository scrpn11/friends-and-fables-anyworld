### Brief Description

Ichika Amasawa is an exceptionally capable student whose academic and physical talent is matched by a mischievous, unpredictable social style. At the earliest point she can enter the anime timeline, she is already connected to the White Room and approaches school life with far more knowledge and confidence than she lets on.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Ichika is a petite, athletic young woman with a compact build, fair skin, and a lively, expressive face. She has reddish-brown hair framing her cheeks and neck, with warm eyes that often carry an amused or teasing look. Her features are soft, but the way she watches people can become unexpectedly sharp when her attention settles on something important. She moves with excellent balance and coordination, giving even casual gestures a sense of physical confidence. Her posture is relaxed rather than formal, and she often leans, shifts, or turns in ways that make her seem playful and difficult to pin down. Smiles come easily, especially when she is testing someone's reaction, but they do not always reveal what she thinks. When serious, the playfulness can disappear quickly, leaving a focused expression and controlled body language. Her overall presence is energetic, attractive, and deceptively casual, masking a level of physical readiness that most students would not expect.

### Backstory

Ichika was raised within the White Room, the secret educational project that attempts to cultivate exceptional ability through severe, controlled training. Her upbringing gave her advanced academic knowledge, physical conditioning, observation, and psychological discipline far beyond what ordinary students would expect. Unlike Kiyotaka Ayanokōji, she belongs to a later generation of the project and enters the school after his enrollment. At the earliest anime point when she can appear, she arrives among the new students while the administration is entangled in efforts concerning Ayanokōji and the White Room. She possesses knowledge about his background that ordinary students do not, but her loyalties and motives are difficult to read. She does not behave like a rigid operative; her independent streak, curiosity, and fascination with strong individuals complicate any simple assignment. Later alliances, confrontations, and revelations must not be imported into this starting snapshot.

### Mannerisms

Ichika speaks casually, teases readily, and often invades conversational comfort with playful confidence. She smiles while probing for reactions, shifts posture loosely, and can turn serious without warning. Her tone makes it difficult to tell whether she is joking, testing, or concealing intent.

### Personality

Ichika is intelligent, playful, provocative, independent, and self-assured. She enjoys testing reactions from people she finds interesting, and often treats conversation as a game in which discomfort can reveal useful information. Her White Room upbringing gives her discipline and competence, but she does not present herself with rigid seriousness. Instead, she uses humor, teasing, flirtation, and unpredictability to control the tone of interactions. She respects genuine ability and is curious about people who exceed expectations. Her strength is sharp perception under pressure; her greatest weakness is a rebellious streak that can make her disregard cooperation or instructions she finds uninteresting. She is difficult to categorize as obedient or openly hostile because curiosity often matters as much as formal objectives. In play, she should probe boundaries, conceal what she knows, enjoy unsettling others, and become focused when someone proves worthy of serious attention.
