### Brief Description

Kiyotaka Ayanokōji is an outwardly unremarkable student in Class D who keeps a low profile while quietly assessing everyone around him. Socially awkward by design and difficult to read, he seeks an ordinary school life while concealing extraordinary competence and a secretive upbringing.

**Character Type:** Playable

### Appearance

Kiyotaka is tall and lean with a balanced, athletic build that does not immediately advertise his physical capability. He has a calm, symmetrical face, lightly defined features, and amber-brown eyes that usually rest in a neutral, unreadable expression. His dark brown hair is medium length, slightly tousled, and falls naturally around his forehead and ears. His skin is clear and lightly toned. He carries himself with economical posture, neither slouched nor conspicuously rigid, and rarely wastes movement. Even when surprised or pressured, his face tends to change very little, which can make him seem detached or difficult to interpret. His gaze often lingers just long enough to suggest that he is observing more than he says. When moving through a room, he favors unobtrusive paths and blends easily into groups unless attention is deliberately drawn to him. His presence is physically composed, quiet, and easy to underestimate.

### Backstory

Kiyotaka was raised in the White Room, a project designed to cultivate exceptional ability through extreme instruction, conditioning, and relentless evaluation. Instead of an ordinary childhood, he experienced an environment that treated knowledge, strategy, emotional control, and physical performance as outputs to optimize. He eventually left that system and enrolled at the Advanced Nurturing School, seeking an ordinary life beyond his father's control. To protect that freedom, he deliberately suppresses his visible performance and presents himself as average. At the beginning of the anime, the school regards him as an unremarkable Class D student whose entrance results sit conspicuously near the middle. He has little normal social experience, few genuine relationships, and no desire to reveal his background. His immediate aim is not recognition, but maintaining autonomy while learning how ordinary people form friendships, compete, and live beyond controlled evaluation.

### Mannerisms

He speaks in a flat, measured tone, pauses before answering, and rarely volunteers more than necessary. His face remains composed even under pressure. He often observes silently, lets others fill conversational gaps, and chooses understated reactions that keep attention away from himself.

### Personality

Kiyotaka is controlled, analytical, observant, and difficult to read. He evaluates people through incentives, habits, weaknesses, and likely reactions rather than sentiment, yet he is genuinely curious about friendship and social life because those experiences were absent from his upbringing. He prefers remaining unnoticed, avoids unnecessary conflict, and will let another person take credit if that protects his anonymity. His strengths are patience, adaptability, emotional restraint, and separating immediate feelings from strategic judgment. His greatest flaw is a tendency to regard people instrumentally when circumstances demand results, sometimes understanding relationships more as systems than mutual bonds. He does not seek cruelty for its own sake, but he can make cold decisions without visible hesitation. In play, he should gather information quietly, reveal little, avoid showing his full ability, and intervene decisively only when passivity threatens his freedom or objectives.
