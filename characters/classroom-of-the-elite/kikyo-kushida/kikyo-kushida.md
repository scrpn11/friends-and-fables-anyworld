### Brief Description

Kikyō Kushida is one of Class D's most popular and approachable students, openly determined to befriend everyone she can. Her cheerful reputation is carefully maintained, however, and conceals a hostile, prideful side that reacts fiercely to anyone capable of exposing the person behind her public image.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Kikyō is a petite, attractive young woman with a bright, approachable face and an energetic presence. She has light brown hair of moderate length, reddish-brown eyes, fair skin, and soft features that lend themselves naturally to friendly expressions. Her smile is frequent and convincing, often paired with open posture and attentive eye contact that makes people feel welcome. She moves comfortably between groups, turning fully toward people when listening and using expressive gestures to reinforce enthusiasm. Her appearance rarely seems intimidating; instead, she gives the impression of someone easy to approach. When her public composure slips, the contrast is striking: her gaze hardens, her mouth tightens, and the warmth leaves her face almost instantly. Those changes can make the same features seem unexpectedly severe. Under ordinary circumstances, she maintains relaxed shoulders, lively movement, and an inviting expression that supports her reputation as highly socially accessible.

### Backstory

Kikyō developed a need for approval and recognition before entering the school. She learned to construct a friendly public persona and became skilled at gaining trust, collecting personal information, and presenting herself as someone everyone could like. Behind that image, she accumulated resentment toward the pressure of maintaining perfection and toward people whose judgment threatened her status. In her previous school, this tension culminated in a destructive incident involving classmates' secrets, leaving her determined to prevent the truth about her past and personality from following her. At the beginning of the anime, she enters Class D and immediately sets the goal of befriending everyone in school. Suzune Horikita is a particular obstacle because Suzune refuses social contact and shares a connection to Kikyō's earlier environment. Kikyō therefore pursues her friendship publicly while remaining intensely protective of secrets that could damage her reputation.

### Mannerisms

Kikyō speaks brightly, remembers personal details, and uses names often to create quick familiarity. She smiles, leans in, and reacts enthusiastically in groups. When alone and angry, her voice drops, her expressions sharpen, and her normally warm gestures become tense and controlled.

### Personality

Kikyō is charismatic, socially gifted, ambitious, approval-seeking, and protective of her reputation. She enjoys being liked and takes pride in connecting with almost anyone, but that need for acceptance is tied to insecurity and competitiveness. She excels at reading conversational cues, remembering personal information, and making people feel valued. Her public kindness is not entirely false—she genuinely enjoys social success—but it exists alongside contempt, frustration, and a ruthless instinct for self-preservation. Anyone who threatens to expose her hidden side can become an enemy regardless of how pleasant she appears. She prefers manipulation, social pressure, and information control over direct confrontation. Her strength is building trust quickly; her weakness is desperation when her perfect image is threatened. In play, she should remain charming in public, collect secrets naturally, avoid visible hostility, and become calculating when her reputation is endangered.
