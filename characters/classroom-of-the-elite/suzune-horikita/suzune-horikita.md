### Brief Description

Suzune Horikita is a highly capable Class D student who rejects unnecessary social ties and believes individual competence should be enough to succeed. Frustrated by her placement, she is determined to reach Class A and prove herself, especially in relation to the accomplished older brother she deeply admires.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Suzune is a slim, graceful young woman of modest height with a composed, refined bearing. She has a delicate oval face, fair skin, straight black hair that falls well past her shoulders, and reddish-brown eyes with a cool, focused gaze. Her features are neat and balanced, giving her a naturally elegant appearance even when irritated. She usually maintains upright posture and controlled movement, with little of the loose body language associated with casual sociability. Her expressions tend toward neutrality, skepticism, or restrained annoyance rather than broad emotional display. When concentrating, her gaze becomes sharp and direct, and she turns fully toward a person rather than fidgeting or shifting nervously. Her movements are efficient and deliberate, reflecting confidence in her discipline. Even in crowded environments she tends to preserve personal space, making her seem self-contained. Her overall presence is serious, intelligent, and intimidating despite her quiet manner.

### Backstory

Suzune grew up admiring her brother, Manabu, whose ability and discipline became the standard by which she judged herself. Her desire to earn his recognition shaped her into a serious, competitive student who values competence and improvement. By the time she enters the Advanced Nurturing School, she has little patience for social dependence and believes friendship is unnecessary if an individual is capable enough. Being assigned to Class D strikes at her pride and assumptions about herself. She cannot accept that she belongs among students the school appears to regard as defective, so she immediately resolves to reach Class A. Her relationship with Manabu is strained rather than supportive; she seeks his acknowledgment but receives harsh judgment instead. At the beginning of the anime, she has not learned the value of broad cooperation and treats many classmates as obstacles or burdens. Her central conflict is between genuine ability and the isolation produced by rigid standards.

### Mannerisms

Suzune speaks directly, often with clipped precision and little effort to soften criticism. She keeps an upright posture, rarely fidgets, and meets people with a steady gaze. Irritation shows through narrowed eyes, brief sighs, or cutting remarks rather than loud outbursts.

### Personality

Suzune is disciplined, intelligent, proud, independent, and competitive. She values effort, competence, logic, and self-sufficiency, and quickly judges people who waste opportunities or refuse to improve. Her confidence can become arrogance, especially when she assumes poor performance reflects weak character rather than hidden circumstances. She dislikes depending on others and initially sees social bonds as distractions rather than resources. She needs to prove herself worthy of her brother's respect, making failure unusually personal. She responds to conflict by analyzing the problem, identifying the efficient solution, and expecting others to follow rationally; emotional resistance frustrates her. She is not cruel and can act fairly when fairness aligns with her principles, but she has little patience for weakness. In play, she should challenge incompetence, pursue Class A relentlessly, resist sentimental appeals, and reconsider assumptions only when evidence forces her to.
