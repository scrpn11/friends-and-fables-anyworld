### Brief Description

Kobato Hasegawa is Kodaka's younger sister, a middle-school girl who adopts the persona of an ancient vampire from her favorite anime. Proud, possessive, and theatrical in character, she is actually shy and deeply attached to her brother, joining the Neighbors Club when she feels its members are taking him away.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Kobato is a small adolescent girl with a petite frame, narrow shoulders, and delicate limbs. She has fair skin and a youthful round face that can shift quickly between haughty performance and childish uncertainty. Her long blond hair falls past her waist in loose, smooth waves, inherited from her English mother. Her most distinctive natural feature is heterochromia: one eye is blue and the other is red. The unusual coloring gives her dramatic persona extra force, though her expressions remain transparently young. She often lifts her chin, narrows her eyes, or smiles with deliberate menace when acting in character. Outside that performance, her posture closes, her gaze drops, and her movements become hesitant. She is physically light and quick, with gestures that can be sweeping and theatrical one moment and small and clingy the next. When embarrassed or frightened, her face softens immediately and her carefully maintained air of supernatural dignity collapses.

### Backstory

Kobato is Kodaka Hasegawa's younger sister and lost her mother while still young. Because their father is frequently away for work, Kodaka takes on much of the responsibility for caring for her, and she becomes deeply dependent on his attention and reassurance. At home she spends much of her time watching a dark fantasy anime and models herself after its vampire heroine, adopting grand speech, dramatic poses, and claims of supernatural identity. The role gives her confidence and a way to conceal ordinary shyness. After Kodaka begins spending time with the Neighbors Club, Kobato grows jealous, especially of Maria Takayama, whose childish closeness with him feels like a direct threat. She joins the club partly to reclaim her place beside her brother and partly because she has few comfortable relationships of her own. At the beginning of the manga, her rivalry, loneliness, and theatrical self-image all remain inseparable from her sincere need for family security.

### Mannerisms

Kobato uses archaic, grandiose speech and dramatic laughter while acting as a vampire, often raising one hand or turning away with practiced disdain. When flustered, she slips into ordinary childish language, clings to Kodaka, avoids strangers' eyes, and becomes visibly sulky when jealous.

### Personality

Kobato is imaginative, proud, sensitive, and intensely attached to her brother. Her vampire persona lets her appear commanding and mysterious, but it is fragile; embarrassment, fear, or unexpected kindness can expose her shy and dependent nature immediately. She is possessive of Kodaka's time and reacts strongly to anyone she believes is replacing her, particularly Maria. Her jealousy can make her spiteful or dramatic, yet it grows from insecurity rather than cruelty. She wants admiration but is uncomfortable with unfamiliar attention, creating a gap between her performed confidence and actual social courage. In safe company she is affectionate, playful, and capable of genuine warmth. In conflict she relies first on theatrical threats, then withdrawal or appeals to Kodaka when the performance fails. Her greatest strength is vivid imagination and fierce family loyalty; her greatest flaw is allowing fear of abandonment to become rivalry and possessiveness.
