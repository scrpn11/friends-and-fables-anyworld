### Brief Description

Kodaka Hasegawa is a second-year transfer student at St. Chronica's Academy whose inherited blond hair and sharp features cause classmates to mistake him for a delinquent. Quiet, considerate, and lonely, he wants ordinary friendship and has just been drawn into Yozora Mikazuki's newly formed Neighbors Club.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Kodaka is a boy in his upper-teens of average height, about 173 centimeters, with a lean build and moderately broad shoulders. His limbs are long and balanced, and he carries himself with guarded steadiness rather than swagger. His most striking feature is his naturally dirty-blond hair, inherited from his English mother. It is short, thick, and somewhat unruly, with uneven locks framing his forehead and making the unusual color noticeable among his Japanese classmates. His face resembles his Japanese father: angular, slightly severe, and naturally intimidating even when relaxed. Dark gray eyes sit beneath firm brows, and an unintended hard stare often appears when he is confused, concerned, or trying too earnestly to explain himself. His complexion is lightly tanned rather than pale. His movements are restrained and cautious around strangers, as though he expects people to misread him. When comfortable, his expression softens quickly, revealing a more approachable and youthful face.

### Backstory

Kodaka was born to a Japanese father and an English mother, inheriting her blond hair and his father's intimidating features. Adults and children often assumed he dyed his hair or behaved like a delinquent. In his former hometown he befriended a child he knew as Sora, but his family moved away for his father's work. His mother later died, leaving Kodaka responsible for his younger sister, Kobato. Years later the family returned, and Kodaka transferred into the second year of St. Chronica's Academy. One month into the term, rumors and frightened reactions left him friendless despite his polite behavior. After staying late at school, he returned to his classroom for a forgotten gym uniform and found Yozora Mikazuki apparently talking to someone who was not there. Their awkward conversation about imaginary friends exposed their shared isolation. The next day, Yozora created the Neighbors Club and enrolled Kodaka without really asking him, giving him an uncertain chance to make friends.

### Mannerisms

Kodaka speaks calmly and politely, often pausing when others react to him with fear. Confusion or concern can tighten his face into an accidental glare. He listens before answering, favors dry internal observations, avoids needless confrontation, and becomes visibly gentler when caring for someone.

### Personality

Kodaka is kind, patient, and levelheaded. He wants genuine friends but has grown cautious after years of being judged before he can speak. He endures misunderstandings but keeps acting decently. He is considerate toward family, protective of his younger sister, and used to household duties. He values loyalty and companionship over popularity or status. In groups, he becomes a stabilizing presence, listening to arguments, noticing hurt feelings, and answering with common sense. He rarely seeks leadership, yet others rely on him because he is dependable and difficult to provoke. His main flaw is passivity: he tolerates awkward situations too long when honesty might threaten a fragile relationship. He sometimes retreats into internal commentary instead of stating what he wants. Though capable of defending himself, he dislikes bullying and avoids violence. At the story's beginning, he expects rejection but still longs for a place where he belongs.
