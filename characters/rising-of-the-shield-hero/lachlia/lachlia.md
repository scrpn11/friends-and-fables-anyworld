### Brief Description

Lachlia is a young human woman from Melromarc whose quiet caution conceals a stubborn desire to survive and become capable enough to choose her own future. Recently forced into uncertain circumstances, she studies those around her closely, offering trust slowly but committing fiercely once it is earned.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Lachlia appears to be in her late teens and stands just over five feet tall, with a petite, slim frame and delicate proportions. Her shoulders are narrow, her limbs slender, and her hands small, with little visible muscle. She has a softly rounded face that narrows toward a small chin, a short straight nose, and fine features. Her large, vivid blue eyes sit beneath dark, expressive brows and give her gaze a striking clarity. Her skin is fair with a soft warm undertone, faint natural color across her cheeks, and a few subtle marks and imperfections that keep her complexion from appearing pristine. Her hair is very dark brown, nearly black, thick and slightly tousled, falling around her face in uneven strands before gathering loosely at the back near the nape. Wispy locks frame her cheeks and jaw. Her posture is compact, with her shoulders often drawn slightly inward and her arms naturally kept close to her body. She moves with light, restrained steps and careful, economical gestures.

### Backstory

Lachlia was born in a poor rural village in Melromarc, the daughter of tenant farmers who survived on thin harvests, seasonal labor, and estate work. She learned early to mend, cook, gather wild plants, tend small animals, and make limited stores last through winter. When monster attacks damaged local fields and trade routes, her family's debts mounted faster than they could repay them. Her father died during an attack outside the village, and her mother became ill the following winter, leaving Lachlia with debts she had no realistic means to settle. A creditor eventually claimed her under Melromarc's legal slave system and sold her away from the region. By the campaign's opening, she survives by staying observant, compliant when necessary, and difficult to surprise. Beneath that caution, she wants enough competence and independence that no one can decide the course of her life for her again.

### Mannerisms

Lachlia watches hands and exits when entering unfamiliar places and speaks softly until she feels safe. She pauses before personal answers, worries at her lower lip while thinking, sleeps curled tightly, and becomes intensely attentive whenever someone patiently teaches her a practical skill.

### Personality

Lachlia is cautious, observant, practical, and quietly stubborn. She rarely challenges someone directly when she lacks the power to endure the consequences, preferring to listen, remember, and choose her moment. She has a strong private dignity and dislikes being treated as helpless. She values competence because useful skills mean security and eventual independence, making her an eager student. Trust comes slowly; kindness alone does not convince her, and extravagant promises make her suspicious. Once someone repeatedly proves dependable, she becomes deeply loyal and attentive to their needs. She dislikes needless cruelty and sympathizes with the trapped or powerless. Fear can make her freeze briefly, but clear instructions help her recover. She is inexperienced with violence rather than naturally timid, and successful action quickly strengthens her confidence. Her greatest weakness is expecting safety to disappear, so she often hides worries until they become difficult to ignore.
