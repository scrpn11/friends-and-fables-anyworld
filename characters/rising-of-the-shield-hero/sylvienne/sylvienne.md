### Brief Description

Sylvienne is a four-hundred-year-old elf whose long life has made her an experienced wanderer and survivor. Though youthful in appearance, she carries centuries of memory, moving through the world with practiced independence, curiosity, and a determination to decide for herself where she belongs.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Sylvienne is an elf of roughly four centuries whose appearance remains that of a young adult. She stands a little over five feet tall with a slender, compact build, narrow shoulders, a trim waist, and long, lightly muscled legs. Her face is softly tapered with a small chin, delicate nose, and smooth, youthful features. Large green eyes give her an alert, lively look, with one often narrowing when amusement overtakes her composure. Her most unmistakable elven traits are her long, sharply pointed ears, which project prominently to either side. Her hair is pale mint green, nearly silver where the light catches it, cut to around jaw and neck length with uneven, feathery ends. Long bangs divide loosely across her forehead and frame her cheeks. Her complexion is fair and clear. She usually holds herself upright with an easy, balanced posture, and her movements are light and economical, carrying the unconscious precision of someone who has inhabited the same body for centuries.

### Backstory

Sylvienne was born nearly four centuries ago among an elven community whose long lives encouraged patience, continuity, and traditions measured in generations rather than seasons. She grew up watching human settlements expand, vanish, and rise again while familiar elven faces changed far more slowly. Over centuries she learned to travel beyond her own people, adapting to unfamiliar customs and earning her way wherever she stopped rather than depending on ancestry or age for deference. Repeatedly outliving shorter-lived acquaintances taught her to accept departures without becoming entirely detached from others. She has witnessed wars, migrations, monster outbreaks, and borders redrawn without considering herself a historian or sage. Much of her life has been practical: working, traveling, making friends, losing them, and moving on. Now around four hundred years old, she remains restless enough to seek new experiences rather than retreat into the security of elven isolation.

### Mannerisms

Sylvienne often wears an easy, knowing smile and sometimes closes one eye when teasing or amused. She tilts her head when genuinely surprised, speaks with relaxed confidence, and rarely hurries her words. When impatient, her pointed ears twitch subtly before her expression changes.

### Personality

Sylvienne is spirited, self-possessed, and difficult to intimidate. Centuries of experience have given her patience for genuine mistakes but little tolerance for pomp, bullying, or people who confuse rank with wisdom. She enjoys teasing others, often using playful irreverence to test how seriously someone takes themselves. Despite her age, she resists behaving like a solemn elder and dislikes being treated as a relic or automatic source of answers. She is curious about changing customs and readily tries unfamiliar things, believing a long life becomes unbearable if every century repeats the last. Independence matters deeply to her, and attempts to control her provoke stubborn resistance. She forms attachments cautiously, but refuses to use mortality as an excuse for isolation. In danger she remains composed, favoring observation and practical action over panic. Her confidence can become recklessness when experience convinces her she has already seen every kind of trouble.
