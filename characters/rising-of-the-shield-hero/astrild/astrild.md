### Brief Description

Astrild is a twenty-four-year-old naga demi-human who has spent nearly her entire life isolated in the forest ruins she calls home. Wary of outsiders and fiercely self-reliant, she survives through instinct, patience, and an intimate familiarity with the wilderness around her temple.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Astrild appears to be a human woman in her early twenties from the waist up, but her lower body is that of a massive serpent. She is tall and slender through the torso, with narrow shoulders, a long neck, fine-boned features, and pale skin so light it appears almost white. Her face is oval with a sharp chin, straight nose, and deep vibrant green eyes set beneath dark brows. Each eye has a vertical serpentine pupil. Long, straight black hair falls past her waist in a smooth curtain, framing her face and upper body. Small pointed fangs are visible when she parts her lips, and her tongue is forked. Below the waist, her body transitions seamlessly into a powerful serpentine tail covered in smooth scales. Her movements alternate between eerie stillness and fluid, silent motion, with her upper body remaining poised while the coils beneath her shift with controlled strength. Her long tail gives her an imposing silhouette, yet every shift of her coils remains precise and fluid.

### Backstory

Astrild has lived in the wilderness for as long as she can remember, making her home among the ruins of an old temple deep within the forest. The decaying structure became both shelter and territory, and she learned its chambers, surrounding trails, water sources, hiding places, and dangers through years of solitude. With no community to rely on, she became accustomed to providing for herself and treating strangers as potential threats until proven otherwise. Her life has been shaped more by isolation than by formal society: hunting, avoiding monsters, reading the forest, and protecting the boundaries of the ruins became ordinary routines. She knows little of cities beyond what travelers and scavengers have revealed, and she has little reason to trust the laws or customs of distant settlements. By the campaign's opening, the temple remains the center of her identity and the only place she considers unquestionably hers.

### Mannerisms

Astrild often becomes completely still when listening, with only her eyes moving. Her tongue flicks briefly when assessing unfamiliar scents, and her coils tighten when irritated. She speaks carefully, rarely wastes words, and watches strangers for several seconds before answering them.

### Personality

Astrild is reserved, territorial, perceptive, and fiercely independent. Years of isolation have made her comfortable with silence and deeply suspicious of anyone who enters her space without permission. She does not seek conflict for its own sake, but she responds quickly when she believes she is being cornered, threatened, or deceived. Patience is one of her strongest traits; she is willing to watch, wait, and let another person reveal their intentions before committing herself. She values honesty, self-sufficiency, and respect for boundaries, while authority based only on rank means little to her. Her social instincts are underdeveloped rather than absent, and bluntness can make her seem colder than she intends. Once she decides someone is safe, her caution softens into quiet curiosity and dry humor. Her greatest weakness is that she expects dependence to become vulnerability, so she resists asking for help even when cooperation would clearly benefit her.
