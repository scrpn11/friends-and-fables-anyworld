# Gilded Aviary Estate Reference

## Overview

The **Gilded Aviary** is Duke Aranis's primary residence and the seat of his authority within the district. He also owns **three neighboring estates** and **several lucrative trade warehouses at the docks**.

Duke Aranis's liquid assets are held in the **estate vault** and **three separate banking houses in the capital**.

His Crown-granted authority includes jurisdiction over:

- Local law enforcement
- Tax collection
- Land disputes within the province

## Succession Requirements

To make Kelabren his heir, Duke Aranis must:

1. Draft a formal decree of adoption and succession.
2. Have the decree witnessed by his legal counsel.
3. Seal it with the ducal signet.
4. Register it with the royal archives.

Once registered, succession to Aranis's title and wealth becomes legally binding upon his retirement or death.
