### Brief Description

Kelabren is a thirty-six-year-old Melromarc fisherman and occasional adventurer known for drinking too much, flirting too freely, and turning danger or authority into a joke. Beneath the careless charm is a capable survivor, a quietly protective man, and someone carrying guilt over the family he failed to save.

**Character Type:** Playable

### Appearance

Kelabren is a 5-foot-8 human man with a practical build shaped by years of rowing, hauling nets, balancing on wet decks, and working through cold coastal weather. His shoulders and forearms are wiry rather than broad, and his movements carry the economy of someone accustomed to unstable footing and repetitive labor. His most memorable features are captivating blue eyes and a broad, genuine smile that makes him seem immediately approachable. His face is mature but not heavily weathered, with subtle tension around his eyes when tired or withdrawn. His dark, thick hair is slightly unruly on top and shorter at the sides. His complexion is lightly weathered by salt, wind, and sun. Across his chest are seven distinct magical tattoos, each bearing the same symbol used when he bestows its corresponding Seven Deadly Sin mark upon another person. He stands with relaxed confidence, shifts his weight easily, and moves with deliberate balance, often pacing in short measured lines while thinking.

### Backstory

Kelabren was born in a fishing settlement under Melromarc's rule, the son of fishers who worked the coast and nearby rivers. His parents taught him boats, nets, knots, weather, bargaining, and how to bring people home safely. His younger sister followed him everywhere and trusted his judgment. During a violent storm compounded by a monster outbreak, Kelabren took responsibility for guiding his family toward shelter. He chose a faster route through flooded lowland rather than risk the longer road, but the crossing failed. His parents and sister died while he survived. Since then he has buried guilt beneath drink, flirting, jokes, and a reputation for unreliability. He still fishes for a living and takes modest adventuring work when coin is short, especially escort, gathering, hunting, or monster-clearing jobs. At the campaign's opening, he remains an independent fisherman of modest means, unaware of the unusual sin-symbol magic dormant within him and without any sin-marked companions.

### Mannerisms

Kelabren grins when tension rises, leans too close while flirting, and answers concern with a joke or exaggerated toast. He sprawls across chairs and doorframes, winks at strangers, and lets his smile disappear for a heartbeat when talk turns to family, storms, or helpless people.

### Personality

Kelabren is charming, flirtatious, reckless, and difficult to embarrass. He turns danger, authority, grief, and his own mistakes into banter, drinks heavily, and acts as though consequences belong to tomorrow. His humor is warm and disarming, making him welcome company even among people who know better than to rely on him. It also lets him dodge sincerity, responsibility, and questions that might expose how deeply he hurts. Beneath the careless performance, Kelabren is perceptive, protective, and far more dependable in a crisis than his reputation suggests. He dislikes cruelty, exploitation, and people who use status to trap the powerless. He prefers improvisation to planning and takes foolish risks when someone else is in danger. Guilt over his family's deaths has convinced him that closeness gives him another chance to fail someone. Rather than admit that fear, he flirts, wanders, drinks, and leaves first, longing for connection while pretending distance is freedom.
