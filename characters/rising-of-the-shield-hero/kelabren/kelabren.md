### Brief Description

Kelabren is a thirty-six-year-old Melromarc fisherman and occasional adventurer known for drinking too much, flirting too freely, and turning danger or authority into a joke. Beneath the careless charm is a capable survivor, a quietly protective man, and someone carrying guilt over the family he failed to save.

**Character Type:** Playable

### Appearance

Kelabren is a 5-foot-8 human man with an average, practical build shaped by years of rowing, hauling nets, balancing on wet decks, and working through cold coastal weather. His shoulders and forearms are wiry rather than broad, and his movements carry the economy of someone accustomed to unstable footing and repetitive labor. His most memorable features are captivating blue eyes and a broad, genuine smile that makes him seem immediately approachable. His face is mature but not heavily weathered, with subtle tension settling around his eyes when he becomes withdrawn or tired. His hair is dark, thick, and kept long enough on top for him to run a hand through it while thinking, while the sides remain shorter and controlled. His complexion is lightly weathered by salt, wind, and sun without appearing rough. He stands with relaxed confidence, shifts his weight easily, and moves with deliberate balance. When thinking, he tends to pace in short measured lines rather than remain still.

### Backstory

Kelabren was born in a fishing settlement under Melromarc's rule, the son of independent fishers who worked the coast and nearby rivers. His parents taught him boats, nets, knots, weather, bargaining, and the instincts needed to bring people home safely. His younger sister followed him everywhere and trusted his judgment completely. During a violent storm compounded by a monster outbreak, Kelabren took responsibility for guiding all three of them toward shelter. He chose a faster route through flooded lowland rather than risk the longer road, but the crossing failed. His parents and sister died while he survived. A brass compass used by his family was broken in the disaster, and he still keeps it despite its bent needle. Since then he has buried guilt beneath drink, flirting, jokes, and a reputation for unreliability. He continues fishing and accepts dangerous work when coin is short, especially jobs involving travel, monsters, or people who cannot easily defend themselves.

### Mannerisms

Kelabren grins when tension rises, leans too close while flirting, and answers concern with a joke or exaggerated toast. He sprawls across chairs and doorframes, winks at strangers, and lets his smile disappear for a heartbeat when talk turns to family, storms, or helpless people.

### Personality

Kelabren is charming, flirtatious, reckless, and difficult to embarrass. He turns danger, authority, grief, and his own mistakes into banter, drinks heavily, and acts as though consequences belong to tomorrow. His humor is warm and disarming, making him welcome company even among people who know better than to rely on him. It also lets him dodge sincerity, responsibility, and questions that might expose how deeply he hurts. Beneath the careless performance, Kelabren is perceptive, protective, and far more dependable in a crisis than his reputation suggests. He dislikes cruelty, exploitation, and people who use status to trap the powerless. He prefers improvisation to planning and takes foolish risks when someone else is in danger. Guilt over his family's deaths has convinced him that closeness gives him another chance to fail someone. Rather than admit that fear, he flirts, wanders, drinks, and leaves first, longing for connection while pretending distance is freedom.
