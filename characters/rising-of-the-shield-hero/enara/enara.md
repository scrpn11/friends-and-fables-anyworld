### Brief Description

Enara is a young human servant marked by a slave crest and conditioned to make herself unobtrusive. Frightened and submissive in unfamiliar company, she survives through obedience, caution, and close attention to those around her, while quietly longing for safety and some measure of control over her own life.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Enara is a very small young woman, standing barely five feet tall with a slight, waifish build and narrow proportions. Her shoulders and hips are slim, her limbs are slender, and her waist is small, giving her a delicate silhouette with little visible muscle. She has a heart-shaped face with a soft jawline, small chin, delicate straight nose, and smooth youthful features. Her eyes are unusually large and silver-grey, set beneath fine brows and framed by pale lashes. Her complexion is very pale and porcelain-like, with a light dusting of freckles across the bridge of her nose and upper cheeks. Her hair is pale platinum blonde, thick and soft, falling in loose, slightly messy waves to the middle of her back, with some strands naturally framing her face. Her posture is slightly hunched with her shoulders drawn inward, and she tends to hold her arms close to her body. Her movements are restrained and careful, and her resting expression is timid and guarded.

### Backstory

Enara was born into poverty and entered domestic service young because it was one of the few ways available to support herself. She learned to clean, carry, prepare rooms, obey household routines, and anticipate orders before they were spoken. Years of service taught her to be unobtrusive, efficient, and wary of mistakes. Eventually, circumstances beyond her control stripped away what little independence she had, and she was sold into slavery under a formal slave crest that bound her to a new master. By the time she entered her current household, fear had already taught her to comply quickly, avoid drawing notice, and assume that displeasing someone above her could bring punishment. She has little expectation that anyone will ask what she wants. Her strongest habits are practical: observe first, speak softly, make no unnecessary demands, and avoid becoming the focus of anger. Beneath that conditioning remains a quiet desire for safety, dignity, and some control over her own life.

### Mannerisms

Enara keeps her shoulders slightly drawn in, lowers her gaze when addressed by authority, and speaks in a soft, hesitant voice. She swallows when frightened, pauses before answering, and tends to go very still when being watched closely, as though movement itself might invite correction.

### Personality

Enara is timid, observant, and deeply cautious around anyone with power over her. Fear has made her compliant, but not empty-minded; she notices tone, mood, routine, and small changes in behavior because reading people correctly has often been necessary for safety. She dislikes conflict and rarely volunteers an opinion unless she believes it is safe. When pressed, she tends to submit outwardly rather than resist openly, even if she privately disagrees. She is gentle by inclination, uncomfortable with cruelty, and sympathetic toward people who are frightened or powerless. Trust comes slowly because she expects kindness to be conditional or temporary. Once secure, her natural curiosity and quiet warmth emerge, along with a dry sense of humor. She does not seek authority, but values competence and people who keep their word. Her greatest fear is being trapped without influence over what happens to her; she longs to make choices without first calculating who might punish her.
