### Brief Description

Kaede is a young fox-eared demi-human from Melromarc who survives by combining quick judgment, sharper instincts, and a stubborn refusal to remain dependent on anyone. She is wary of authority, fiercely protective of people she accepts as her own, and determined to become strong enough to choose where she belongs.

**Character Type:** Non-Playable

### Appearance

Kaede appears to be in her late teens and stands a little over five feet tall, with a compact, athletic build that remains slim rather than muscular. Her frame is light, with narrow shoulders, long legs for her height, a defined waist, and quick, balanced movement. She has a heart-shaped face, a small straight nose, high cheekbones, and amber-gold eyes with slightly narrowed outer corners. Her skin is fair with a warm undertone and a faint dusting of freckles across her nose. Thick reddish-brown hair falls past her shoulders in layered waves, with shorter pieces framing her face and a few unruly strands near her brow. Two triangular fox ears rise naturally from the top of her head, covered in reddish-brown fur with darker tips and pale inner fur. A single full fox tail extends from her lower back, matching her hair with a darker tip. Her posture is alert and springy, and her expressions shift quickly from guarded focus to sly amusement.

### Backstory

Kaede was born in a mixed demi-human settlement near Melromarc's eastern trade roads, where her family survived through trapping, leatherwork, and seasonal caravan labor. Human prejudice was ordinary enough that she learned young when to keep her ears lowered, when to leave before trouble started, and when running would only invite pursuit. After a local lord increased levies following monster damage and a failed harvest, her family fell into debt. Her older brother vanished while escorting a merchant caravan, and her mother later died of illness, leaving Kaede responsible for herself and a few remaining relatives. Rather than submit to a creditor's claim, she fled and spent months moving between villages, camps, and roadside work. She learned to hunt, read people quickly, and disappear before anyone could own her labor. By the campaign's opening, she is still free but one bad season away from desperation, determined never to be trapped by debt, law, or dependence.

### Mannerisms

Kaede's ears swivel toward sudden sounds before her head follows, and her tail gives away irritation even when her face does not. She crouches rather than sits when waiting, speaks in short direct sentences, sniffs unfamiliar food, and flashes a quick grin when she thinks she has found an advantage.

### Personality

Kaede is quick-witted, independent, suspicious, and difficult to intimidate for long. She dislikes being ordered around and reacts badly to pity, but she respects competence, honesty, and people who keep their word. Her first instinct is usually to protect her own freedom, yet she has a strong weakness for anyone being cornered, exploited, or treated as disposable. She prefers practical solutions over ideals and will steal, lie, or bend rules if doing so prevents a worse outcome. Trust develops slowly because she expects obligations to become chains, but once she accepts someone she becomes intensely loyal and territorial about their safety. She enjoys teasing people who are easy to fluster and often uses humor to test boundaries. In danger she becomes focused rather than reckless, favoring speed, escape routes, and opportunistic action. Her greatest flaw is assuming dependence always leads to control, which makes cooperation harder than it needs to be.
