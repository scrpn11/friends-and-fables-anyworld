# The First Room

**Area:** The Between  
**Type:** Reincarnation Chamber  

## Description

The First Room is the sole stable place within the Between and the destination of every arrival after death. It exists to turn unrestricted desire into a coherent next life. The Guide waits here and leads a patient conversation covering the chosen world, continuity and timeline, identity, species, body, age, gender, family, status, abilities, possessions, relationships, reputation, temperament, history, opening circumstances, and deliberate changes to canon. No request is rejected for being strange, powerful, contradictory, or drawn from fiction; instead, the Guide identifies conflicts and asks which outcome the player prefers. Nothing chosen here grants memory of the meeting. When the design is complete, the room forms a passage into the selected reality, and the resulting person awakens or is born with only the memories and personality appropriate to that life. The room cannot be entered from a living world, used to observe past incarnations, or used to carry knowledge between lives.

## Campaign Introduction

There is no pain now.

No falling. No final breath. No weight of a body against the ground.

For one impossible moment, there is only pale light and the certainty that something has ended.

A smooth white floor comes into focus beneath you, reflecting neither your face nor any shape you remember wearing. Beyond it stretches a luminous mist without walls, horizon, sky, or visible source. You cannot recall a name. You cannot recall a home, a death, a promise, or even the kind of person you were. The emptiness where those things should be does not hurt. It simply is.

Ahead, two comfortable chairs wait beside a low glass table. A young woman occupies one of them as though she has been expecting you for some time. She is poised without being formal, warm without seeming false, and entirely at ease in a place that should not exist. Behind her stands an archway filled with soft, shifting light. For an instant it resembles a city at night. Then a forest. Then the deck of a starship. Then a school corridor drawn in ink before becoming blank again.

The woman welcomes you gently and explains the only fact that matters: your previous life is over, and nothing from it remains. You are free to choose what comes next.

Any world. Any era. Any identity. Any body, family, power, history, role, relationship, or beginning you desire. The life created here will be wholly its own. Once it begins, you will not remember this room, this conversation, or that another life ever came before it.

She leans forward with an inviting smile, ready to listen.

The first choice is yours: what world would you like to live in?

## Appearance

A circular white chamber rests alone in the luminous emptiness, open at the edges as though walls were never needed. Two comfortable chairs face each other across a low glass table. Behind the Guide, an arch of soft light changes color and texture as possible worlds are discussed. The air is still and scentless. Every spoken word sounds close and private, while the surrounding mist swallows all sight beyond the room.
