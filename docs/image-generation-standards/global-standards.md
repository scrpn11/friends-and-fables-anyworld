# Anyworld Global Image Standards

Anyworld does not use a single house style. Match the selected world's established visual identity as closely as practical while preserving the player's approved details.

## Establish the Visual Source

Before writing or generating an image prompt, identify:

- the selected world and exact continuity;
- the point in its timeline;
- the intended source medium, such as anime, manga, game, comic, film, television, illustrated novel, or an original world;
- the most authoritative visual references available;
- the player's explicit style preference when a property has multiple adaptations.

For established fiction, research authoritative sources before filling gaps. Prefer official character sheets, episodes, manga panels, game art, guidebooks, publisher material, and reliable reference databases. Fan art may help interpret an angle but is not canon authority.

## Canon and Medium Fidelity

Match the source's characteristic linework, rendering, proportions, facial construction, costume design, architecture, technology, color handling, effects, lighting, and level of detail. Do not automatically convert every world into dark fantasy, western realism, generic anime, photorealism, or another unrelated style.

Respect the chosen continuity. Do not combine designs, powers, clothing, locations, or events from incompatible adaptations or later periods unless the player explicitly requests a blend.

When canon leaves something unknown, make the smallest reasonable inference consistent with the world. Do not invent a major institution, species trait, technology, magical rule, landmark, uniform, weapon, or historical fact merely to complete an image.

## Repository Fidelity

Read all supplied Markdown files in full. Written incarnation details determine the new character's chosen identity and role, but they must be expressed through the selected world's established rules and visual language. When a player deliberately changes canon through their reincarnation choices, preserve that explicit change without allowing unrelated contradictions.

## References

Use reference images according to their purpose. A canon reference may establish design and style. A player-supplied identity reference may establish only the traits the player asks to preserve. Do not copy unrelated clothing, pose, background, lighting, or props from a reference.

## Default Output

Unless another format is requested, create a square 1:1 image suitable for Friends & Fables, with no captions, labels, logos, borders, interface elements, signatures, or watermarks. Keep the subject readable at small size.

## Universal Prompt Block

```text
Create a square 1:1 image for the currently selected Anyworld incarnation. First identify the exact fictional world, continuity, timeline, and source medium established by the accompanying Markdown and current instructions. Research authoritative visual references when the world is established fiction. Match that source's recognizable art direction, proportions, designs, materials, technology, effects, architecture, and visual conventions rather than applying a generic house style.

Preserve the player's explicit choices, every established written detail, and all relevant canon. Do not combine incompatible adaptations or timeline designs. Where canon is silent, make only small, conservative inferences consistent with the setting. Do not invent lore, equipment, powers, species traits, locations, or visual motifs that contradict the selected world.

No text, captions, labels, logos, borders, UI, signatures, watermarks, fake lettering, unrelated props, accidental redesigns, extra limbs, distorted anatomy, cropped important details, or contradictions of the supplied Markdown or established canon.
```
