# The Between

## Description

The Between is the quiet interval reached after a player character dies and before another life begins. It belongs to no religion, cosmology, universe, or timeline, and it does not judge, reward, punish, or preserve the dead. Every arrival is stripped of the incarnation that ended: memories, personality, loyalties, skills, powers, relationships, and sense of identity are gone. The player remains able to choose the next life from outside the character, but the person eventually born or placed into that world will believe that life has always been their own. The Between contains no settlements, factions, wilderness, treasure, or native dangers. Its sole known inhabitant is the Guide, who receives each arrival and helps define the next world, continuity, era, body, role, history, abilities, relationships, circumstances, and desired deviations from canon. Once the choices are complete, the new incarnation begins at the selected point with no awareness of this place or prior existence.

## Appearance

The Between appears as a boundless pale expanse beneath a sky without sun, stars, or horizon. Distance has little meaning; smooth reflective ground fades into luminous mist in every direction. There is no wind, temperature, scent, or natural echo, yet footsteps and voices remain clear. Far away, indistinct lights sometimes bloom and vanish like doors glimpsed through fog, never close enough to reach without the Guide.
